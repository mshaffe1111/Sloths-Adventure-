## 1. The signature move: stillness is Pokey's strength (new; the biggest change)
- **What:** hold still and Pokey blends into moss and bark (Matt's idea #7 algae camouflage, folded with #24 heartbeat calm). Hunters
  that look for movement (eagles, the gaze sweeps, night hunters, the Drake) lose him while he is still. The tell is always visible: a
  soft moss tint creeps over him and a small calm sound; it never needs a HUD gauge.
- **Why:** it is the one verb only a sloth game can own, and it turns the story's wound ("too slow", the General's word) into his
  strength. It is what a player tells a friend about. Today the map has it parked (R-165.94) and World 1's 1-7 uses a bush instead.
- **The arc:** taught in **1-7 A Very Good Bush** (bushes are the safe first version, then stillness anywhere in moss); comes back in
  World 3 (sleepers stay asleep while he is still), World 4 (gaze sweeps), World 8 (night hunters), World 9 (the Drake's gaze), and is
  mastered in **World 10's hold-on rule** (freeze on the lightning flash), so the last world asks for the first world's lesson at its hardest.
- **Engine cost:** none new beyond pieces already on the list, moved earlier: B8 one meter (stillness), B10 noise, B11 gaze sweeps.
  They become World 1 pieces.
- **Open with the story keeper:** whether World 5's "don't stop" rule fights this arc (it punishes stopping while the game's spine
  rewards stillness). Asked 11-11 6:45 PM ET; its answer decides World 5's third rule.

## 2. A "look" per stage (new): style for little cost
- The brief gets a `look` field: day, dawn mist, sunset silhouette (archetype #20), rain, night glow. One colour-grade and light layer
  over the same art, inside the PERFORMANCE-RULES draw budget (drawcheck proves it). Two neighbouring stages never share a look and a twist.
- Why: the fastest way to make 90 stages look different without new paintings. Engine piece, World 1 list, 4-8 h (a guess, no history).
