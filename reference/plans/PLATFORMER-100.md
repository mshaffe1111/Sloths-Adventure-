# Matt's 100 side-scrollers (Matt, 11-14 2:21 PM ET) and what we can take from them

Matt: "here's a good list that we can use on top of what we already have to draw inspiration from... because we focused on just what
would be considered the best of the indies and best of the Nintendos, we don't think of other things like this... Out of this list, what
are things that we could implement? So when designers and the leads are looking at things, it's kind of just picking and choosing."

How to use this file: the lead and every designer pick from the PICK LIST below (and from INSPIRED-BY.md: Matt's own favourites and the
84 ideas' games), mixing several games per level with our own ideas (STAGE-RULES tribute rule; DESIGNER.md "Variety"). Inspirations only:
we take a principle and build our own rainforest version, never a recreation. Each pick is researched before it is designed (what made it
great, how the original taught it, how it feels on a phone).

## The pick list (lead's first pass, 11-14; a designer deepens each pick when a level uses it)
Our fit rules: touch only, a slow sloth (climb, swing, hang, stillness; no projectiles, no power-ups that change jump reach), World 1 has
no made things, 30 fps, both orientations, the first appearance taught. "Have" = an engine piece exists; "new" = an engine piece to build
once, for every stage (the engine drives development).

## Not for us (and why, so nobody re-asks)
Run-and-gun and beat 'em ups (Contra, Streets of Rage, Cuphead, Gunstar, Katana Zero, Skul): no projectiles or combat loops for a sloth;
we take their pacing and set pieces only. Power-up suits and transformations that change jump reach (SMB3 suits, Mario's mushrooms):
OUT (they move every reach check). Time rewind (Braid, Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown): OUT before launch. Open crafting (Terraria): not
our game. Undertale and Sea of Stars: their side-scrolling is minor; take only the tone.
