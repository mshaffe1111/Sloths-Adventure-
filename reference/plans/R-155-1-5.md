## The shape (Normal; seconds on the quick player's clock, the builder's own `expert`)
Every part is one or two **rhythm groups** (§0b.1) with a **rest** (no hard input, about 1.5-2 s of plain ground) between groups; a
**checkpoint flag on the rest before parts 3 and 4** (§0b.2). The stage is authored to length: no `fillTo` anywhere.

| part | s | groups (each 5-20 s) | what it asks | what a miss costs |

## The pieces
Existing (protoStage.ts kit, unchanged): `creakRun`, `fernTree`, `climbWall`, `ropeGap`, `highRoadOver`, `bounceWall`, `glideLookout`,
`homeRun`, `rest`, `flag`, `logHop`.
From w8-rope2 (R-159.1, he writes, I place; he adds only exported pieces to w1Sections.ts and never 1-5's order):
- `kick-teach`: about 1,100 px; a 300 px flat run-up with no gap and no animal within 400 px; the coiled boa (its 'coil' trick comes from
  w1Tricks.ts at load); its ledge at least 520 px long (the bowling lane); the hollow stump with the petal cache at the lane's end; 200 px
  of clear ground after. No pit. Every layout.
- its twist version on a breaking-branch ledge with a real drop beyond the stump (`kickCreak` above).
New (mine, World 1 builder; existing art only):
- `creakDrop`: `creakRun`'s branches with the ground under them replaced by a lower path 150-200 px down, and a trunk back up at the
  run's start (the setback). Today's art: the branches, the low path's earth and the trunk. When R-159.1's painted pits land (Matt
  approved the pit list: drop, lip, bed, high drop), the drop can use them; the plan does not wait on them.
- `snapRun`: the same, with snap-twig timing (§0b.4: `crumbleAfter` about 0.15 s on Normal) so holding right does not outrun it: each
  twig gives way under a runner who does not keep hopping. Easy keeps the creak timing (0.55 s, the value 1-5's existing twist
  branch already uses).
- The snake that waits on `kickCreak`'s far branch (a patrol on that branch, as `snakeWalk`'s snakes): the patience half of the twist.

**Gone from 1-5**: the `fillTo` finishes (fights, climbs, bounces), the `highLeaps` and `highRun` pieces (the mushroom up to branches
240 px over the floor: the unit that makes 1-1, 1-5 and 1-7 look alike) except the one `bounceWall` in the finale, `doubleArcs`,
`mushroomCache`, `dipArc` chains. 1-1's moves stay in World 1; they just stop being 1-5's body.

**Rolls (variety)**: the kick teach and the fern tree are on every layout. Each other group comes in two versions (order and side: the
climb before or after the rope; the drop left or right of the trunk; the boa's lane on the high run or the low one), picked by
w1Sections' own stream as today, so the validator's "like the stage before/earlier" stays low across layouts, not just on layout 1.
