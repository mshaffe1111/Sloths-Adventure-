## One trick per World 1 animal, each with a 0.5 s tell (Easy 0.8 s)
1. **Tree snake: the coiled lunge.** It coils and its head sways for 0.5 s (rear today is 0.38 s), then lunges. Punch in the coil: dazed
   1.5 s. Stomp: always works. The sleeping snake stays the free first punch (1-3's teacher).
2. **Vine snake (new, the research's idea):** hangs among lianas looking like a vine; 0.5 s tell (eyes open, tongue flick) before it bites
   whoever grabs it. Punched, it stiffens ~1.5 s into a straight pole Pokey can stand or climb on, then slackens. A vine you choose.
3. **Boa: the armoured coil.** Coiled = armour (stomp bounces, punch "TOUGH!"); its coil tightens for 0.5 s before the strike; a kick
   (ground or air) breaks the coil and bowls it. Its strike stays the threat; the boa guards the switch it can be bowled into.
