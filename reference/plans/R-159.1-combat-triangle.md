## One trick per World 1 animal, each with a 0.5 s tell (Easy 0.8 s)
1. **Tree snake: the coiled lunge.** It coils and its head sways for 0.5 s (rear today is 0.38 s), then lunges. Punch in the coil: dazed
   1.5 s. Stomp: always works. The sleeping snake stays the free first punch (1-3's teacher).
2. **Vine snake (new, the research's idea):** **R3, a choice for Matt (with the picture list):** (a) a vine-coloured snake, the liana's
   green-brown and texture, given away by its tell; or (b) an obvious snake on a vine, punched to make the vine safe. (a) is the
   research's idea and the stronger moment, only if its tell reads from the take-off ledge at phone size. As (a): it hangs among lianas looking like a vine. **Its tell starts before the commitment:** eyes open
   and a tongue flick from when Pokey comes within a jump's length (~230 px), so it reads from the take-off ledge, never only once he is
   in the air. Grabbed anyway, it bites. Punched, it stiffens ~1.5 s into a straight pole Pokey can stand or climb on, then slackens.
   Proof stills from the take-off ledge, upright and sideways: it must read as a snake there (§0b.7, read by shape).
3. **Boa: the armoured coil, read by shape.** Coiled = armour, and it **looks** armoured: a tight coil with a glint (the "coil tightens"
   frames on the picture list, plus a small code glint), different from the loose boa, so "TOUGH!" is never how the player first learns
   it. Its coil tightens for 0.5 s before the strike; a kick (ground or air) breaks the coil and bowls it. Show-first stills of the
   armoured look go with the others.

## How it is proven
- **Engine tests per trick, the wrong move fails and the right one works** (bare world, real engine): punch dazes the tree snake 1.5 s;
  stomp bounces off the coiled boa, the kick bowls it into a row of snakes and trips the switch; the punched vine snake holds Pokey.
- **Every world's animals before/after (D21)**: the fight tests, plus the fixed first-timer on every stage with animals in Worlds 1-10,
  Normal and Easy, base vs branch. Only World 1's TRAITS change, so other worlds must come out identical.
- **Without-the-move proof per spot:** for every vault, stun and bowl, a real-engine test where holding right and jumping (no stomp,
  no punch, no kick) fails that spot, and the right move passes it.
- **The validator's mechanics table** gets stomp-vault / punch-stun / kick-bowl columns per stage; World 1 must show all three.
- **The fixed first-timer** (Normal and Easy, layouts 1-3): no runs with rescues counted until R-150.7 (mushroom walls) lands.
- **Clips, upright and sideways**, of each trick and its tell, filmed by stepping the engine (stage-fix.md); a polish check per clip.
- **Proof in sequence:** a stage is done when it passes the quality supervisor's in-order re-recording of World 1 (tools/quality/),
  judged beside the baseline, not only in its own tests.
- Gates: tsc, the full tests, build, artcheck, drawcheck, perf budget (PERFORMANCE-RULES §1).
