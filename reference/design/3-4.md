# 3-4 The Sinkhole: "Climb the Moonlight", the level as designed (DESIGN-33-35 final, from PICKS-33-35.md:13-17: PITCH-33B's idea + PITCH-33A's breather rule; written 7:17 PM ET 11-19 by `date`). DESIGN ONLY: no build before its engine (NEW 1-2 below, each proven on a real stageTable row, level.md rule 23)
**One idea:** "The moonbeam swings from wall to wall; where it touches, the moonflowers open into steps: climb in the light, wait on stone." A player says: "the one where the flowers open
in the moonlight." **The hook:** a calm, beautiful climb after the busy hollow, World 3's breather (slot 3.5, below 3-3's 4.5 and 3-5's 6). The only question is WHEN, and the answer is
drawn in light: the beam comes, the wall blooms, he climbs; it leaves, the flowers droop (the warning) and close, and he waits on stone. A sloth climbing slowly toward the moon: patience
is the skill. 3-1's light was SUMMONED (stand still); 3-4's MOVES on its own and MAKES the ground. **The breather rule (A):** 3-4 costs time, never a heart, on its teach; a closing flower
drops him to the stone below (soft or a short fall: no fall loses a life, 3-1.md flown), never into thorns. **One new thing:** light that moves and makes the path.

## Tributes (each opened by the pitcher who cites it; D58)
- **Rainbow Islands** (Taito 1987; via PITCH-33B). a. Loved: "one of the greatest platform games ever made. The vertical rainbow-fuelled play is unique" (GamesRadar). b. One verb, up: rainbows
  "act as weapons, makeshift platforms, and item collectors" (Wikipedia). c. Ours: steps made of light that last only while the light does. d. Not copied: making the steps, the rising sea.
- **Tower Toppler / Nebulus** (Hewson 1987; Matt's own favourite, INSPIRED-BY.md:9; via PITCH-33A). a. Loved: Matt, "you just go around on circles, go into doors, but you had to jump and do
  platforms while you were doing it". b. Taught: the first things that come at you are slow and on your floor [S]. c. Ours: wall to wall, storey by storey, waiting at the right door (stone)
  for the light to come round. d. Not copied: the rotating tower, the timer.
- **Sea of Stars** (Sabotage 2023; via PITCH-33B). a. Loved: "glowing flowers light the way in an underground cavern" (GamesRadar). b. "the shadows rotating and the sun and moon swapping
  places" (PC Gamer). c. Ours: the moon's light moves and the world answers it; we make him wait for it. d. Not copied: turning the time yourself.
- **Celeste** (2018; via PITCH-33B). a. Loved: "the difficulty does relent. You will finish this game" (PC Gamer). b. Each screen adds one modifier. c. Ours: each storey adds one thing to the
  beam (rests, swings, rises, both); kind on Easy, tight on Normal; every fall soft. d. Not copied: the dash, death as the teacher.
**Different:** the first ascent in World 3 (3-1 went down); no creature (the breather); no lift or wheel carries him: he follows the light. 1-4's climb had rotting boughs and the two-vine pair,
nothing that came and went with a light.
**Pushback (design):** (1) PITCH-33B's droop warning (1.0 s) is right for Normal and too short for Easy: flown, Easy needs 1.6 s for a 0.5 s reader's last step plus margin. (2) A held jump tops
out 97 px (flown), so any flower stair over 100 px a step needs the double and the breather would become a test: the verdict must refuse rise > 100. (3) A rising band over 70 px/s drops the
slow Normal reader (90 px/s FELL, flown): the dial stops at 70. **Learned:** fly the climb as a thumb does it (walk to the edge, stop, jump): a stair flown with Right held throughout ran off
every 100 px step and read as unclimbable.
