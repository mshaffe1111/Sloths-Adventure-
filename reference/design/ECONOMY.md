## 1. What the classics did (principles only; we copy no level, character or look)
- Super Mario Run (Nintendo, 2016): 5 pink coins a course, then harder purple/black sets: a small visible set gives a one-thumb run a reason to replay. Take the set of 5; never paid continues.
- Super Mario 3D Land (2011): 3 star medals a course, COUNTED never spent, open bonus worlds, never the main road; White Tanooki after 5 deaths. Take: count-not-spend, bonus only.
- Super Mario Odyssey (2017): no lives; a fall costs 10 coins, not time; moon quotas sit far below totals. Take: failure costs something earned, a price below the total.
- Donkey Kong Country (1994) / Returns (2010): KONG letters and 100 bananas buy lives; by mid-game lives pile up (Returns: dozens) and the reward means nothing. Never: rewards that feed lives (Matt 11-13: "most people won't need an extra life").
- Rayman Legends (2013): no lives, a heart is one extra hit; the lums score is noise to children; rescued Teensies (a count) open worlds. Take: a count opens bonus content; drop score.
- Celeste (2018): strawberries are worth only pride; unlimited retries cost seconds; Assist Mode carries no shame. Take: the hard star is optional; help never shames.
- On phones and for children: spendable currencies bring regret and store pressure (Play Store families policy, PLAY-STORE-RULES); a score number means nothing to a 7-year-old.
- So: four stars a stage, each a picture and a different skill (finish, explore, flow, care); counted, never spent; they open bonus content only; Normal's lives can run out at a fair, bounded cost.
## 2. Today, checked in code and measured (5 bots x 12 seeds, 420 runs; summary.txt)
- Score: +100 a fruit (engine.ts:3131) and +100-1000 a foe (rules.ts calculateHitScore); SHOWN on the HUD when it changes (VineHud.tsx:551-566), on Stage Cleared with "New best" (StageCleared.tsx:241) and Game Over (GameOverCard.tsx:52). It buys nothing today. Measured (summary-stars.txt): it separates skill, through petal chains (engine.ts:3142-3143, +50 x chain, a chain lapses 2.5 s after the last petal, :1901-1904): points per petal child 143-215, middle 187-283, expert 197-314; on the same seed the expert outscores the child 1.3-2.0x, the middle 1.07-1.23x.
- Stars (rules.ts:157-178): 1 to finish; 2 for half the hearts left; 3 for no life lost + 75% hearts + on a timed stage a finish in 60% of timeLimit (1-1: 168 s; expert bot median 183 s, child 484 s), else 85% of the goal, which a finished goal always meets. Speed decides 1-1's third star although badges.ts:8 says "Nothing is about finishing a stage quickly". Stars open nothing; they feed 3 medals (badges.ts:296-304, Platinum :264).
- Hearts 5 a life (secret.ts:55; +1 Thorn Heart), refilled at every respawn (engine.ts:5659). Lives 3 a stage (assist.ts BASE_LIVES), +2/+4 offered after 3 Game Overs (assist.ts:16). Game Over: Continue at the last flag with every life back and the stage's progress kept, without limit (engine.ts:5299 continueFromCheckpoint): losing costs nothing.
- Pits and water take a whole life whatever the hearts (engine.ts:2675-2685): 411 of the 456 lives the bots lost were water (6-3); the rest thorns (40) and an ocelot (5) on 3-4, so hearts only matter on stages that hit.
- World 1, all 180 runs (child/middle Easy+Normal, expert Normal): 0 lives lost, at most 2 hearts lost in a run. 1-2 Normal lays 7 hearts, 1-3 Normal 6; nobody needs one. Flags: 10-15 a stage on Normal, 17-28 on Easy.
- 3-4 Normal: hearts lost a run 4.8-9.4, Game Over 1-3/12 runs; 6-3 Normal: 5.6-10.7 lives a run (means) from water swings, Game Over 10-12/12 (bot weak on lianas, STAGE-RULES 343). 3-4's bots were rescued 7-18 times a run (climbs, STAGE-RULES 584): read later worlds as direction, not truth.
- Fruit: a bot that only walks the route gathers 58-97% of the fruit laid (p10-p90) (1-1 ~79%, 1-2 ~93%, 6-3 ~72%; fruit tally in raw/f-*.jsonl): a share of everyday fruit cannot be a star.
- A flat score target (K x fruit laid) fails: at K 190 the Normal middle reaches it on 12/12 runs of 1-3 and 0/12 of 6-3 (summary-target.txt); chain room depends on petal spacing, so the target comes from each stage's own par. Par = the expert's score on the same layout: at 0.9 x par the Normal middle gets it on 3-9/12 runs, the child on 0-1/12.
- Clean runs (no heart, no life lost): 1-1 and 1-2 12/12 for every tier (a free star today), 1-3 6-10/12, 3-4 2-5/12, 6-3 0/12 (water).
- Finds: one kind per stage on stages 2-6 and 8 (finds.ts:45), about 37 in the game: under half the stages hold one.

## 4. What it moves in what we built
- Shipped stages 1-1..1-3: no change in lives or Game Overs on Normal at 3 hearts (180 runs, summary-h3.txt). Each needs 5 basket fruits laid (gen2 packet), else ★2 cannot be earned: no stage ships STARS-4 until its basket is laid (D40 order).
- Meter (S3, R-165.172): the meter reads lives a minute; 3 hearts raises Worlds 3-10 toward their bands (0 of 72 sat in band, DIFF-ALGO); S3 re-scores AFTER hearts-3 lands.
- Medals (badges.ts:296-304, :264): Star Collector 30/150/300 of 300 -> 40/200/400 of 400; Perfect Collector (every star on a stage) keeps 5/30/100; Platinum reads "every star on every stage". Medals already earned stay earned (badges.ts:12).
- Old saves: 0-3 stars under the old rules do not map to the new tasks: a cleared stage keeps ★1, the rest are earned on replay (players of the live site see fewer stars on the map until they replay; their medals stay). The stored best score stays and becomes the score star's record.
- Shipped 1-1..1-3: ★4 is free today (every tier clean on 1-1, 1-2): it needs 1-4/1-5's bite, not a change to the shipped stages; ★3 needs each stage's par.
- Tonight's 1-4/1-5 builders: fly Normal with `--hearts 3` too, so the levels are tuned against the numbers that will ship.
