# 1-4 Up the Kapok: the level as designed (D14, 11-12; REVISED by F15 10:23 PM ET for difficulty and the vine field, Matt 10:06-10:15 PM). DESIGN ONLY: Matt plays the lattice feel room first
**One idea:** the kapok is hung with a field of little vines; he climbs vine to vine between its falling pods and frees the fruit that weighs down the bough across the way up.
**Normal target 4.5** (DIFFICULTY §4; band 3-6), **designed 4.4, Easy 2.3**. **Goal:** reach the crown (stagePlan.ts:41 `ascent`); her last petal lies low at the roots, none up
the tree. **Length:** 214 s designed; quick player 2:20+ (climbs count 20 s short); bots measure. **Cast:** 1-3's snake, twice; a file of leafcutter ants (new). **Look:** up, not
right: roots in mist, outside boughs, INSIDE the hollow trunk, out at the crown into sun. The hand-built 1-4 (protoStage.ts:5337) and its lift rooms are deleted.
**Inspired by: what made it great, what we take, what we don't copy**. The 1982 arcade vine climber: two vines climb faster, one vine slides down faster; red creatures wander up
and down the vines, blue ones come down fast and fall off; touching a hanging fruit drops it on whatever is below, worth more for each in a row; the last screen is six keys
pushed up their chains into locks; a bonus timer runs down. **Why it worked (F15's reading):** (1) up and down are DIFFERENT problems, so his posture is a choice every second;
(2) threats ride the same lanes he does, so where he hangs matters; (3) fruit turns good position into reward; (4) the push-up goal shows progress piece by piece, in his own
order. **We take** those four. **We don't copy** its screens, chains, keys, cage, creatures, look, scores, timer or death by falling. Sources (search extracts only: the pages are
outside this session's network): en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Jr., arcade-history.com/game/668, strategywiki.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Jr./Walkthrough.
**The vine field (replaces "why two vines").** A strangler fig's hanging roots and passion vines drape the kapok: MANY short vines (150-450 px), 70-90 px apart, in fields four to
six wide. **Moves, no new button:** on one vine, up climbs at today's 220 px/s (engine.ts:2037) and down SLIDES at twice that. Push toward a neighbour in reach and he takes it
too: two hands. On two, up is twice as fast and down is slow; push again and he lets the back one go. A crossing is two pushes with a held moment between, and that moment is the
choice: two hands to race a pod's beat, one to slide out of trouble, or a wait a vine over. Straight up or down always climbs (Matt's rule); a diagonal still jumps off to a
bough. **What comes at him:** ripe pods down a column on a beat (a creak and fluff trickling 1.0 s before; 620 px/s), and a file of leafcutter ants walking a vine (slow, steady:
a nip if he shares it). Two hands span two columns, so the fast way is the exposed way; one vine is often right. **The cost (Normal):** a pod or a nip costs a HEART and drops him
to the catch bough below (sound wood within 450 px); Easy: the drop only. **Fruit:** passion fruit and figs hang on the vines: the climb's pickups.
**The purpose: free the fruit to open the way (Matt 10:13 PM). Three options, F15's pick first.** **A, Lift the bough (pick):** each way up is shut by a bough sagging under fruit
clusters; each cluster hangs at a vine's top; climbing up under one pushes it up its vine to the bough, where it tears free and drops down its column (beside anything below,
never on it); the bough lifts a notch for each and springs open on the last; his order. No carrying (Matt's rule: carrying cannot climb), no new AI; it reuses the falling pod and
the drop-trap load. **B, Carry the baby home:** a baby sloth frozen low on a vine; climbing under it nudges it up to its mother, who moves off the way on. The sweetest, but an
escort is new AI (MAP §6 #5 parked it). **C, Feed the doorkeeper:** a kinkajou dozing in the hollow's mouth; freed figs drop to it and it ambles after them. Overlaps 1-9's lure.

**Rules (checks the build passes, both settings).** First of everything safe: the first rot over sound wood, the first field with nothing falling, the first pod in a column he is
not on, the first cluster on a quiet vine, the first snake asleep. Pods fair: the tell 1.0 s+ before one lets go; a free vine within one push of every vine a pod falls on; sound
wood within 450 px under every point (STAGE-RULES §2). Steady, then grown (R-165.174): every column in beats 4-5 keeps ONE beat and every field one spacing; the beat quickens
only from beat 7. Ants and pods never share a vine. A freed cluster lands beside any animal, never on it. Rot is whole on any set-back. D46 on every landing; Dot's lines on
Normal's DEFAULT Tips on the real page (D50); drawcheck and every stage gate play 1-4 (D51).
**What makes it 4.5 on Normal**: a timed crossing is scored by its open share of the beat: the fields' 1.2-2.0 s of 3.0-3.6 s, the hollow's and crown's 0.8-0.9 s of 2.4 s, each
at a heart; jumps onto rot at 0.70-0.80 of reach; the hollow packs six crossings into 30 s. Scored with score.ts's own `combine` (reference/qa-reports/F15/design14-score.ts.txt).
