# 1-4 Up the Kapok: the level as designed (D14, 2026-11-12). DESIGN ONLY: the build waits for its engine pieces and for Matt's feel test
**One idea:** the kapok drops its ripe pods on a beat; he climbs between them, waiting in a hollow on one vine or beating the drop on two. A player says: "the
one where you climb the giant tree between falling pods, to see where they took Meeca." **Goal:** reach the crown (stagePlan.ts:41 `ascent`; her last petal
lies low at the roots, none up the tree: STORY-KEEPER §5). **Length:** 198 s designed (first-timer); quick player 2:20+ (STAGE-RULES §1: climbs count 20 s
short); bots measure it. **Cast:** 1-3's snake, twice. **Look:** up, not right: roots in mist, outside boughs, then INSIDE the hollow trunk (MAP-REVISIONS §6
#16), out at the crown into sun. No made things. The hand-built 1-4 (protoStage.ts:5337) and its squirrel lift rooms (w1Sections.ts:383) are deleted.
**Why two vines (Matt's first question):** the tree keeps time. Ripe pods let go on a beat (MAP-REVISIONS §8: they fall on their own), and a vine is how Pokey
spends time: one is his steady climb (220 px/s today, engine.ts:2037, unchanged everywhere), two are a strand per long arm, twice as fast. The pair is the
hurry answer to a clock he sees and hears, the hollow the patient one (STAGE-RULES §0b.3). Natural, not a gimmick: ONE liana draped over a bough, its loop in
view (a plant, never two placed ropes); no new button (up climbs; between the strands he takes both); arms out, a quicker rustle; never where nothing falls (bar
its safe teach and the twist's bait); one vine never wrong, only steadier (a single and hollow by each pair pods fall on, bar the crown). Never "slow" (MAP §1 guard a).
