# 1-4 Up the Kapok: the level as designed (D14, 11-12; REVISED by F15 10:23 PM ET for difficulty and the vine field, Matt 10:06-10:31 PM; last edit 10:34 PM ET). DESIGN ONLY: Matt plays the lattice feel room first
**One idea:** the kapok is hung with a field of little vines; he climbs vine to vine between its falling pods and frees the fruit that weighs down the bough across the way up.
**Normal target 4.5** (DIFFICULTY §4; band 3-6), **designed 4.4, Easy 2.3**. **Goal:** reach the crown (stagePlan.ts:41 `ascent`); her last petal lies low at the roots, none up
the tree. **Length:** 214 s designed; quick player 2:20+ (climbs count 20 s short). **Cast:** 1-3's snake, twice; a daytime flyer and a file of leafcutter ants (both new).
**Look:** up, not right: roots in mist, outside boughs, INSIDE the hollow trunk, out at the crown into sun. The hand-built 1-4 (protoStage.ts:5337) are deleted.
**Inspired by: what made it great, what we take, what we don't copy**. The 1982 arcade vine climber: two vines climb faster, one vine slides down faster; red creatures wander up
and down the vines, blue ones come down fast and fall off; touching a hanging fruit drops it on whatever is below, worth more for each in a row; the last screen is six keys
pushed up their chains into locks. **Why it worked (F15's reading):** (1) up and down are DIFFERENT problems, so his posture is a choice every second; (2) threats ride the same
lanes he does, so where he hangs matters; (3) fruit turns good position into reward; (4) the push-up goal shows progress piece by piece, in his own order. **We take** those four.
**We don't copy** its screens, chains, keys, cage, creatures, look, scores, timer or death by falling. Sources (search extracts only; the pages are outside this network):
en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Jr., arcade-history.com/game/668, strategywiki.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Jr./Walkthrough.
**The vine field.** A strangler fig's hanging roots and passion vines drape the kapok: MANY short vines (150-450 px), 70-90 px apart, in fields four to six wide. **Moves: Matt's
rule (10:32 PM):** "you start with one climbing vine. If you go towards another vine, then he hangs on with both hands... If you do it again towards the new vine, he'll let go of
the previous vine, go to the new vine. That way, you have a way of controlling two-handed and one-handed without having to add new controls." One hand: up at today's 220 px/s
(engine.ts:2037), down a SLIDE (about twice; the room tunes it). Two hands: up at 1.6x (Matt's pick after playing the room, 10:31 PM), down slow. The held moment between two
pushes is the choice: two hands to race a pod's beat, one to slide out of trouble, or a wait a vine over. Straight up or down always climbs; a diagonal still jumps off to a
bough. **What comes at him:** ripe pods down a column on a beat (a creak and fluff trickling 1.0 s before); a FLYER crossing the field at one height on a steady pass (its call
1.0 s before; the keeper picks the bird: Matt's bat is a night animal); a file of leafcutter ants walking a vine (slow: a nip if he shares it). Pods are dodged SIDEWAYS (change
vine), the flyer UP or DOWN (race or slide), the ants by timing. Two hands span two columns, so the fast way is the exposed way. **The cost:** any of the three knocks him down to
the catch bough (sound wood within 450 px), and on Normal costs a HEART. Nothing he does harms them. **Fruit:** passion fruit and figs hang on the vines: the climb's pickups.
**The purpose: push the fruit up to open the way (Matt chose A, 10:31 PM; the field is TAUGHT here and comes back in later levels, grown).** **A, Lift the bough:** each way up is
shut by a bough sagging under fruit clusters; climbing up under one pushes it up its vine to the bough, where it tears free and drops down its column; the bough lifts a notch for
each and springs open on the last; his order. **The danger is in the push (Matt):** under a cluster he climbs at 0.6x (two hands 1.0x) and cannot slide; when a pod's tell sounds
on his vine he steps a vine over, the cluster stays snagged where he left it, and he steps back. Options B and C (this file at 29d87e69) were not taken.

**Rules (checks the build passes, both settings).** First of everything safe: the first rot over sound wood, the first field with nothing falling, the first pod in a column he is
not on, the first cluster on a quiet vine, the flyer and the ants watched from the halfway bough, the first snake asleep. Pods fair: the tell 1.0 s+ before one lets go; a free
vine within one push of every vine a pod falls on; sound wood within 450 px under every point. Steady, then grown (R-165.174): every column in beats 4-5 keeps ONE beat and every
field one spacing; the beat quickens only from beat 7. Never two tells inside 1.5 s (World 1: one thing at a time); ants and pods never share a vine; the flyer never crosses a
rest or a flag. A freed cluster lands beside any animal, never on it. Dot's lines on Normal's DEFAULT Tips on the real page (D50); drawcheck and every stage gate play 1-4 (D51).

hollow packs six crossings into 30 s. The flyer's and the ants' passes TAKE crossings one for one, they do not add them. Scored with score.ts's own `combine`
(reference/qa-reports/F15/design14-score.ts.txt). **Option, not decided (R-165.173): the double jump taught here** if 1-1's doubles move: beat 1 teaches it safe (off a vine's top
