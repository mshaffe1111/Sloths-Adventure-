## Tributes (quotes as PITCH-36A/B cite them, from pages they opened; D58)
- **Terraria** (Re-Logic 2011; post-2005). a. Loved: "How nervously I threw a torch over the edge to find out what new creature was emitting the awful growling noise" (pcgamer.com,
  Terraria review 2011). b. Taught: you carry light and place it; throwing one is the player's own discovery. c. Ours: THAT moment by design: the next shelf is black until the toss lights it
  (beat 2). d. Not copied: crafting, digging, endless torches (one cap; a patch regrows it).
- **Adventure** (Atari 2600, 1980; pre-1990). a. Loved: Warren Robinett: "I liked the idea of staying in real-time, so I made it so you can only carry one object at a time"
  (gamedeveloper.com, Warren Robinett reflects). b. Taught: the key lies beside its gate [S]. c. Ours: one thing in his paws changes what his thumb does; the puzzle is when to let go.
  d. Not copied: the dragons, the bat.
- **Montezuma's Revenge** (Parker Brothers 1984). a. Loved: the descent hunt, "searching for treasure by descending in a large pyramid" (gamedeveloper.com, World design for 2D
  action-adventures); [S] "Joe has to find a torch if he wants to see anything" (hardcoregaming101, search-index text). b. Taught: the dark rooms show nothing until you hold the torch.
  c. Ours: the light is found at the entrance and carried. d. Not copied: one-touch deaths, keys.
**Different:** 3-1 waits for light, 3-2 hides from it, 3-4 climbs in light that moves by itself; here he MOVES the light. 1-5's keepsake is a crouch at a fern you can see.
**Pushback (design):** (1) The pick's "toss on one tap" is the KICK button, not Jump: the kit maps Jump to the HIGH toss (apex 303, only 131 px on); flown, the forward arc is the Kick
(294 px). ENG-LIGHT keeps the kit's mapping; Dot's line must name Kick. (2) A forward toss cannot land on a shelf above him (flown: it hits the side): up-shelves are Jump-toss beats only,
and the ENG-LIGHT verdict must use both flown arcs, never one. (3) The carried ring shows a 160 px drop only 95 px out: the design leans on that (the toss is needed), so no rim may be
"lit by carrying" in the verdict when it is a drop. **Learned:** fly the BUTTON the player presses, not the throw's name: "flick up" and "toss" were two buttons on the Pad.
