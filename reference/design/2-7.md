player crosses at 87-137 px/s, half a bank run; skill buys 1.5-2.4 s a river (middle over ride-only). The jumps home are 5% longer, not "the longest in World 2" (PITCH-26B): the home leg's thrill is the
race, not the leap. (2) **Against the current, boldness pays**: hopping onto oncoming logs is the fastest water in the game for an expert (222-232 px/s) and slow for the careful (69-133); riding never
crosses. So the out leg is the skill test and `twin` -80 is too much for a careful player (29 px/s): the out leg's hard row is `upstream` -75. (3) The race can only be decided where his thumb is faster
than the current: the banks, the bounce, not waiting on a log.
