# 1-8 The Long Branch: the level as designed (D17-18, 2026-11-13 4:32 PM ET). DESIGN ONLY: no build until the lead's look, the tribute check (a/b/c, D58) and the engine study (R-165.205)
**One idea:** "The branch is breaking behind you: hop the nuts that roll, run under the fruit that bounces, never stop." A player says: "the one where the whole branch falls apart
behind you and you swing off the tip as it snaps." (All three lists; Matt's #5 "jump the low, pass under the high", MAP-REVISIONS.md:108.)
**The chase, measured from code first (chase.ts:21-40; packet facts 3-4).** The collapse runs 230 px/s against his 285 (physics.ts:13), up to 420 when he is 560+ px ahead, +60 on
the last stretch (290: faster than his run); it starts 520 px behind, cracks the sections 300 px ahead of its front, catches at 24 px; caught or off the branch = a heart, the last
flag, the branch whole, a 1.6 s hold. So the **stop budget** (gap - 24) / speed is about 2.2 s at the start, 2.3 s at 560 px ahead, 1.85 s on the last stretch: every chase beat is
passable IN STRIDE (bot-proven, both settings). A whole liana swing (2.85 s, physics.ts:471) is 655 px of collapse, more than the 520 px start: every chase liana is phased to a
runner's arrival, or it is unfair, not hard. Today the chase is the hand builder's only (protoStage.ts:499, :6326); ChasePlan holds one start/end (chase.ts:13-19); no Easy numbers.
**Difficulty:** Normal **target 7.5** (DIFFICULTY.md §4: after 1-7's breather at 5.5, under 1-9's 9.5; the lead's ruling 4:36 PM; the old 1-8 reads 32.2, §4: the gen2 chase comes
DOWN into the band). Hard because: every move timed by the front (a heart, x1.25), two heights read at a run, one threat at a time but only 1.5 s apart at the tightest (the lead, 4:36 PM),
a stop from full run (the twist), a last stretch where the front out-runs him. **Easy ~4** (at most 0.6 x Normal = 4.5): the front at most 200 px/s and never faster than his run (NEW 6),
nuts 2.5 s+ apart, the fruit's arc higher and slower, the twist's pass harmless, sound boughs for creaky ones. **Length** ~165 s designed; bots must read 2:00+ quick, or a fourth stretch goes in (more pieces, never padding).
**Goal** petals. **Cast** the harpy eagle (one post, the twist only). **Look** one huge branch rising gently from its trunk to a far cannonball tree (M-10-d, inventory w1.json), mist below, tiny squirrel tracks beside his (the clue, src/components/stageClue.ts:23).
**Why the nuts roll at him** (list C): the branch climbs toward the far tree, so what it drops rolls DOWN at him. Low: a round palm nut, rolling and rattling (tap: hop it). High: a cannonball fruit bouncing tall with its own ground shadow (do nothing: run under). One glance: small and rolling = hop; big and bouncing = stay down.

## Tributes (the lead checks a/b/c before any build, D58; sources carried from lists A-C, search results only)
**Jungle Hunt / Jungle King (Taito, 1982), the boulder hill (Matt's #5).** a. Small rocks roll (jump them), big ones bounce (run under or duck); one glance, one answer, at a run (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_Hunt; https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Jungle_Hunt; https://www.mobygames.com/game/133). b. It is the third scene, after the vines and the river, so players meet it fluent in the jump; no source on its first boulders (none claimed).
c. Beats 1-2 teach each height alone and safe before the crack, then K3 sets them in rhythm, 1.5 s+ apart; tap for the small one, nothing for the big one; the fruit's shadow gives its height on a phone. d. Not copied: the explorer, the hill, the cannibals, the rescue, the scoring, the look.
**Pitfall! (Activision, 1982), the rolling logs.** a. A log costs points, never a life: the gentlest hazard sets a run's rhythm without ending it (https://vgpavilion.com/mags/1982/12/joystik/pitfall; https://www.hardcoregaming101.net/pitfall/). b. Players learn the jump on logs long before a mistake can end a run (the manual: https://discmaster.textfiles.com/view/1886/1000gamemanuals.iso/Atari%202600/Pitfall.txt). c. Beats 1-2: the first nut and fruit only nudge him back. d. Not copied: Harry, logs, pits, crocodiles, treasure, the look.
**Donkey Kong (Nintendo, arcade 1981), the barrel.** a. The jump was made FOR a rolling barrel: one threat, one verb, timing that rewards nerve (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_(1981_video_game); https://tcrf.net/Prerelease:Donkey_Kong_(Arcade)). b. The first screen rolls barrels down sloped girders so they are seen coming far off (https://www.ataricompendium.com/archives/articles/donkey_kong/donkey_kong.html). c. Our branch slopes up, so the nuts roll down at him, heard (thunk) and seen dropping in before they roll. d. Not copied: barrels, the ape, girders, hammers, ladders.
**Crash Bandicoot (Naughty Dog, 1996), "Boulders".** a. A boulder behind, keep running: the threat behind, the answers ahead (https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Videogame/CrashBandicoot1996; https://medium.com/@varunbajaj/crash-bandicoot-designing-games-to-balance-challenge-and-fun-a-game-design-critique-225af9d0661a). b. One of the easiest levels, moved earlier for balance (https://tcrf.net/Proto:Crash_Bandicoot/April_8,_1996_build); its known flaw is obstacles seen late (the critique above).
c. Ours is soft on purpose: slower than his run, every beat passable in stride, a rest at each living fork, the camera leading, the threat told by sound and the crack. d. Not copied: Crash, the boulder, the toward-camera view, the temple, the music; nobody is crushed: a miss is a heart and the flag.
## Nature check (left for the story keeper)
The cannonball tree (Couroupita guianensis) grows from Costa Rica to Brazil and Bolivia; its round woody fruit reaches about 25 cm and 1 kg (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couroupita_guianensis; https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/2/8/2827); palm nuts roll. A bouncing fruit is storybook; a breaking bough is a real canopy danger. The harpy: 1-7.md.
