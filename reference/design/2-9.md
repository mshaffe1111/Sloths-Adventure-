**One idea:** "The whole court dances to the king's drum: hop, ride, swing and bop on the beat, and the King's Barge carries you to the shrine." A player says: "the one where everything
moves to the drum." **Why a finale** (B): every World 2 toy had its own clock (logs, pads, the relay, stones, the wheel); here they all keep ONE beat you can hear. Nothing new to learn: the
old toys make a song. **The ending** (A): the King's Barge drifts him into the shrine steps and he rings the court bell; the drum's last BOOM lands on the next ONE. **No guards** (A's guards on
rafts are parked, PICKS-29-32): the gauntlet's defeats are 2-5's snappers, bopped on the beat (Pushback 1: the royal guard's journal card).
**The beat, read from the code:** one liana swing (physics.ts:534 TIMED.period 2.85 s; Easy 3.3, setTimedSwing :552) is one bar of four: **B = 0.7125 s Normal (84.2 BPM), 0.825 s Easy (72.7)**.

  holds (margin 0.90 s); Normal at the run pitch he runs off the pad's far end into the water (stopping instead: stood 1.03-1.33 s on a 1.0 s pad, it sinks). The drum's lesson in one rule: one beat a pad.
- **Snap stones on the beat** (2-5's jaws, beatBrick.ts brickNow('bites') along his real path; counts of B, bite on 4; the bar starts on his first hop = ONE). Normal (water 60): **2 in step: first hop on
  ONE or TWO is safe** (any phase 41%); **3 in step: only from ONE** (16%); 5 in step never. **The WAVE (each stone a count later): 5 stones safe from ONE or TWO, 65% of any phase**: on the beat the wave

  Normal's 65%), so **Easy lays no wave longer than 2** (rule 4, as 2-5).
