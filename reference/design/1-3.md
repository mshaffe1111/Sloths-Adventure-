# 1-3 Branch Snakes: the level as designed AND built (W1C, 2026-11-12). It is in World 1's shipped kit: the game rolls it
**One idea:** Pokey does not have to fight. A snake is on a fallen log; a hive hangs over it; a vine hangs by his hand. Shake the vine,
the hive drops, the snake glides away, nobody is hurt. The whole level grows that one idea, and its last room asks for all of it.
**Goal:** purge: every TRAP (11 of 15 snakes on Normal, Matt's three-in-four share of what the stage holds; the four on bare logs are
his choice). A stomp, a punch at the log's end, a kick or a trap all end in the same glide. **Length:** 3
minutes for a first-timer (never under 2), 2.5 for a quick player. **Cast:** the Golden Tree Snake only. **Look:** fallen logs on root
banks in warm evening light; the route stays LEVEL (steps of 50 px, no pits, no fall that costs anything): the only risk is a strike.
**Where it lives:** `src/game/gen2/worlds/w1Arc13.ts` (graph, brief, every piece, the words), `w1Cast.ts` (snake rows, trap pairs,
arming), `stageTable.ts` (its row). Hand-built 1-3 is deleted. `tools/engine/library13.test.ts` holds the level beat for beat.

## Rules of this level (each one a check the build passes, in library13.test.ts unless named)
- **The first of everything is safe.** First snake asleep under the first hive. First awake snake has a trap. First bare snake sleeps.
- **Dot teaches before the vine:** her trap line is a TEACH line said at the lesson's flag, 800-1,100 px (3 s at a walk) before the
  first vine, and no animal stands before it. (Said 232 px before, a first-timer had walked past the vine before he had read it.)
- **A trap is in plain view and safe to use:** handle 140-190 px before its hung load and 105 px or more from the nearest the snake
  can come; the load 100 px over the log, over the middle of the snake's 105 px patch, whose far end is 215 px from the vine (the
  snake is whole on an upright screen wherever it paces); a sleeper lies at the patch's near end, the load landing a coil's width
  BESIDE it, never on it; no pickup in front.
- **Nothing strands him:** every snake can be cleared where he meets it; the rope is never the way and its hollow is a tap's
  climb deep, so he can walk back (a deep pit with steps out one side stranded a bot before the shrine). Dot explains a sealed totem.
- **Easy** keeps EVERY trap (a trap's own snake stays, softened: `Socket.easy 'stay'`) and loses only bare snakes. Its snakes are the
  kinds Normal kept (a socket cannot yet name its own Easy kind: ruling 1, owed), so the Easy-only sleepers in the data do not show yet.
- **Flags, hearts and the goal are the game's own rules** (Matt's; a design table never overrides a verdict-enforced rule): a flag
  before every hard beat and no span over 4,000 px (12 on a roll), a heart by every second flag once something can hurt (6), the goal
  three in four of the animals. My first table asked for 3 flags and 2 hearts; a child bot's 4 hits a run say the game is right.
- **Words (all the story keeper's):** the mission line; the lesson's "See that hive? Punch the vine, down it comes!" then "Don't
  worry, it only gives him a fright."; "No vine? Walk him to the end of his branch."; "Off he glides, not a scratch, just very
  offended!" on the first trap sprung. The tester survey's names for its beats are hers too (ctestStretches.ts).

draws a block a full step thick as a green hedge, and one over 400 px long as two pieces with a seam.
