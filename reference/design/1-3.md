# 1-3 Branch Snakes: the level as designed (W1C, 2026-11-12). The build follows this; if the builder changes, build from this
**One idea:** Pokey does not have to fight. A snake is on the branch; a hive hangs over it; a vine hangs by his hand. Shake the vine,
the hive drops, the snake glides away, nobody is hurt. The whole level grows that one idea, and its last room asks for all of it.
**Goal:** purge (clear every snake; a stomp, a punch at the branch end, a kick or a trap all end in the same glide). **Length:** 3
minutes for a first-timer (never under 2), 2.5 for a quick player. **Cast:** the Golden Tree Snake only. **Look:** low logs and root
banks in warm evening light; the route stays LEVEL (steps of 50 px, no pits, no fall that costs anything): the only risk is a strike.

## Rules of this level (each one a check the build must pass)
- **The first of everything is safe.** First snake asleep under the first hive. First awake snake has a trap. First bare snake backs
  away. First tendril (beat 6) has its handle outside the strike like every handle. First perch (7) is the only handle not on his road.
- **A trap is always in plain view and always safe to use:** handle 140-190 px before its hung load, 105 px or more from the nearest
  the snake can come; the load 100 px up over the middle of the snake's 122 px patch; no pickup on the handle's ledge or the branch.
- **The exit never locks him out of a clear:** every snake can be cleared from where he stands when he meets it (a trap, or its own
  branch end to punch it off). A snake he walked past is still on its branch behind him: Dot explains a locked totem.
- **Hearts and flags follow risk:** flags at beats 1, 5, 8 (never more than about 60 s apart); hearts at 5 and 10 only. He has 5
  hearts and a strike costs 1: a first-timer who learns nothing still finishes; nobody needs a third heart.
- **Easy** keeps every lesson and drops only pressure: 8 snakes for 12, the finale's rest ledge, sleepers where Normal has pacers.
- **Words:** two lines only, both the story keeper's: the lesson's tip and "Off he glides...". Nothing else speaks.
