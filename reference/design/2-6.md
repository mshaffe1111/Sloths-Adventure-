Every wheel, both settings: he stays in his bucket a whole turn and more doing nothing ("stayed in"); a bucket rising past the jetty never lifts him off it (he stays on the jetty: it passes behind);
**a missed step-off rides round again** (a turn, 14-25 s); only a walk or hop at the wrong moment drops him, always into the pond under the wheel (never a pit). **What the flights say:** (1) boarding is
easy at every phase (the bottom of a turning wheel is a shelf of buckets): the teach is the ride and the step-off, not the board. (2) A street reaching IN over the top (the buckets pass behind it)
doubles the step-off window against one at the sweep's edge: the teach uses it. (3) **The carry does not throw him** (PITCH-26B beat 8, unflown there): a held jump out of a bucket at the top
clears 171-174 px level vs 162 from a stand on still ground (+9-12 px): steering toward his way, any speed past his run (285) is braked in the air (physics.ts:198-204 airStep, the engine's own order), so the bucket's speed adds almost nothing. Beat 8 is a jump UP
off the top instead (15-16% of a turn, 2.3-2.6 s; Easy 60 px: 4.1 s). **Numbers for the engine: Normal R180 V70 N6, teach R160 V60; Easy V x 0.85 (V50-60); buckets 120 px, DROP 28 px; one
bucket every 2.4-3.4 s; a step-off window >= 1.5 s a turn on Normal, >= 2.5 s on Easy; the pond under every wheel; nothing solid inside the sweep but the jetty and the street, drawn in front.**
