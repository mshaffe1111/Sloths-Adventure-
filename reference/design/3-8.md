## Tributes (quotes as PITCH-36B cites them, from pages it opened; D58)
- **Frogger** (Konami 1981; Matt's own favourite; new reason: the DIVING turtles). a. Loved and feared: "you see some of the turtles submerging, and you get scared that the ones you are on
  will submerge as well" (gamedeveloper.com, What makes Frogger fun). b. Taught: turtles show themselves going under first. c. Ours: every sleeper's eye cracks a whole swing before it looks.
  d. Not copied: the timer, instant drowning (Easy drops him on moss).
- **Klonoa: Door to Phantomile** (Namco 1997). a. Loved: "take a simple ability - grabbing and throwing enemies - and show how to use it a hundred different ways" (gamesradar, Klonoa
  review). b. Taught: the first enemies are slow and harmless [S]. c. Ours: the danger IS the handhold, and the first can't hurt (a 12 s sleep over moss). d. Not copied: throwing them.
- **Shovel Knight** (Yacht Club 2014). a. Loved: "jump and then shovel for a downward, pogo stick-like attack" (pcgamer, Shovel Knight review): what threatens you becomes your way
  across. b. Taught: the first targets are harmless [S]. c. Ours: soft moss under the first sleeper. d. Not copied: attacking, the pogo.
**Different:** 3-5 sneaks past sleeping vines; 3-8 rides them. 1-6 asked where to let go, 2-4 onto what; here WHEN, on a clock that belongs to the danger.
**Pushback (design):** (1) **PLAN-W3's D7 ENG-THORNSWING was A's idea** (thorns over a liana gap, swinging unseen); the pick is B's, so the engine job is the holdable sleeper (re-scoped
here, same size). (2) **The goal line says "without touching the thorns"** while the stage is riding them: the keeper's call. (3) **The stir must be LONGER than the old maker's** (protoStage.ts:2972 thornChain:
stir 1.1 s, look 1.8 s): a 1.1 s eye-crack can fall between two windows (worst wait 2.17 s flown), so a rider who obeys the tell is dropped: unfair; 3.3 s is the floor.
**Learned:** fly the let-go WINDOW at every frame of a swing, not the sweet-spot numbers: the window onto a real shelf is 0.70 s of 2.85, narrower than sweetFrom..sweetTo suggests.
