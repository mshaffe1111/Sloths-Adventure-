## follow.ts: the engine packet's contract (NEW 1, M: 3-4 h by B's estimate, unmeasured; level.md rules 21-23)
**Data** (a w2.ts row, Designer numbers): `FOLLOW = { pace: { normal: 190, easy: 230 }, gap: 34, scamper: 300, rejoin: 60 }`. **State:** `Trail { pts: {x, y, s, wait?}[]; s }` (his feet, arclength
s); `Follower { kind: 'duck' | 'otter' | 'capy'; s; x; y; moved; state: 'join' | 'follow' | 'spooked' | 'home' }`. **Pure API** (src/game/follow.ts, no engine, no canvas): `newTrail(x, y)`;
`record(t, p, mode)` with mode `walk` (grounded, or airborne from his own ground jump) / `hold` (a climb, grab, ride or fall: the last point becomes a WAIT, never passed until he is back on the ground);
`stepLine(t, line, pace, gap, dt)` (follower i chases the one ahead along the trail, at most pace px/s, never closer than gap, never past a WAIT); `join(line, from)` (a freed one: a drop from its
cage to the ground or water below, then straight to the tail at pace); `spook(f, setbackX)` / `rejoin(f, heX)`; `regroup(t, line, at)` (bunched behind him: a flag or a splash-back); `home(line, exitX)`.
Over splash-back water a follower keeps its trail s and is drawn at the surface, paddling. **Never:** climb, ride, fall, take a heart, be hurt. **The heron (NEW 2, S ~1 h):** World 2's watcher
cast (gen2/stillLay.ts castWatches, as World 1's eagle) drawn from dressnew-w2#w2Heron; its look sweeps the ZONE in world x (x0 - 160 to x1 + 160 over WatchZone.pass), not the view (Pushback 1),
and reads each little one: `watcherSees({ seesStill: true }, inSight, !f.moved, inCover(...))`; a seen one is `spook`ed to the zone's setback; Pokey is never read. **gen2's check** (lineFaults,
next to stillFaults): a hide < 260 px (Easy 340); open mud over 500 px (two herons: 350; Easy: 350); a watched pond over 360 px; two herons whose longest safe window < 2.0 s; a climb whose foot and dismount don't share
ground; a post cage whose drop doesn't land on ground or water joined to the foot. **Proven on planted toys** (each RED with its plant): the line on a bare floor (D 600: widest 357 +-10 px,
bunched 1.0 +-0.2 s; plant: no pace cap); the hop replay (a follower's apex at his hop's x +-4 px; plant: record grounded only); the climb (no follower past the foot while he holds; plant: WAIT
ignored); the heron (one follower outside cover in the look is spooked, one still in cover is not; plant: read Pokey); the regroup at a flag; the goal (home 5 / Easy 3). **Bot skills** (rule 21,
tools/inspect/firsttimer.ts): `wait-for-the-tail` (go right after a look; stand in cover until the line is still inside) and `fetch-the-spooked`, each on a toy RED with the skill removed.
