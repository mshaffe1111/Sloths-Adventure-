DESIGN ONLY: no build before its engine (NEW 1-2 below, each proven on a real stageTable row, level.md rule 23)
**One idea:** "Going down into the dark, stand still at the edge and the glowworms below wake and show you where to drop." A player says: "the one where you wait for the glowworms to
light up the way down." **The hook:** World 3 is the patience world and stillness is a sloth's own nature: the thumb's job here is to NOT press, then drop. The first gen2 stage that goes
DOWN. **The teach** (B's kind falls, Pitfall II): every drop in the teach is soft, so the first dark costs seconds, never a heart. 3-1 teaches DARK; 3-2 teaches HANG UNDER (one new thing a stage).

## Tributes (quotes as PITCH-29A/B cite them, from pages they opened; D58)
- **Metroid** (Nintendo 1986). a. Loved: "dark, stark, lonely, and creepy - full of impossibly long corridors and extremely steep drops" (gamedeveloper.com, Retro Game of the Day). b. Taught
  with no words: the shafts invite the drop. c. Ours: the steep drop as wonder, never death (no fall hurts, flown). d. Not copied: the dread, the guns, the maze.
- **Pitfall II: Lost Caverns** (Activision 1984). a. Loved: it "discards entirely lives, health, and even death... remove the requirement of having to be really good to see it all" (gamedeveloper.com,
  Design Essentials 20). b. Taught by going down screen by screen; crosses mark the return. c. Ours: every teach drop soft; a wrong drop past the teach costs a flag's walk, never the run.
- **Donkey Kong Country** (Rare 1994). a. Loved: "painstakingly arranged so that the player could 'go first time'" (gamesradar, The making of DKC); Squawks' lantern. b. Torchlight Trouble's light
  shows the next platform. c. Ours: the light shows the landing before he needs it; the puff beat is our blink. d. Not copied: a carried light (3-6), lights that cut out mid-jump.
**Different:** the first gen2 stage going down; the first where the dark hides the way; the first where NOT moving is the move (1-7's bush hides him; here stillness reveals).
**Pushback (design):** the pitch's "a shaft needs the lights below to wake" is backwards on main: the wake already reaches every y (flown: 3000 px below wakes); the job is to BOUND it.
The pitch's Easy "glowworms wake at once" would skip the lesson on Easy; a shorter stillness (0.4 s) keeps it. **Learned:** fly which lights wake, not only when: a reach read by x alone looked fine
on flat 3-x and is wrong in a shaft.
