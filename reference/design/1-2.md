# 1-2 The Petal Trail: the level as designed (D12, 2026-11-12). The build follows this; if the builder changes, build from this
**One idea:** Meeca's petals lie low on bark, and across the thorns the only bark is a mushroom's throw apart: he walks into the
mushroom and it carries him cap to cap over the thorn bed to her next petals. The level grows that idea (over, longer, up, nothing to
stand on, DOWN where no thorns grow, the long crossing by her island) and its last room asks for all of it. A player says: "the one
where you bounce over the thorns, following her petals." **Goal:** harvest (85-90% of reachable petals Normal, 65% of that Easy),
every petal on the road a thumb-held run takes. **Length:** ~3 min first-timer (178 s on the time model, 185 px/s; ~145 s fast). No
cast. **Look:** rain (w1rain backdrop), root banks, painted thorn hollows; LEVEL. **Built on** P12S's kit road (listedRoll, w1Arc12.ts).

## Rules of this level (each a check the build must pass)
- **The first of everything is safe:** thorns seen before any is asked (`hazard.untaught`; none within 1,300 px of the spawn); the first
  chain on 160 px stumps on both settings; the first no-thorn hollow floored (no gap, nothing to lose).
- **Every chain inside chain.md §3, both settings:** cap to cap S 205-220 level, 198-206 up 24-36, 185-200 up 54-66, 216-224 down; stumps
  110-130 \| 150-170; 2-5 caps \| 2-3 (2 either side of an island); exit bank 40-100 \| 40-80 past the last stump, top 0-90 \| 0-60 over
  it; the floor mushroom 20-60 px before the lip; no double gap over thorns. The lift's face 160-180 px: past a single jump's 123 plus the
  26 px hoist (the mushroom is the way out), under the bounce's 223 with 40 px+ spare (chain.md §1).
- **Matt's three, every roll, both settings, never loosened:** `chain.mount` (every first cap a walk-on floor mushroom, 30 px),
  `landing.dead-zone` (every petal's bark reached by a walk-off, full jump or thumb-held bounce, with margin, the arc uncut),
  `bounce.no-purpose` (every mushroom carries him over thorns or up a face a walk does not climb).
- **Petals low on bark the whole way** (the story's rule, brief `petals: 'low'`): never over a gap, a cap or a stump he bounces past.
  **The thorn bed only under 1-2's chains** (`pits: 'lesson'`; w1-thorn, painted, the pit polish on this base), 150-170 px deep.
- **Every roll is this level:** one graph, one brick a beat (`dealExact`); a test holds the 14 beats in order, every seed, both settings.
## Hearts and flags (Matt's rules, never this table's: STAGE-RULES §1, ROLLED_FLAGS pipeline.ts:1343, V21; lesson D49)
- **Flags:** the lesson's first ledge, every rest, her island, the finale's middle bank, the last within 2,000 px of the totem; one within
  2,400 px before every hard beat (T X W S C); never under 900 px apart; no span over 2,300 px across thorns on Normal (a thorn costs a
  heart there), 4,000 elsewhere and on Easy; a beat whose thorns run longer carries its own flag socket. About 12 on Normal.
- **Hearts:** he has 5; on Normal a thorn costs 1 and sets him back on the ledge he left (engine.ts heartFall). One heart on the bank
  just after U, X and S, each after a stretch that can cost one: 3. **Easy: none** (a thorn sets him back with the hurt sound and a
  blink, no heart). Laid by the bricks' data, not the game's heart-by-every-second-flag pass.
- **Easy** keeps every lesson, drops only pressure: stumps 160 (a miss by 40 px lands on wood; 20 on Normal); caps T 2, P 2, U 2, X 3,
  S 2+2+2 with two islands, C 2 and 2; thorns soft; no hearts; the same hollows, lifts, petals. Never harder than Normal in any number.
