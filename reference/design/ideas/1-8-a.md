## TRIBUTES (web search 2026-11-13; page fetches not tried, every fact from search results at the URLs cited)

**Donkey Kong (Nintendo, arcade 1981): jump the rolling barrel**
a. The jump was invented FOR the barrel ("If you had a barrel rolling towards you, what would you do? ... you'd jump over it!"): one clear
   threat, one clear verb, a timing that rewards nerve (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_Kong_(1981_video_game),
   https://tcrf.net/Prerelease:Donkey_Kong_(Arcade)).
b. The first screen sends barrels down the girders where you can watch them come from the top; later loops throw faster and diagonally
   (https://www.ataricompendium.com/archives/articles/donkey_kong/donkey_kong.html).
c. Ours: a tap jumps the low pod; taught on calm branch before the chase, then asked for at pace. The pod rolls along the bark toward him,
   rumbling, so the ear sees it before the eye.
d. Not copied: barrels, the ape, girders, hammers, ladders, any Nintendo art or sound.

**Kung-Fu Master (Irem, arcade 1984): high or low, jump or duck**
a. Knife throwers throw high or low; you read the height and jump or duck; sometimes two threats at once force a choice
   (https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Kung-Fu_Master, https://arcade-museum.com/Videogame/kung-fu-master).
b. The first floor introduces one enemy kind at a time down one long corridor (strategywiki, above).
c. Ours: two pods that look and sound different (roll vs bounce); the thumb holds down to duck under the high one. The pair "hop-duck"
   is the stage's rhythm. Never two at once until the finale, and never on Easy.
d. Not copied: fighting, knives, the temple, any character or art.

**Crash Bandicoot "Boulders" (Naughty Dog, 1996): the chase that never stops**
a. The first chase level: a boulder behind, one route, run flat out; loved for the panic (https://www.gamepressure.com/crash-bandicoot-n-sane-trilogy/boulders/z59eb5,
   https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Concepts/Running_Towards_the_Screen).
b. It comes after the player knows the moves; its known flaw is the camera: obstacles appear with little reaction time
   (https://medium.com/@varunbajaj/crash-bandicoot-designing-games-to-balance-challenge-and-fun-a-game-design-critique-225af9d0661a).
c. Ours runs sideways with the threat behind and the camera leading ahead (bullet 13), the crack line as the warning, and a restart at the
   last flag with the branch whole (Matt, 2026-10-24, chase.ts:5-7): panic without unfairness.
d. Not copied: the boulder, the camera angle, any character, temple or art.
