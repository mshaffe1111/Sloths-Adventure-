# 1-4 "Up the Kapok": idea list B (idea helper B, 2026-11-12 6:09 PM ET)

1. **THE ONE IDEA:** "Two vines are fast, but the fast lane is where the fruit falls": every stretch up the kapok asks him to pick speed or shelter, until he learns to clear the lane himself.
2. **The foot (first climb, taught):** buttress roots, Meeca's petals walking up the bark; one short single vine straight up to a wide low bough with ground right under it; nothing can go wrong. Feels: "oh, this one goes UP."
3. **Two vines (taught):** a single vine and a pair hang side by side to the same bough, petals on both; Dot's line names the pair; he sees the pair is twice as fast, then a second pair where it plainly pays. Feels: clever, quick.
4. **Rotting boughs (taught):** the first rotting bough sits over a sound bough one step below; it sags, creaks, shows its notched underside and drops after a beat; he lands on the sound one and loses nothing. Then "now you": two in a row to cross at a walk, no stopping. Feels: nerves, then "I can read that."
5. **Fruit down the trunk (taught):** a rest ledge BESIDE the column; high above, a laden bough shakes and a shadow grows on the bark, then a kapok pod tumbles past and bursts into white fluff (a soft knock, never a splat); he watches the rhythm. Then a single vine with a knot-hole halfway to step into; the first knock drops him only to the bough below. Feels: "watch, wait, go."
6. **Fast lane or safe lane (grown):** the pair hangs right in the fruit column, the single vine climbs under a bough overhang out of it; petals reward the brave lane; both lanes always reach the top. Feels: choice and nerve.
7. **Rotting steps between vines (grown):** short rotting boughs are the only footing between vine stretches, each crumbling as he reaches the next vine (required now, whole again on respawn); one stretch adds fruit on top. Feels: flow, keep moving.
8. **Rest high up:** a wide sound bough, a bromeliad, Meeca's scratched petal in the bark, a flag; the camera shows the forest floor far below. Feels: pride, a breath.
9. **THE TWIST: the snake climbs too.** A Golden Tree Snake starts up the pair BELOW him and a single vine can't outclimb it; at a laden bough he shakes its vine (1-3's handle) and the pods tumble down the column; the snake glides off to the next tree, nobody hurt. The fruit that knocked him off all level is his to drop, kindly.
10. **THE FINALE:** the crown stretch: pairs in the fruit lane, singles under shelter, rotting boughs as the only steps, one laden bough to shake to clear the lane for a sprint, and one more snake to send gliding; a flag at its start, and a miss costs one stretch only.
11. **The moment people talk about:** he pushes up through the last leaves and is ABOVE the whole forest for the first time; the camera pulls all the way back at sunset, the last petal sits in a bromeliad on the crown, Dot goes quiet (her "dead stop when something is real"); a far-north speck of the tower on the skyline only if the story keeper agrees.
12. **The trap to avoid: losing height unfairly.** A fall that costs a minute of climbing, or a pod from off-screen (a sideways phone shows little height) makes a climb cruel and dull; every drop's tell is on screen first, every knock lands on the bough below, and no vine stretch is long enough to be just "hold up."
