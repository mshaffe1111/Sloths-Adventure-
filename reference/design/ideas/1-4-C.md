# 1-4 Up the Kapok: idea list C (idea helper C, 2026-11-12 6:15 PM ET; packet IDEAS-1-4.md)

1. **The one idea:** "The one where you climb the giant tree between falling pods: two vines to dash, a hollow to hide, up to see where they took Meeca."
2. **Arrive, the roots:** buttress roots in mist, the last petal low on the bark, a heap of burst pods at the foot (explained at the top); the camera tilts up the trunk into the leaves. He walks in, small: "I have to see where they went."
3. **The first climb (nothing can hurt):** a vine from a root to the first bough, then a taller one; the stage turns from going right to going UP and the camera leads him. He climbs vine to bough to vine; the floor drops away; steady.
4. **Two vines (taught):** a single and a pair side by side to the same bough, Dot's line first (the story keeper's; never "slow"); on the pair he reaches with both arms, twice as fast (up still always climbs); then a long pair alone. "Whoa, fast!"
5. **Rotting boughs (taught, then needed):** the first wears bracket fungus and a notched underside, creaks, shakes and drops him a short hop onto sound wood (nothing lost); then two or three are the only footing between vines, crossed without stopping; whole again on respawn. "Not the spongy ones."
6. **Falling pods (taught):** first watched from a safe bough: kapok fluff drifts down, the crown rustles, a squirrel chitters, a pod thumps down the NEXT column; then a hollow beside his own vine: climb, tuck in, it passes, go. The squirrels toss their harvest to their store, not at him (pokeyJournal.ts:51 already puts them in 1-4). "Listen... now!"
7. **His pick, then a breath:** a single vine with two hollows (sure, stop and go) beside a pair with none (one dash between drops); then the halfway bough and Pokey's "Meeca climbs this every Sunday. For fun." Brave or sure, his call.
8. **The tall column (set piece):** the longest stretch: pair and single side by side, pods on both, a rotten stair between, and a vine strung bough to bough to switch columns (the Shimmy Rope 1-3 gave him, skills.ts:37). The rhythm of it.
9. **The twist, his turn to drop one (sits after 7):** a tree snake coils on the pair; he climbs the single beside it and shakes a ripe pod off its stem above (1-3's handle, a pod for a hive); it thumps down beside the snake, never on it, and the snake lets go and glides off, very offended. All of it on one upright screen from where he chooses. "What hit me can help me"; look before you dash.
10. **The finale, the crown:** no rests: a rotten stair to a hollow, a pod passes, a pair dash between drops, one more pod to drop to clear the last pair, the last rotten boughs crumbling behind him, up through the leaves.
11. **The moment:** he climbs out of the canopy into open sky; the camera pulls back over the whole tree, the forest goes quiet, and far north the Highlands ("They are very far": stageClue.ts:19). Dot lands beside him; the squirrels on their pod pile stare. The light rises with him all climb: mist at the roots, sun at the crown.
12. **The trap:** a pod he could not see sending him far back down. Sideways the phone shows little above his head, so every drop's tell (fluff, rustle, chitter) plays inside his screen in time; a SOUND bough (never a rotten one) catches every knock-off within about one section (STAGE-RULES, falls in climbs); no hollow hang long enough to be dead time.
