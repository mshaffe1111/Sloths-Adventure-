## TRIBUTE
### Red Light, Green Light / Statues (the playground game; Matt's "predator shadow, red light green light", MAP-REVISIONS.md:138)
a. The leader turns away ("green light") and everyone creeps forward; on "red light" the leader turns, and anyone seen moving is sent back to the start. Loved for the held breath: the whole game is the instant between running and freezing (https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/red-light-green-light/; https://folklore.usc.edu/childrens-game-american/; https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Statues_(game)). One guide gives two seconds to stop.
b. It teaches itself in one round: the first turn-around catches the greedy runners, and everyone learns to watch the leader's back. Variants soften it (caught players become "yellow lights", not sent back: folklore.usc.edu).
c. Beat 4 and the finale: the screech is the turn-around, with a 2.0 s warn (the game's "two seconds"), and the first pass is free like a practice round; the thumb lifts and Pokey goes green. Sent back = a heart only after the free pass.
d. Not copied: the chant, a human "it", and Squid Game's version (a TV show; its doll and its stakes are nothing of ours). A real hunter of sloths and real canopy cover only.
### Metal Gear (Konami, MSX2, 1987): hiding from a watcher's line of sight
a. Kojima turned a combat brief into a game about NOT fighting: guards see straight ahead, and crossing their line of sight is the one way to be spotted (alert, then everyone comes). Loved because the danger has a shape you can read and the clever player is never seen (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_(video_game); https://www.denofgeek.com/games/why-1987-s-metal-gear-is-still-important/; https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-how-kojima-defied-the-impossible-throughout-i-metal-gear-i-s-history).
b. Snake starts unarmed, so the opening forces sneaking (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_(video_game)). I found no source on how the first area's guards are laid out, so no teaching order is claimed. Its sequel Metal Gear Solid 3 (2004) made camouflage itself a number (a camo index shown as a %) (https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_Solid_3%3A_Snake_Eater/Camouflage).
c. 1-7 takes the readable sight shape (the shadow's 70 px either side, watcher.ts:53) and MGS3's idea that you blend into where you stand, but shows it on Pokey's fur as moss, never as a number.
d. Not copied: soldiers, guns, alerts, boxes, the "!" mark, any % gauge, codecs, the look. No people in World 1; our hunter is a real harpy.
