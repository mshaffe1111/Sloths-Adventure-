**Why each road is worth picking.** At every split her mark's arrow names HER road: it holds the folded petals and, at its far end, the next mark. The other is the squirrels'

HEARTS (snakes, the creek). Normal's goal needs her road at most splits; Easy's is met on shared ground. A missed mark is never lost: every rest has a vine joining the roads.

**The card road (beat 6). What he sees:** berry-juice paintings on big living leaves, each a doodle of what its stretch HOLDS. Beats 1 and 4 teach that the paintings tell the
truth at splits he can see past; only then the curtain. He picks by walking (up the vine or down the ramp): touch only, no menu. The picked sign's squirrel scampers ahead; the
other rolls its leaf up. **How a pick lays the next stretch.** Data: one pick node in the graph holding two stretches (two bricks each; the same entry, exit, marks and points).
Build: from one seed the pipeline lays TWO whole plans, one a pick, identical before the signs and after the join. Each plan is judged, flown, flagged, gated and locked as an
ordinary stage; a seed is proven only if both are; a stage with no pick node builds byte for byte as today. Play: the stage holds both; crossing a sign's line sets the stretch
while its first differing piece is a full sideways screen away (700 px+: no pop-in, nothing per frame); the pick holds through set-backs (the flag is before the lines) and clears
on a new run. No stage id, seed or x in engine code. **If the card road is cut:** the level stands: beat 6 keeps a plain sign pair and the seed lays one of the two stretches
(both stay in the pool); nothing else moves. It loses the reason to play twice.
