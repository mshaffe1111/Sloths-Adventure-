# FEEL-LATTICE: the feel room v2, the vine field, for Matt to play BEFORE 1-4 is built (F15's spec, 10:22 PM ET; design reference/design/1-4.md "The vine field")
1. **What Matt decides:** does climbing vine to vine feel good; is two hands worth it and is one vine ever the right call; is pushing the fruit up under falling pods and a flyer fun or a chore. One question at a time.
2. **Where:** the QA build only (VITE_QA_BUILD, FEEL-PAIR's entry: never the shipped game), built on FEEL-PAIR's grab (t-feel-pair): the pair becomes any two vines in reach.
3. **Moves (no new button):** one vine: up 220 px/s (unchanged everywhere), down a slide. Push toward a neighbour within 90 px: two hands. Two hands: up fast, down slow; push again lets the back vine go. Straight up or down always climbs; a diagonal jumps off.
4. **Speeds on a sign at the foot** (he tries each): two hands up 1.6x or 2x; the slide 1.6x or 2x; two hands down 0.5x. A stopwatch a climb.
5. **Lane 1, pure feel:** sound floor; five vines 80 px apart, 450 px tall, a bough on top; then six of mixed lengths (150-450 px) with one 110 px gap he must jump; eight fruit zig-zagged; a fruit counter. Nothing falls.
6. **Lane 2, pods and the flyer:** the same field, pods down two columns on one steady 3.6 s beat, the creak and fluff 1.0 s before; then a flyer crossing the field at mid height every 4 s, its call 1.0 s before (a code-drawn stand-in, named plainly). A knock drops him to the floor (no hearts in the room); a knock counter; a second sign sets the quicker beats (2.4 s, 3 s).
7. **Lane 3, pushing with things falling:** one cluster low on a quiet vine: climbing under it pushes it up (0.6x; two hands 1.2x; no slide) to the bough, it tears free and drops, the sagging bough lifts a notch. Then a gate of two clusters with pods down the push vines and the flyer's pass: he steps a vine over for a pod, the cluster stays snagged, he steps back. A sign adds a file of ants on one vine (a stand-in).
8. **Rules:** the single vine's feel unchanged everywhere else; no stage moves (the lock); upright and sideways; touch only; 30 fps; the perf budget; every new test in CI; CLAUDE.md "Checks" green.
9. **Return:** stills both ways and a short clip of each lane for the lead to judge (STAGE-RULES §5b); every placeholder named; REPORT.md of 40 lines or fewer; a 10-line message to the lead. No PR.
10. **Not in the room:** the ascent lay, the camera, painted art, hearts, bots. If Matt cuts two hands, lanes 1-3 are replayed on one-hand crossings before anything is built.
