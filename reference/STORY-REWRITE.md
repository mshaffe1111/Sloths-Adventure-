## World 1: Emerald Canopy

### Act card

*Ten realms lay between Pokey and the Highland Fortress. Each one had a guardian, and every guardian answered to the General.*

### Leaving home

*The squirrels who lived under the old vine had put up a sign years ago, the day Pokey sat on that vine for two days without moving. It still said THE SLOTH, with an arrow pointing up.*

*They'd been watching him leave for two days now. He was almost out of sight.*

**Squirrel:** Hey! Sloth! Where are you going?

**Pokey:** North.

**Squirrel:** North! At that speed? See you next year!

*Somewhere behind the squirrel, a lot of smaller squirrels laughed.*

**Pokey:** I'll write.

### The first petal

*The first petal was stuck to the bark of a kapok tree, pressed flat, at about the height of someone being marched past. Pokey peeled it off with one claw and held it in both hands.*

**Pokey:** She pressed it flat. With a soldier on each arm.

**Dot:** Pokey! Pokey, there you are, oh, thank goodness. Don't move. Don't you move. Do you know how many pink things there are in this forest? Do you have any idea? I followed the petals for two days, and one of them was a hibiscus, which is a completely different pink, and I went all the way around a hill for it, a big hill, and—

**Pokey:** You said you'd be straight back.

*Dot's wing went to the pouch at her side.*

**Dot:** I know what I said. I know what I said. And I said don't you go anywhere without me, and here you are. Going.

**Pokey:** Not very fast.

**Pokey:** Did you see her? Is she okay?

**Dot:** She's okay. She's okay. They took her all the way up to the Highlands, Pokey, to the fortress, the big one up in the clouds, and she walked the whole way with her chin up like she was on her way to a party. She thanked them for the march. She thanked every one of them.

**Pokey:** Did they see you?

**Dot:** One of them looked right at me and said, "Is that a hummingbird?" And a different one said, "Where?" And by then I was already in a bush. I stayed in that bush an hour. It was a nice bush.

**Dot:** Okay. Now listen. They put her in a tower. And there's this netting, all the way around the mountain, iron thorns, from the ground up into the clouds, and there's exactly one gap in it. About as wide as a folded letter. Notes go out. Nothing goes in. I tried to carry one in. Twice. Same thorn, both times.

**Pokey:** So I can't tell her I'm coming.

**Dot:** She knows you're coming.

*Pokey didn't say anything to that.*

**Dot:** She got three notes out before they closed it. This is the first one.

**Dot (reading):** "Pokey. If you have this, you found the petal. I put it low so you wouldn't have to look up. I saw you run. I didn't know you could do that. Don't you listen to a word he said. And don't stand in the rain and think about it. Follow the petals. Love, M."

*Pokey read it again himself, slowly, the part about running. Then he put it in the satchel.*

**Pokey:** Which way?

**Dot:** North. Everything's north. The petals go north.

*On a branch above them, something long shifted its weight. It had been told to look fearsome and had been doing it since breakfast. It was a warm morning, and it was tired.*

### Hidden note (in plain sight)

> Pokey, if you're reading this, you're already further than they think you'll get. They walk too fast to look down. I've been dropping things the whole way and nobody's noticed. Keep your eyes low.
>
> Sorry about the handwriting. I'm writing this walking.
>
> P.S. If you see Dot, tell her I saw her in that bush.
>
> Love, M.

### I am fine (halfway through the world)

**Dot:** I've got another one. I was saving it. You looked like you needed it.

**Dot (reading):** "Pokey. I'm fine. I have a window, a chair, a blanket that smells like mothballs, and a lieutenant who says sorry every time he brings the porridge. Nobody has been unkind. I am fine. Love, M."

*Far away, in a tower, a sloth sat up straight at a desk by a window. She had been sitting that way for an hour.*

**Pokey:** Let me see it.

*He ran a claw under the writing. The words sat on the line, evenly, from one edge of the paper to the other.*

**Pokey:** She falls off the end of the line. Always. She runs out of room and writes the last word on top.

**Dot:** Look, she even left margins. She had room for—

*Dot stopped.*

**Pokey:** Yeah.

*He folded the note along its own creases and put it in the satchel with the first one. It took him two tries to get the flap shut.*

### The guardian: Venom Fang

*Before the fight.*

**Venom Fang:** Ssloth. What are you doing on my branch?

**Pokey:** I'm going to get Meeca back.

*The snake looked at him for a long time, from the top of his head all the way down, and then back up, slower.*

**Venom Fang:** You're going to take her from him?

*Something under the snake's collar seemed to pinch. He shifted.*

**Venom Fang:** ...From the General. Up there. I was told to stop anyone who followed her. I pictured a soldier. A hawk, maybe. I did not picture this.

**Dot:** Pokey, he's long. He's really long. How long is he? I can't see the end of him. Where does he end?

**Venom Fang:** Further than you'd like. You'll have to get through me, ssloth. Take all the time you need. Someone has to.

**Pokey:** I usually do.

**Venom Fang:** I have been told to bite you. Hold still. It's quicker for both of us.

*After the fight.*

**Venom Fang:** Beaten. By a sloth. Not even a fast one. The slowest sloth in the whole rainforest.

**Pokey:** Wait till the neighbors hear.

**Venom Fang:** If anyone asks, I tied myself in this knot on purpose.

**Pokey:** Sure you did.

*The snake didn't untie himself. He scratched at the iron collar with the end of his tail, where he thought nobody could see.*

**Venom Fang:** Don't look at the collar. It itches. It always itches. She went north, toward the river. She thanked me too, on the way past. Nobody thanks me. The king at the river has a crown now. Don't laugh at it.

### Hidden keepsake

> A tiny iron key on a green ribbon. The teeth are worn smooth from years of being carried. Somebody kept it close.

### Hidden photo album: The Young Scholar

These are the General's younger years. Unchanged except the third caption, which was a little speech.

- **Top of the class.** He was first in his class and didn't seem to know it.
- **Graduation day.** He held the diploma as if it might fly away.
- **Three friends, one bench.** Three friends on one bench. Years later, two of them would sit on his council.
- **The first posting.** A border outpost, a map, and a lot of patience.

### What changed in World 1, and why

**The squirrels are new.** They set up "Wait till the neighbors hear" after the snake fight, so the line answers something instead of landing in the air. They also make the General's "He'll never make it" echo at home. Pokey's "I'll write" is him brushing it off with a joke, because that's what he does when it stings. Cut them if they don't fit.

**Dot coming back late.** She promised "straight back" in the prologue and turns up two days later, which is her fear coming true in the very first scene. Pokey's "You said you'd be straight back" isn't mean. It's just true, and it hits her where she lives. She hits back with "don't you go anywhere without me," because scolding is how her fear comes out. His "Not very fast" is the first time we hear him use a joke to get them both past it.

**Pokey asks about Meeca first.** In the old script he asked whether Dot got away from the soldiers. After two days of not knowing, the first thing he'd ask is whether Meeca's okay.

**Food is gone.** The old World 1 had Meeca's note telling him to eat, and Pokey eating a leaf because she said so ("That isn't eating, that's apologizing to a leaf"). The story notes liked that joke, but it breaks the rule you set: after the kidnapping, nobody's thinking about eating. Her first note now says what Meeca would really be worried about. She knows he'll blame himself for being slow, so she tells him she saw him run and not to listen to the General.

**The handwriting pays off twice.** The note dropped on the march is messy ("I'm writing this walking"), so when the tower note comes in neat, with margins, the player has seen her real handwriting. Dot trails off when she realizes what the margins mean, and Pokey's "Yeah" says the rest.

**The snake follows your direction.** "What are you doing on my branch?" gets "I'm going to get Meeca back," then disbelief, then the slip ("You're going to take her from him?") that only makes sense once the player knows who the General is. Dot's teapot story is gone (the notes called it out, and it was a joke about Dot in the middle of a threat). In its place she panics about how long the snake is. After the fight: "Beaten. By a sloth." Then "Wait till the neighbors hear," with no apology for the knot. The snake softens on his own afterward: the collar, the clue, and the warning about the crocodile's crown, which sets up World 2.

**Fewer hissing letters.** The old snake doubled every s ("Ssstand ssstill"). It reads as clutter in a subtitle box, so now only a word or two per line carries it.

**British and courtroom words are gone.** "Post," "I want that on the record," and "Wonderful."
