# Gemini prompt: World 4 wide waterfalls

Today the falls are built from the one painted strip of falling water there is: the pour under World 4's `lipPour` rock, about 35 px
wide, made seamless and laid in columns (w4Art.ts drawW4Pour, w4Play.ts drawPouring). That reads as water, but a wide fall (4-3 has
falls 550 to 1310 px wide and up to 940 tall) is still a straight-sided sheet: its box is the hazard's push box, set over the climbing
steps, and nothing painted gives it a ragged lip, side spray or a churned foot. These pieces would.

style and the water colour, and `v2/falls-wide-after-sideways.png` for where it sits.
