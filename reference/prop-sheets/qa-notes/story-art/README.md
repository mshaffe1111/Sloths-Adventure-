# Story art (branch story-art, 2026-10-29)

Matt: "It feels like it was our very first concept arts that we had in there that are still stuck in there." Every story figure and plate
repainted on Hugging Face (tools/art/storyart, jobs log tools/art/storyart/jobs.txt), and the story picture reworked to fill an upright
phone.

- `before/`: the story as it was (stills, upright 390x844 and sideways 844x390, phone pixel ratio 3).
- `after/`: the same pictures now.
- `compare-*.jpg`: before and after side by side, per chapter.
- `contact-*.jpg`: every new figure on dark, named.
- `*-old-new-*.jpg`: each figure's old frame (scaled up) next to its repaint.
- `face-check.jpg`: each character's new story paintings next to the game's own sprite of them.
