# 7-4 "The Way Down": Gemini prompts for Matt (v2, the wooden flume)

The look to aim for is Matt's concept picture, `reference/prop-sheets/gemini-batch-2026-10-29/7-4-slide-concept-gemini.jpg`: a worn
wooden flume diving into a cave under the temple, rope bridges, scaffolds, torches and lanterns, glowing mushrooms, cobwebs, thorns,
signs, eyes in the dark. The game lights everything itself (it is dark underground; only torches, lanterns, fungus and the way out give
light), so every prop is asked for **evenly lit**, with no dark corners and no cast shadows, and the light sources painted bright on
their own. Every sheet: cut-out ready on flat magenta (#FF00FF), each piece on its own with space round it, no captions, no frames.
"Seen from the front" means as the player sees it, looking down the flume.

The concept picture's Pokey has his satchel right (strap from the screen-left shoulder to the bag at screen-right), so he can be used as
a pose reference too.

---

## Sheet 1: the flume (attach the concept picture)

> In the same hand-drawn storybook style as the attached picture, a sprite sheet of pieces of an old worn wooden water flume, the chute a
> little sloth slides down in the picture. Evenly lit, soft flat light, no dark corners, no cast shadows. Eight pieces in two rows of
> four, each on its own with plenty of space around it, on a plain flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, no captions, no frames.
> Row 1: (1) a straight stretch of the flume seen from directly above, its floor planks running from the top of the picture to the
> bottom, worn smooth down the middle, nail heads, a few dark knots, a low side rail along each edge; it must tile seamlessly from top to
> bottom; (2) the inside face of one side rail, seen straight on from the side, a long weathered plank with nails and moss, tiling
> seamlessly left to right; (3) a broken end of the flume seen from the front, the planks snapped and splintered, the dark drop under
> them; (4) the same broken end seen from behind, as you come up to the edge of a gap.
> Row 2: (5) a low barrier of rough planks nailed across the whole width of the flume, seen from the front, about half a sloth tall;
> (6) a heavy wooden beam lying across the flume at head height on two short posts, seen from the front; (7) a pile of fallen crates
> and stones big enough to fill one third of the flume's width, seen from the front; (8) the timber trestle that holds the flume up,
> seen from the front, cross-braced legs going down into the dark.

## Sheet 2: lights (attach the concept picture)

> In the same hand-drawn storybook style as the attached picture, a sprite sheet of light sources for a dark cave, sixteen pieces in four
> rows of four, each on its own with plenty of space around it, on a plain flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, no captions, no frames.
> Paint the flames, the lantern glass and the mushroom caps bright and saturated; everything else evenly lit, no cast shadows, no glow
> painted onto the background.
> Row 1: (1) a torch in an iron bracket for a wall, lit; (2) the same torch, the flame a moment later; (3) a torch on a tall wooden post,
> lit; (4) the same, the flame a moment later.
> Row 2: (5) an old iron lantern hanging from a short rope, lit, warm glass; (6) the same lantern swung a little to the left; (7) the
> same swung a little to the right; (8) a lantern hanging from a wooden beam end.
> Row 3: (9) a cluster of tall glowing green mushrooms; (10) a cluster of small glowing purple mushrooms; (11) a few glowing green
> mushrooms growing on a rock; (12) glowing purple mushrooms growing on a pile of rubble.
> Row 4: (13) a strand of glowworm threads hanging down, tiny lights; (14) two glowing yellow eyes in a dark hole in rock; (15) three
> pairs of small glowing eyes peeking out of roots; (16) a single big pair of glowing green eyes behind thorns.

## Sheet 4: the cave around the flume (attach the concept picture)

> In the same hand-drawn storybook style as the attached picture, four big layered cave pieces for a game where the camera looks down a
> tunnel, each on its own on a plain flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, evenly lit, no dark corners, no captions, no frames, in two rows
> of two. (1) the mouth of the cave seen straight on from the front, a ring of roots and toothy stalactites and stalagmites all round a
> big round opening; the opening itself is magenta (see-through); (2) the left wall of the cave as a tall layer: rock, roots and moss,
> its right edge ragged, everything right of it magenta; (3) the same for the right wall, its left edge ragged; (4) the ceiling of the
> cave as a wide layer: roots and stalactites hanging down, its lower edge ragged, everything below it magenta.

## Sheet 5: Pokey on the flume, from behind (attach the concept picture and `src/assets/hero/hero-standard.webp`)

> In exactly the same style as the attached pictures, the same little brown three-toed sloth, seen from directly behind as he slides down
> a wooden flume, sitting, his hands gripping the two side rails as in the first picture. His brown satchel strap goes over his left
> shoulder (on the left of the picture, since we see his back) and down across his back to the bag, which sits on his right hip (on the
> right of the picture); keep the satchel exactly there in every pose. Bandages on both forearms. Eight poses in two rows of four, the
> same size, each on its own with plenty of space around it, on a plain flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, no flume, no ground, no
> captions, no frames, evenly lit. We see only his back and the back of his head, except where his head turns.
> Row 1: (1) sliding straight, gripping both rails; (2) the same a moment later, bumped up a little; (3) leaning left, both hands on the
> left rail; (4) leaning right, both hands on the right rail.
> Row 2: (5) jumping, let go of the rails, arms and legs spread wide; (6) ducking flat, pressed low, arms over his head; (7) hurt,
> knocked sideways, arms flung up; (8) shooting out at the end, arms up high, happy.

> leaning right, his weight to the right, his right arm low and out, his left arm up for balance, paws empty.
