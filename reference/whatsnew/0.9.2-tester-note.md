# Sloths Rainforest Adventures, version 0.9.2 (tester note, Nov 1 2026)

## Meeca's stage (5-M) is fixed
- Meeca no longer goes invisible. The fix also stops other characters and creatures from vanishing on some phones.
- Meeca has soft moonlight on her, and a glow at her hands when she's carrying something, so she's easy to follow at night.
- No more falling through things or dying unfairly. The plank no longer drops on Meeca in the tea room, and she only climbs where there's room above her.
- The log crossings over the pits are fewer and more varied.
- Hints come sooner. The tea room points you to the teapot, and the knot hint arrives on time.
- The camera keeps up with Meeca and no longer jumps.
- Lots of smaller fixes: the chair, the shy steps in the last room, and the knot that sat behind the screen labels.

## New menus: you can always get back
- Every screen has a Back button in the same top-left spot. The phone's Back button does the same thing.
- Every pop-up closes three ways: the X, tapping outside it, or Back.
- The Pause menu has Resume, Restart, World Map and Start Screen. It asks "Are you sure?" before you'd lose progress.
- The World Map is redone to match the start screen. Use the leaf arrows or the Worlds button to move between worlds.

## New: the Satchel (the bag on the start screen)
- Finds: everything you've collected.
- Trophies: your medals and coats. Tap a medal to see it up close.
- Journal: Pokey's journal, with a page for every stage you've played, written from how you played it, and a review at the end of each world.
- Scenes: every cutscene in story order, from the very beginning. Watch one or tap "Play all". New scenes unlock as you progress.
- Creatures: tap any creature for its story.
- A medal now pops up at the top of the screen the moment you earn it.
