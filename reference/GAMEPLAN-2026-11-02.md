## 2. The scorecard (how we gauge every helper, every job)
Scored by the lead at acceptance and written to reference/HELPERS.md.

| Area | Points | How it's measured |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | 40 | Passed the lead's check first time (20); redos (10 at 0, 5 at 1, 0 at 2+); spot-checked facts that held (10) |
| Initiative ("strives for greatness") | 20 | Found and fixed things beyond the brief, built a check after its own slip, method updates the lead accepted |
| Communication | 15 | Report complete (Method used / Lessons applied / Method update), rough spots named plainly, queue read before reporting |
| Efficiency | 15 | Real hours against the measured estimate; tokens and HF dollars against similar jobs |
| Learning | 10 | 10 if no printed lesson broken; 0 if it broke one already on LESSONS.md |

- **A (85+):** reuse first; gets the follow-on work in its area.
- **B (70-84):** reuse with a tighter brief.
- **C (55-69):** one more job, smaller; a second C means retire.
- **Below 55:** retire.

## 3. Permanent retirement (any one of these)
- Hiding or misreporting (a rough spot not named, a check claimed but not run, a fact stated that it didn't check). This is law zero: immediate.
- The same printed lesson broken twice WHERE THE DEFECT ESCAPED (reached the work, a release, or Matt). A slip the helper catches and reports itself costs scorecard points and obliges the lead to fix the system (LEAD-PLAYBOOK 8d); it is not a retirement (lead's rule, Matt left it to the lead, 2026-11-02, D27).
- Three redos on one job.
- Spending Hugging Face money without Matt's approved list; pushing to main or opening a release PR on its own.
- (Changed 2026-11-02, Matt) nobody is retired or rotated for a long memory: every helper keeps notes (reference/notes/README.md) and carries on after compression, like the lead.

## 4. Onboarding: every helper states the contract before it works
The laws and lessons print automatically at session start. Every brief now opens with:
"Before any work, reply with: (1) your job in one line, (2) the method file you'll follow, (3) the three lessons most likely to bite
this job, (4) what gets you sent back and what gets you retired." The lead reads it. A wrong or vague restatement is corrected
before a single token goes into the job.
Honest note: an AI saying "I understand" isn't proof. The scorecard is the proof. The restatement is cheap, though, and it catches
a misread brief in the first minute instead of after an hour.
