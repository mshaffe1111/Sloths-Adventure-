## What went wrong

  in every save state; the playerText gate.
- **D13: generic icons beside the painted satchel.** The bar moved up for one item, not for the screen. Fix: law 15 extended; the icon
  register gate.
- **D7: the lead acted on memory** ("Worlds 9-10 aren't out") and briefed two helpers on it. Fix: check the fact in code before briefing
  (law 3).
- **D20: clocks an hour off all day** (DST ended). A helper nearly stopped an hour early. Fix: time from the machine in every brief.
- **D18/D19: the lead accepted the journal on the helper's own test.** Then the shared gate tested the wrong builder. Fix: acceptance
  waits on the shared gate, and a gate calls what the screen calls.
- **D21: the same mistake twice** (lastY fixed, lastX not). Fix: the bot self-test, and a spread check in every fix.

## What we will STOP doing
1. **Approving words, art or screens from a list or a happy-path shot.** That is law 2: every state, both orientations.
2. **Briefing on memory.** That is law 3: name the file where the fact was checked.
3. **Trusting a measuring tool we never tested.** That is stage-fix.md step 0: check the ruler first.
4. **Raising the bar for one item only.** That is law 15: everything on the same screen comes up, or is named.
5. **Working out times by hand.** That is LEAD-PLAYBOOK 7: `TZ=America/New_York date`, deadlines in ET and UTC.
6. **Comparing art only to a weak "before".** That is hf-backdrops.md: the absolute floor from floor.py.

## What we will CONTINUE doing
1. Quality check-ins every 30 minutes, with rough work sent back on the spot (PARTNER.md first rule, law 16).
2. The defect log with a root cause and an automatic check for each (CONTINUOUS-IMPROVEMENT.md).
3. Methods: every brief names its method; every report updates it (reference/methods/).
4. Gates in CI with shrink-only lists (gates.md).
5. Asking for proof before keeping a change (stage-fix.md: "the fixed bot still needs it").
6. Show first for big look changes (the icon sheet went to Matt before any wiring).
