# "What's ours": the living list (story keeper; Matt, 11-12 4:10 PM ET: "we haven't finished the whole game... inspired by doesn't mean a one-to-one copy")

The About screen's "What's ours" is a LIVING page. The keeper keeps this running list of things that are ours as they ship, and
proposes an update to "What's ours":
- when a world is finished;
- before launch.
Rule: a highlight goes on About only when it is in the shipped game. Broad wording stays true release to release, so a specific
highlight is added only when it will stay true.

## The approved list today (Matt, 11-12)
> Pokey, Meeca, Dot, and everyone they meet along the way.
> Their story, from the first petal to the last letter, and the journal Pokey keeps.
> Ten rainforest worlds, with art made for this game in its own style.
> Gameplay crafted to tell that story the whole way through, and many ideas of our own along the way.
> Dot, who helps when you're stuck, and never tells.
