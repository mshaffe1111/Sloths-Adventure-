# Sloths story rewrite: Prologue and World 1

Nov 1, 2026 · \@Matt Shaffer

This is the prologue and all of World 1, written in full. Every scene says where it goes in the game, roughly how many pictures it takes and how it plays. Lines in italics are pictures, not words on screen. Everything else is a line of dialogue, kept to about ten words where it can be. Best way to test it is to read it out loud with Jen in voices. If a line sounds like a book, it goes back.

**Updated with the lead\'s notes (Nov 1).** The two long scenes are split, the day count is fixed, the journal no longer mentions the snake twice, and Dot\'s long lines are already broken into two text boxes. Wherever a picture needs art we don\'t have yet, the scene says what plays until it\'s painted. All new art gets painted on Hugging Face.

**When it ships.** Your test link first. It goes live together with Worlds 2 and 3, because the candle, Venom Fang following Pokey, the General\'s humming, the honey and the ledger all start here and need picking up there.

## For a second reader (Gemini, or anyone)

Please read this as a script for a mobile platformer that plays between levels on a phone. The bar is a Pixar film that kids and adults both enjoy. Notes that help most:

-   Any line that sounds like it was written by a machine: too tidy, too clever, too symmetrical, or a little speech that wraps a scene up.

-   Any line two characters could swap. Each voice is described below.

-   Anything a seven year old wouldn\'t follow, or a parent would roll their eyes at.

-   Any scene that runs long for a phone. Between levels, a scene should be under 30 seconds.

Please leave the story events in place. The order of the plot is already decided.

## Prologue · The slowest morning

**Where it goes:** storyIntro.ts, the MORNING chapter. **About 20 pictures, around 75 seconds.** This is the one scene allowed to run long, because it\'s the bond the whole game rests on. Built from existing poses on the World 1 plates.

*Dawn in the Emerald Canopy. Pokey hangs from his branch, eyes closed. Below him, a thick old root crosses the path.*

**POKEY** (eyes still shut)**:** Morning, root.

*Next door, Meeca\'s garden branch: pots, string, a watering can. Meeca kneels in front of a closed bud, whispering to it.*

**MEECA:** Come on. You can do it. No pressure.

**MEECA:** Some pressure.

*A blur. Dot hits the watering can. It rings like a bell.*

**DOT:** I\'m sorry, I\'m so sorry I\'m late, I have letters, important ones\...

**DOT:** \...and I promised myself first thing this morning\...

**POKEY:** It\'s still morning.

**DOT:** Yesterday morning.

**POKEY:** Oh. You\'re really late, then.

**DOT:** There was a headwind over the river, and then a second headwind, a completely different one\...

**DOT:** \...and you know I hate being late, you KNOW I\...

*She stops and looks at him properly.*

**DOT:** Have you eaten? You look thin.

**POKEY:** I had a leaf.

**DOT:** Today?

**POKEY:** It was a big leaf.

**MEECA:** Dot! Pokey! Get over here. LOOK.

*The bud is opening. There\'s light inside it, pink and gold.*

**MEECA:** It\'s never opened for me. Not once. Not since Grandma.

**NARRATOR:** The Heartbloom opens for one keeper at a time. For fifty years, it opened for Meeca\'s grandmother.

**NARRATOR:** While it stays open, the ten realms stay green, and the thorns stay asleep.

**MEECA:** Everybody sit. Sit, sit. I\'ll get tea. Everybody gets tea.

*Pokey, who has not moved, is sitting.*

**POKEY:** How long are we watching it?

**MEECA:** All day, if it takes all day.

**POKEY:** It won\'t grow faster with us staring at it.

**MEECA:** You once sat and watched a snail win a race.

**POKEY:** He trained for it.

**DOT:** You can\'t watch a flower on an empty stomach. Snacks! Don\'t let it open without me.

**MEECA:** It\'s already open!

**DOT** (far away, already gone)**:** THEN DON\'T LET IT OPEN MORE!

*Pokey and Meeca, side by side, the bloom between them. Quiet.*

**POKEY:** Meeca.

**MEECA:** Mm?

**POKEY:** There\'s a thing I\'ve been meaning to say.

**MEECA:** For how long?

**POKEY:** A while.

*She turns all the way round to face him. Full attention. He opens his mouth. It takes a long time.*

**POKEY:** It\'ll keep.

**MEECA:** Everything keeps, with you.

*She bumps his shoulder with hers. Then every bird in the canopy goes quiet at once.*

*Far north, over the Highlands, the clouds go dark. Thunder.*

**POKEY:** Was that thunder? You know how I feel about thunder.

*Both his hands are on the branch.*

**POKEY:** It\'s only weather. It\'s only weather.

*Meeca puts her hand over his. Dot comes back fast with no snacks. Her wing goes to the pouch at her side.*

**DOT:** That\'s not weather. Inside. Meeca, leave the flower. Pokey, let go of the branch.

**POKEY:** I\'m working on it.

*The Heartbloom folds shut, petal over petal. Its light goes out.*

*Soldiers march through the garden in two straight lines, right over the pots. The one in front is bigger than the rest, with one iron claw.*

**GENERAL:** The General does not knock.

**MEECA:** It\'s a garden. There\'s no door.

**GENERAL:** That one. The keeper.

**MEECA** (her host voice, on its own)**:** Hi! Hello. Would anybody like tea? There\'s tea.

*Twenty soldiers look at the General.*

**GENERAL:** The General does not take tea.

**MEECA:** Sorry. You\'re on my marigolds.

**GENERAL:** Come, little bloom.

**MEECA:** Who told you to call me that?

*The General doesn\'t answer. For a moment he doesn\'t move at all.*

**GENERAL:** Take her.

**POKEY:** Excuse me, sir. I think there\'s been a misunderstanding.

**GENERAL:** There has not.

**POKEY:** Are you feeling okay? You seem a little grumpy.

**GENERAL:** The General has flattened forts for less. The General will count to three.

**GENERAL:** One.

**NARRATOR:** Pokey moved faster than he had ever moved in his life.

**GENERAL:** Two.

*He\'s halfway there. His arm is out. So is hers.*

**GENERAL:** Three.

*The soldiers lift her. Their hands miss by the width of a claw.*

**MEECA:** Pokey! Follow the petals! Follow the petals, Pokey!

*Dot shoots past his ear.*

**DOT:** I\'m the fast one! I\'ll follow them, I\'ll see where\...

**DOT:** \...and I\'ll come straight back, STRAIGHT BACK, don\'t you go anywhere without\...

*She\'s gone. Pokey looks at the empty sky where she was. Then he goes anyway.*

## Prologue · The chase

**Where it goes:** storyIntro.ts, the CHASE chapter. **For now, 6 pictures with the lines below. Later, a short playable run.**

**Until the playable run is built** (2 to 3 days of engine work, after the words are approved), the chase plays as pictures: Pokey running, the soldiers pulling away, Meeca\'s petals falling, each line below over its own picture, then the root. Petals keep being taught in stage 1-1, as now.

**When it\'s playable.** One long run along the garden branches. You can\'t fail it and you can\'t win it. The soldiers march without hurrying and pull away whatever you do. Every few seconds Meeca drops a petal over a soldier\'s arm. Picking one up plays the petal sound and adds one to the counter, so the first thing the game teaches you is what the petals mean. The lines pop up as shouts while you run. Nothing stops. At the end, controls lock and Pokey trips on the root.

**MEECA** (far ahead, over a soldier\'s shoulder)**:** Follow the petals!

**A SOLDIER:** Sir. The sloth is following.

**GENERAL** (not turning)**:** Let him.

**GENERAL** (louder, over his shoulder)**:** He\'s too slow. He was always too slow. Nothing slow has ever reached the General.

*The old root from the first picture, across the path. Pokey trips over it.*

**NARRATOR:** He tripped over the root he said good morning to every day. This time he didn\'t say anything to it.

*He looks up. The soldiers are a line along the ridge.*

**NARRATOR:** Then they were only rain.

## Prologue · The vow

**Where it goes:** storyIntro.ts, the VOW chapter. **5 pictures.** Grandma\'s cel exists.

*Pokey alone in the rain, by the shut Heartbloom. Beside it, for a second, an old sloth in a gardening apron, looking right at him.*

**GRANDMA:** Follow the petals, Pokey.

*He blinks the rain out of his eyes. Nobody\'s there.*

**POKEY:** Grandma?

**NARRATOR:** On his wrist, the faded petal Meeca tied there years ago began to warm. Slowly, all by itself, it turned to the north.

*Thunder. Both hands go to the branch. He looks at the petal. Then he lets go of the branch, one claw at a time.*

**POKEY:** Hang on, Meeca. I\'m slow. But I\'m coming.

**Act card (EXPEDITION, unchanged):** Ten realms lay between Pokey and the Highland Fortress. Each one had a guardian, and every guardian answered to the General.

## World 1 · Arrival

**Where it goes:** storyWorlds.ts, WORLD_STORIES\[1\], before stage 1-1. **About 7 pictures, around 30 seconds.** The second half moved to the start of stage 1-2 (next scene).

*Card: Two days later.*

*The edge of the home forest. On an old vine, a squirrel-painted sign: THE SLOTH, with an arrow pointing up. A row of squirrels has brought snacks to watch Pokey leave. He is not very far away.*

*Until the sign is painted: the same card without it.*

**SQUIRREL:** Are you leaving?

**POKEY:** I\'ve left.

*The squirrel looks at how close he still is. Behind it, a lot of smaller squirrels laugh.*

**POKEY:** I\'ll write.

*The first petal, pressed flat to the bark of a kapok, at about the height of someone being marched past. Pokey peels it off with one claw.*

**POKEY:** She pressed it flat. With a soldier on each arm.

*A blur hits a fern. Dot comes out of it upside down, then right side up, in his face.*

**DOT:** Pokey! There you are! Don\'t move. Don\'t you move.

**POKEY:** Historically, not a problem.

**DOT:** Do you know how many pink things there are in this forest?

**DOT:** One was a hibiscus. A HIBISCUS. I went round a whole hill for a hibiscus.

**POKEY:** You said you\'d be straight back.

**DOT:** I know what I said, and I also said don\'t go anywhere without me\...

**DOT:** \...and here you are, going. At your speed. Which barely counts. It\'s the principle.

**POKEY:** Is she okay?

**DOT:** She walked the whole way with her chin up, like she was off to a party.

**DOT:** She thanked them. For the march. Every one of them.

**DOT:** One soldier looked right at me and said, \"Is that a hummingbird?\"

## World 1 · Setting out (start of stage 1-2)

**Where it goes:** new small slot: a scene that plays before a stage starts. The same slot carries the letter before stage 1-6, so it\'s one piece of code used twice. **About 6 pictures, around 30 seconds.**

*Dot scratches a picture in the dirt with a twig: a mountain, a tower on top, and a ring of thorns round the whole thing.*

*Until the dirt drawing is painted: hold on the dark north sky while Dot talks.*

**DOT:** They\'ve got her in a tower. There\'s a net round the mountain, iron thorns, ground to clouds.

**DOT:** One gap. About as wide as a folded letter. Notes come out. Nothing goes in.

**POKEY:** So I can\'t tell her I\'m coming.

**DOT:** She knows.

**DOT:** And they\'re still building it. I heard one say, \"Shut by the first storm.\"

*Pokey looks north. The sky over the Highlands is darker than yesterday. His hand finds a branch.*

*Dot holds out a folded leaf. The note fills the screen.*

> Pokey. If you have this, you found the petal. I put it low so you wouldn\'t have to look up. I saw you run. I didn\'t know you could do that. Don\'t listen to a word he said. And don\'t stand in the rain thinking about it. Follow the petals. Love, M.

*He reads it again, slowly. The part about running.*

**POKEY:** Which way?

**DOT:** North. Everything\'s north.

*On the branch above them, a very long snake in an iron collar is halfway through a yawn. He sees them, snaps his mouth shut and does his best fearsome face.*

## World 1 · Halfway: the tower, night one

**Where it goes:** storyWorlds.ts, WORLD_MID\[1\], after stage 1-5. It replaces the current halfway scene. **About 10 pictures, around 45 seconds.** That\'s the longest stop in World 1, because it introduces the tower. Later tower scenes stay at eight. The letter that used to end this scene now plays before stage 1-6.

*A round stone room, much too big for one sloth. A blanket like a rug. Meeca stands on the chair with both hands on the window bars, looking south. Card: The tower. The first night.*

**PELL** (through the door)**:** Miss? It is Lieutenant Pell. I have your supper. I am going to open the door now.

*The lock turns. Turns back. Turns again.*

**PELL:** I am sorry. It is a new key.

**MEECA:** Come in! Please. Sorry. It\'s your door.

*Pell comes in sideways, a tray in one hand and a big shield in the other, held between them. The shield is one new prop, painted on Hugging Face.*

**MEECA:** Is the shield for me?

**PELL:** Regulations, Miss. In case the prisoner is dangerous.

**MEECA:** I knit.

**PELL:** Yes, Miss. The General said that is how it starts.

*On the tray: a bowl of oatmeal, and a tall candle painted in rings. The bottom ring is black, with a little storm cloud on it.*

**PELL:** The General sends a candle. One ring a night, Miss.

**MEECA:** And when it gets to the black one?

**PELL:** I am not permitted to say.

**MEECA:** Do you know?

*Pell looks down at the shield, as if it might know.*

**PELL:** I am also not permitted to say that.

*The candle is the story clock, so it waits for Worlds 2 and 3. Until then, these two lines replace Pell\'s and Meeca\'s candle lines, and the rest stays the same:*

> **MEECA:** Pell, what does he want me for?
>
> **PELL:** I am not permitted to say.

*She tastes the oatmeal and makes a face. By the time he looks up, it\'s a smile.*

**MEECA:** Oh, it\'s lovely. Tell the cook it\'s lovely. And thank you for the shield.

*The lock: turns, turns back, turns. She\'s alone. Close on the bowl. The honey sits in a little dish on the side, not stirred in.*

*Until the close-up is painted: she says the line over the picture of her at the desk.*

**MEECA:** Honey on the side. Only Grandma ever did it like that.

*Footsteps in the corridor. Big ones. She freezes, spoon up. A shadow stops in the light under the door. It stays there a long time. Then it goes. Eleven steps, fading.*

**MEECA** (at the door, whispering)**:** You could knock.

**MEECA** (louder, nearly brave)**:** It\'s rude not to knock!

**MEECA** (small)**:** Sorry.

*She pulls the pin out of her hair, climbs back on the chair and scrapes at the stone around the bars. Tick. Tick.*

**MEECA:** Okay, Pokey. I\'ve got a pocketful of petals left. Don\'t you dare stop for anything.

*The letter on the desk, in her neatest writing, every word sitting right on its line.*

*Later, with the tower scenes for every world: the scraping becomes a tiny moment you play. Tap to scrape. When footsteps come, stop, or the shadow under the door stays longer.*

## World 1 · Venom Fang

**Where it goes:** storyBosses.ts, BOSS_STORIES\[1\]. A retry already plays only the intro\'s first lines. **Intro about 9 pictures, defeat about 10.** The knot pose exists.

Venom Fang is the guardian who keeps coming back. In World 1 he blocks the way and admits nothing. In later worlds he trails Pokey, then helps him, so players watch one enemy slowly change his mind.

### Intro

*A thick branch across the only way north. Coiled on it, the long snake in the iron collar, eyes half shut. A little wooden sign hangs from a twig: CLOSED. The sign is one small prop, painted on Hugging Face.*

**VENOM FANG:** Closed.

**POKEY:** The whole branch?

**VENOM FANG:** The whole forest. North of here. Orders.

**POKEY:** I\'m going to get Meeca.

**VENOM FANG:** Everybody\'s going somewhere. Name?

**POKEY:** Pokey.

*Venom Fang runs the tip of his tail down a list that isn\'t there.*

**VENOM FANG:** Not on the list.

**POKEY:** There\'s a list?

**VENOM FANG:** There\'s always a list. They said stop anyone following the girl. I pictured a hawk. Soldiers, maybe.

*He looks Pokey up and down. It takes a while.*

**VENOM FANG:** I did not picture this. Take all the time you need, ssloth.

**POKEY:** I usually do.

**Tip (unchanged):** Venom Fang strikes after a red ring flashes. Jump over the lunge, then punch his head.

### Defeat

*Venom Fang, tied in a knot around his own branch.*

**VENOM FANG:** That didn\'t happen.

**POKEY:** You\'re in a knot.

**VENOM FANG:** I meant to be in a knot. It\'s a stretch. For my back.

*He tries to untie himself and gets tighter.*

**VENOM FANG:** Don\'t watch.

**POKEY:** May I go past now?

**VENOM FANG:** \...You asked.

*Pokey goes past, very slowly. It takes a long time to go past a snake that long.*

**VENOM FANG:** This isn\'t over. I know where you\'re going.

**POKEY:** North.

**VENOM FANG:** EVERYBODY knows where you\'re going! You\'ve been going there ALL WEEK!

*Pokey is gone. Venom Fang scratches hard at the collar where the thorns sit. He stops. He looks at the CLOSED sign for a while, then flips it over. OPEN. Then he slides off the branch and goes north too, a long way behind.*

*If the sign isn\'t painted yet: the flip happens off screen, and the last picture is him sliding north.*
