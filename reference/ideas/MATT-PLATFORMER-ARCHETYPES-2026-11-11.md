# 24 stage archetypes from the great 2D platformers, and a 9-stage World 1 pacing (Matt, 11-11; outside research, summarized)

Matt: "this should give us the variety needed." Principle: every stage changes the primary verb or one environmental constraint for that
stage only, on the same physics and art. Inspirations only (we build our own rainforest versions). Engine claims unverified: the
Architect checks fit (CAPABILITY.md, R-165.111); the Designer reconciles the pacing with World 1's existing stages.

Environment and weather: 1 predator shadow (red light / green light to hiding spots); 2 monsoon gusts (tail/head wind on a cycle);
3 flash-flood escape climb (rising water/ants replace the fall penalty); 4 bioluminescent night (pulsing light, glowing breadcrumbs);
5 slippery moss and mudslides (low friction, slide to clear gaps).
Objects: 6 drifting lily-pad raft (auto-scroll); 7 sinking pads; 8 push a giant seed through the stage (crush hazards, step, hold
switches); 9 bounce chain over a deadly floor; 10 rotting boughs that fall 0.75 s after touch, regrow in 3 s.
Movement and gravity: 11 under-branch hand-over-hand (very sloth; R-165.79/R-160.1); 12 pendulum vine swing; 13 leaf parachute and
updrafts; 14 seesaw / counterweight branches.
Structure, camera, goals: 15 the hummingbird rides with Pokey (flutter hover, pecks far switches); 16 inside a hollow kapok (bark
walls, knotholes, gates); 17 wrap-around stage; 18 foreground/background layer swap; 19 totem switch, then race back right-to-left
through a changed stage; 20 sunset silhouette look; 21 baby-sloth rescue caravan (scatter on a hit; R-165.110); 22 sound stealth
(noise wakes sleepers; soft landings and bushes keep it low); 23 wash away thorns to open the way; 24 treetop vine ziplines.

Suggested World 1 pacing: 1-1 basics; 1-2 sinking pads + bounce chain; 1-3 rising flood or updraft climb; 1-4 predator shadow or sound
stealth; 1-5 hummingbird / partner stage; 1-6 giant seed push or totem switch-back; 1-7 cave slide or lily-pad raft; 1-8 wind gusts or
sunset; 1-9 Meeca segment / predator gauntlet with under-branch clinging.
