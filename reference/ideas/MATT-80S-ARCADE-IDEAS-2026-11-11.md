# 30 more stage ideas from early-1980s arcade and console mechanics (Matt, 11-11; outside research he brought in, summarized)

Matt: "Don't read everything as it's designed to fit right now. Look at the potential, not exactly how it's phrased... look at it as a
big picture, like the last 30." Mechanics are inspirations only: we build our own rainforest versions, never a recreation of any game.
Its engine claims (PixiJS, 2400 px trees, "Poki"/"Mika") are unverified; the Architect checks fit in CAPABILITY.md (R-165.111).

## Jungle and vine
1 Two-vine fast climb (single vine slow, pair of vines 2x up; fruit dropped down the column knocks climbers off)
2 Over/under routing: dangerous upper canopy vs faster root tunnel below, joined by vine ladders
3 Tempo mud pits that crust over and open on a clock
4 Snap-jaw stepping stones (caiman/flytrap tops safe on beats 1-3, snap on 4)
5 Rolling boulders/nuts at two bounce heights: jump the low, pass under the high
6 Rising bubbles that lift Pokey (pop early with Jump to dodge a hazard above)
7 Monkey drops fruit down a vertical climb; duck into alcoves as it rolls past
## Climbing and rope
8 Shake the vine from the top to drop climbing predators
9 Ride a rising seed-fluff up a hollow shaft, steering left/right
10 Safe-fall recovery (no lives: a leaf floats him back to the last safe spot) - we already do this in 1-1 (fix A1)
11 Multi-column trunk climb with bark sections that open and close on a clock
12 Cut the tendril above to drop a log/hive on a predator below
13 Mushroom bounce up to an overhead moving current that carries him
## Classic platformer
14 Scratch a hole in a moss walkway; a chasing predator gets stuck and becomes a bridge
15 Step painting: every platform lights up with flowers when walked; finish 75-100%
16 Collecting an item changes the layout elsewhere (a vine drops, a lily-pad bridge rises): route-order puzzle
17 Bump the platform from below to flip a spiky beetle, then push it off
18 Hollow-log drum, 3 uses: stuns all grounded predators on screen
19 River swim with dive-under-for-4-seconds past floating logs
20 Drifting sap-bubbles to bounce on, or to trap a predator in
## Exploration and gadgets
21 Firefly-lit tree tunnel that goes dark when a nest is knocked down
22 Grapple-vine to the underside of platforms (no jump) as a stage mutator
23 Overgrown ruins: timed stone doors, mud currents, colour seed-keys for matching gates
24 Drowsiness meter refilled by a fruit trail; small hits make him drowsier instead of killing
25 Orchids that cycle colours on a clock (speed, high-jump, dizzy)
## Environment and physics
26 Ride a vine elevator up and down, bouncing berries across at predators
27 Up-only waterfall climb: break weak ceilings from below, drifting mist platforms
28 Boulder on a leaf bridge: pull the knot, it wobbles, then drops and crushes predators and opens floor gates
29 Push a rock or bush the "wrong" way to find a hidden gust warp or hover mode
30 A pursuing predator copies Pokey's path; lure it off a ledge by landing on a small pad it cannot use
