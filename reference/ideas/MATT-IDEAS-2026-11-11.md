# Matt's level ideas, 11-11 (sent about 3:18 AM ET; 23 ideas, 19 and 20 pasted twice) and the lead's triage

Matt: "determine what looks good and has potential. Put that to the roadmap. And if anything meets and would help level 1-1, then
maybe we can work it into." Each idea is logged with an R-number, folded into an existing one, or swatted with a reason (LAWS 21).
Two facts the ideas text gets wrong, so packets don't copy them: the hero is Pokey and the second sloth is Meeca (not Poki, Mika);
the game draws on Canvas 2D today (the WebGL move, R-3.1, is planned, not done), and the art pieces vary in size (most kept cuts are
600-2000 px), so "zoom 4x and stay sharp" does not hold.

## For 1-1: none, on purpose
1-1 teaches run, jump, double jump and bounce, and the play window says players fail the basics there (36 lives to raised pits; taps
read as punches). Every first appearance of a mechanic must be taught (STAGE-RULES), so a new mechanic in 1-1 adds a second lesson to a
stage that hasn't landed its first. The best of these start in 1-2 or later worlds.
