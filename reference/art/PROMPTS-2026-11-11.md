# Picture prompts for Matt, 2026-11-11 (Matt: "I'm going to need prompts for the additional stuff")

Matt makes these sheets himself, then Content cuts them. This replaces the 10-30 "no Gemini prompts" line for sheets Matt chooses to
make himself (CLAUDE.md:25 still governs Hugging Face spending). Built from what the three cut batches taught us:
- labels and printed prompt text on a sheet had to be boxed out by hand: ask for NO text;
- most "standable" pieces failed the flat-top test (only 6 of 108 had a dead-flat top): say "dead-flat top" for anything to stand on;
- top-down views (sheet 02 of 11-09) can't be used: side view only;
- pieces under 600 px were rejected: 4 objects per 2000 x 2000 sheet, each filling its quarter;
- soft pink grounds and smears cost a cleanup round: pure flat #FF00FF;
- dirt pads under rocks are fine for grounded pieces, never under anything meant to float.

## The template (paste first, then one sheet's list)
Hand-drawn, vibrant 2D game art matching a lush rainforest platformer: bold dark outlines, painted texture, soft natural colour.
Strict side view at eye level, orthographic: no top-down angle, no isometric tilt, no perspective. Every object level, never rotated.
Exactly 4 separate objects in a 2 x 2 grid, each filling most of its quarter, fully uncropped, with a wide gap of plain background
between them. Background: one perfectly flat solid #FF00FF magenta, no gradient, no texture, no vignette. No shadows of any kind,
no ground plane, no extra pebbles or small clusters. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, captions or writing anywhere.
Anything meant to be stood on has a dead-flat, perfectly horizontal top edge.
