# Boss sheets (new set, 2026-10-21)

Matt is redrawing the bosses and sending the sheets set by set. They are 1024 x 1024, 4 x 4 cells of 256 px on flat grey, kept here byte for byte, named `<boss>-set<N>-sheet<M>.jpg`.

`scripts/boss/cut_boss.py <outdir> <sheets...>` keys the grey out of every cell and writes numbered contact sheets so the cells can be named by eye.

## venom-fang set 1 (7 sheets, 112 cells)
Mostly the coiled S-shaped idle in many small variations (front, back, looking left and right), plus: rearing up with the mouth open (sheet 6 cell 1), open-mouth strikes (sheet 5 cells 2, 6, 10, 11), low lunges (sheet 3 cells 2, 5; sheet 4 cell 14), tail raised behind the body (sheet 4 cells 8-11), flat low glides and a slither wave (sheet 4 cells 5-7, sheet 6 cells 14-15, sheet 7 cell 2), a tight ring coil (sheet 7 cell 5) and a ball (sheet 6 cell 9).
Not in this set: the green venom spit, a hurt recoil, a dizzy stun, a dead pose, a clear tail slam. The in-game Venom Fang atlas (`src/assets/enemies/venom-atlas.webp`) is still the earlier art; the new figures are rounder and brighter, so the fight is not switched over until the pose set is complete.
