## Where Pokey is
He has just been told the worst of it, before the stage starts. Meeca is in a tower on the mountain, inside a net of iron thorns "ground
to clouds", with one gap "about as wide as a folded letter", and "Shut by the first storm." Then her note: "I put it low so you wouldn't
have to look up. I saw you run. I didn't know you could do that... Follow the petals." He reads it twice, "the part about running".
"Which way?" "North. Everything's north." Above them, a long snake in an iron collar snaps out of a yawn and tries its fearsome face.
So he starts 1-2 with a purpose, and a little lift from her words. The sky to the north is darker than yesterday.

## What the player should feel
- Start: purpose. Now he knows where he's going and what's at stake, and the snake gives a small comic jolt.
- Middle: following her. The petals are her voice; every petal is one she chose to drop. The stage is the trail, and it reads as
  "she went this way". The rain is starting.
- The low road under the roots ("There's something down by the roots..."): quieter, closer, a secret. The hidden note "Written walking"
  sits in plain sight by a pink bell.
- End: another petal low on the bark, "where you would not have to look up" (the clue card). Her care for him, not a thrill.

## How it should look (unlike 1-1 and 1-5)
- 1-1 is ground-led, the forest floor rising.
- 1-5 is boughs on kapok trunks in the canopy.
- 1-2 is the trail high and low: up along branches and hanging growth in the rain, and back underneath among the ROOTS, the
  stage's own place, in WORLD 1's OWN pieces (LABELS rule 4, the theme lock): hollow logs, root-gate pieces (the open gate as an arch),
  knots and mossy banks under the overhang. W1 has no painted root arch or root bridge yet: those are art gaps (R-165.42), reported by
  the builder, never stood in by another world's art.

Lead with roots and the underside of the forest, not boughs on trunks. It's the dimmest, closest-feeling World 1 stage so far.
