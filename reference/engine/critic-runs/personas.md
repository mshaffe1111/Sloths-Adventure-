CHEAPER ALTERNATIVES
1. Use the pieces already on disk before building an engine. 28 of World 8's 80 sections and 7 of World 6's 41 are in no shipped roll (R4 §5.6, measured). 1-2's 80/90 px rises are never laid (R4 §5.3). Do step 0 (one shared reach function), the rejection/coverage report (R4 §6 idea 1, about 1 h, estimate) and a cap-of-2 refusal that prints the missing pieces (R1 §4 idea 1). This costs hours, not a 3-8 day build.
2. Cut the judging, not the generation. Approve each layout in grey first (R1 L7, L10: Ori's stamp, Hollow Knight's black tiles), reviewed as a text catalog diff (R4 §6 idea 11, Brogue CE) plus a strip image of where the bots end (R4 §6 idea 6), and judge art once per world kit. Evidence: all 4 of 1-1's send-backs on 11-04 were look or petal issues (STATUS.md:903, :933, :937, :943), and 177 of 324 commits that night were docs or reports.
3. Take hazard blocks from A without the full room library. DESIGNER §2.6 point 2 measured 8 hazard blocks cutting look-alike neighbours from 22% to 9% at 4 rooms a job. Add Leap Day's themed pools per stage (R4 §6 idea 9) and a "same ground, second goal" replay (R4 §6 idea 8). That buys variety per hour at a fraction of 50-70 rooms a world, and B's solver is kept only to propose rooms for coverage holes (DESIGNER §10.0).
4. Fix "easy" directly on what already ships. Fit the bots to the testers' recordings (R4 §6 idea 4), add a stage survival budget (R3 F1) and the limited-agent gate (R2 §5 idea 3; 1-2 s a run), and add risk on the avoided lines (R4 lesson 1, hazardSpots). This is a one-time cost, and it is a dependency A, C and the sketch all need anyway.

SUMMARY
- None of the five specs cuts judging, which is the measured slow part: 177 of 324 commits that night were docs or review, and 1-1 had 4 send-backs in one day, all for looks or petals. So a stage still costs about a day under every engine.
- A's "1.25 h a stage" leaves out its own room library. Spread over a world's stages, the designer's numbers give 3-5 h a stage, about the 3.8 h measured today. The HF budget ($72 total) cannot dress 50-70 rooms a world, so stages still look alike.
- Every spec proves "never unfair" with uncalibrated bots or shared provisional formulas. The gates pass easy, samey stages, and only Matt catches it, after a world has shipped, which costs a full rework loop per world.
