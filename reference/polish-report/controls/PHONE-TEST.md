# Controls phone test for Matt (Build 2, about 10 minutes)

From the polish report's 10-minute test, cut to what Build 2 changed. Use the test link once the lead has merged the branch. Landscape first, then portrait. Write down the counts: they tell us which fixes landed.

1. **Running jumps, 2 minutes (CT-1, CT-11).** On a flat run with a gap, run right with one thumb and flick up and forward, lifting at once, 10 times. Then 10 more keeping the thumb on the glass.
   - *Before:* the lift-off jumps sometimes came up about 40% short.
   - *Now:* both sets should land within about 10 px of each other. None come up short. A flick that curls back toward your palm should not stop or turn him as he takes off.

2. **Trunk and liana by touch, 2 minutes (CT-3, CT-4).** Climb a trunk with relaxed, slightly slanted strokes. Stop halfway, by lifting or easing back, and hold still for a second, a thumb a little to one side.
   - *Before:* he leapt off on some slanted strokes, slid on about 50 to 90 px after you stopped, and hopped off the side.
   - *Now:* slanted strokes only climb. He stops where you stop and stays on.
   - To get off, flick up and over (a quick diagonal). That should still leap, including the swing card's "up and forward".

3. **Trunk and liana with the Pad, 1 minute (CT-2).** Switch to Pad. Climb, let go, push up again. Try down. Try a liana.
   - *Before:* the second push up leapt off, and a liana let go.
   - *Now:* up and down always climb.
   - To leave, use the Jump button, or push the pad well over (45 degrees or more, and up).
   - A thumb resting a little to the side no longer drops him off. A hop off sideways needs a clear sideways push held for a moment.

4. **Two thumbs, 1 minute (CT-7).** Steer with the left thumb. Rest the right thumb on the glass for about half a second, then flick it up.
   - *Before:* a mega punch, and the flick did nothing.
   - *Now:* a rest under about half a second throws no mega, and the flick always jumps. Holding still longer still throws the mega, and the flick after it still jumps.

5. **Small flicks, 1 minute (CT-9).** Standing still, do 10 tiny quick flicks up, about a centimetre. Use each thumb and either side of the screen. Count jumps, punches and nothing.
   - *Before:* short flicks often did nothing or punched.
   - *Now:* they should all jump. A tap still punches.

6. **Jump height, 1 minute (CT-12).** Flick-jump 10 times standing still, then jump right after punching an animal.
   - *Before:* heights wobbled a little, and a jump straight after a hit came out low.
   - *Now:* every flick jumps the same height.

7. **Next to a trunk, 1 minute (CT-10).** Stand right beside a trunk and flick up and *away* from it, or steer away while flicking.
   - *Before:* he grabbed the trunk.
   - *Now:* he jumps. Straight up or toward the trunk still grabs it.

8. **Edges, 1 minute (CT-6).** During a stage, swipe Back from the screen's edge (Android), or use the browser's Back.
   - *Before:* the browser could leave the game.
   - *Now:* the stage pauses and the Pause menu opens. Resume, swipe Back again: it pauses again.

9. **Ledge ends, 1 minute (the lead's 2-6 item).** Walk right to the end of a plank or ledge, slowly, and stop at the very end.
   - *Before:* he could stand with half his body over the air.
   - *Now:* he takes a small step back and teeters, with about a quarter of him past the edge. Running off the end, and running jumps, should feel exactly as before.

Not in this build:
- **CT-5** (the second thumb's straight flick on a vine still lets go) waits for your call.
- **CT-8** step 1 and **CT-20** are another helper's.

# Build 3 (about 8 minutes, after Build 2's steps)

10. **Show touches, 1 minute (CT-14).** In a World 1 stage, Screen mode, steer and jump, punch and hold for a mega.
    - *Before:* nothing on screen; on an iPhone no buzz either.
    - *Now:* a faint ring under the steering thumb with arrows the way he runs, and each move flashes its name at the finger. In World 2 and on they are gone (Settings, Show touches: On keeps them, Off hides them).
11. **Second-thumb taps, 1 minute (CT-15, CT-17).** Run right with the left thumb in portrait so it slides toward the middle; tap with the right thumb close beside it, 10 times. Then 10 quick flicks up with that thumb.
    - *Before:* taps near the steering thumb vanished, and a punch came only as the thumb lifted.
    - *Now:* every tap punches, the moment the thumb lands. The flicks jump and throw no punch (at most one in a hundred might).
12. **Grip, 1 minute (CT-16).** Hold the phone with the palm or a finger resting on a side edge, then steer with the thumb.
    - *Before:* the grip could take the steering, and Pokey would not run.
    - *Now:* the grip is ignored; the thumb steers.
13. **Pad buttons, 2 minutes (CT-17, CT-18, CT-26).** Switch to Pad. Press Punch: it punches on the press; hold it: the mega. Tap just off the edge of each button, and in the gap between Jump and Kick. With the pad pushed up (a jump), press Jump in mid-air.
    - *Before:* Punch waited for the release, near-misses did nothing, and Jump with the pad up gave no second jump.
    - *Now:* near-misses press the nearest button, and Jump with the pad up is the second jump.
14. **Ropes, 1 minute (CT-21).** Shimmy along a rope one way, then flick up and the other way.
    - *Before:* he hopped the way he crept.
    - *Now:* he hops the way you flicked.
15. **Double jumps, 1 minute (CT-24).** Jump, and let the thumb drift down and back up slowly in the air, 10 times.
    - *Before:* the drift often gave a second jump.
    - *Now:* only a quick wiggle or flick does.
16. **Settings, 1 minute (CT-28).** Sensitivity at 135%: small flicks jump more easily; at 75% it takes a bigger flick. Steer with: Right, and steer with the right thumb.
    - *Now:* the right half steers at once and the left half taps, flicks and punches. "Either" comes with CT-8.

# Build 4: Matt's four calls (about 5 minutes)

17. **Straight up on a vine (CT-5).** On a trunk, rest the steering thumb and flick the other thumb straight up, 5 times. Then 5 times leaning well over.
    - *Before:* the straight flicks let go.
    - *Now:* straight flicks climb a little each time; leaning ones leap off that way.
18. **Slanted standing flick (CT-27).** Standing still, nothing steering, flick up and clearly over to one side (45 degrees), 5 times each way. Then 10 ordinary flicks on a small platform.
    - *Now:* the slanted ones carry Pokey that way at a gentle run. Watch the ordinary ones: if a careless flick carries him off a small platform, say so (about 1 standing jump in 10 leans that far by chance).
19. **Pad side and hop (CT-19, CT-13).** Clear site data or use a fresh browser, open the game, switch to Pad.
    - *Now:* the movement pad is under the left thumb and the buttons on the right. Settings can flip it.
    - Tap Jump quickly: a short hop. Hold it: full height, as the lesson says.

# CT-8 step 2: steering from anywhere (about 2 minutes)

20. **One thumb, the "wrong" half.** In Screen mode, put one thumb down on the RIGHT half (the half that is not for steering) and slide left and right, 10 times, upright and sideways.
    - *Before:* the run started a beat later than on the left half.
    - *Now:* it starts as soon as the thumb slides, the same as on the left half.
    - From the same spot, a quick flick up still jumps (no run), a tap still punches, and holding still is still the mega.
21. **Left-handed.** Settings, Touch controls, Steer with: Right. Repeat step 20 with the thumb on the LEFT half. Then set it back.
