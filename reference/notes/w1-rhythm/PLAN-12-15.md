## Facts that shaped it (checked in engine.ts)
- The shrine is the last x: the engine stops Pokey at exitTotemX + 35 (engine.ts ~2253). So a loop-back needs three legs (out,
  back, out again), and the shrine is at the right end.
- A pit fall on Easy already sets him back on the ledge he left, nothing lost (engine.ts softFall, every hand-built stage). On
  Normal, `heartFalls` x-spans cost a heart and set him back (engine.ts heartFall); elsewhere a pit costs a life.
- Checkpoints only take when further RIGHT (engine.ts ~2509: `cp.x > this.checkpoint.x`), so flags on a leftward leg don't move the
  respawn. Every leftward or under-the-loop gap is a heartFall, so a miss there never needs a flag. ASK (engine hook for w8-rope2,
  not blocking): a stage flag to take checkpoints in build order, so the leftward leg's flags count too.
- Lianas grab on touch (physics.ts canGrabSwing). So no liana hangs where a miss from a higher leg falls through.
