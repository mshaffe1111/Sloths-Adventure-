## Prompt 12 (3-1 to 3-3): the grotto pieces

```
Create ONE sheet of game props for a side-scrolling platform game: one landscape image, 3:2 (about 1536 x 1024 px), laid out as a grid of 3 columns x 2 rows (six cells), with one prop centred in each cell.
STYLE: the same soft painted storybook style, brush texture and thin dark outline as the attached Mossy Grotto prop sheet, rich but calm colour, light from the upper left. Every prop is seen straight from the SIDE, as the player sees it in the game: a flat front face, with only a thin sliver of the top surface showing.
BACKGROUND: everything that is not a prop is ONE flat, even, pure magenta (#FF00FF): no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow, no glow or haze bleeding into it, so each prop can be cut out cleanly. Do not use pink, magenta or purple anywhere on the props. Glowing parts glow only inside their own outline.
LAYOUT: each prop sits fully inside its own cell with a clear margin; nothing touches a cell edge or crosses into the next cell. No grid lines, borders, numbers, labels, captions or text of any kind. NO characters and NO animals.
Reading order: row 1 left to right is props 1 to 3, row 2 is props 4 to 6.
THE WORLD: Mossy Grotto: a cave forest inside a vast limestone sinkhole at night, lit by threads of tiny blue-green glowworms: wet grey-green limestone, thick moss, ferns and small glowing mushrooms. Teal, deep green and near-black.
THIS SHEET: the grotto pieces.
PROPS, in reading order:
1. Glowworm threads, LIT: a small knot of root and rock at the top with eight to ten fine silk threads hanging from it, of different lengths, each thread beaded with tiny glowing blue-green drops. About four times as tall as it is wide.
2. The SAME glowworm threads, the same size and shape, but ASLEEP: the beads dull grey-green and barely glowing.
3. A big stalactite hanging down: a long wet limestone cone with moss around its root at the top and one water drop at its tip. About three times as tall as it is wide.
4. A stalagmite group rising from the ground: two or three wet limestone cones of different heights with moss around their feet. The bottom edge is a straight flat line.
5. A cluster of five or six small mushrooms with softly glowing blue-green caps, on a little mound of moss.
6. The grotto mouth: a big arch of mossy limestone, dark inside, with ferns at its foot and two glowworm threads hanging inside the opening. About as wide as it is tall; the bottom edge is a straight flat line.
Return only the single finished image.
```

4. A hollow fallen log, thick with moss, open at both ends and dark inside, lying on its side. About three times as wide as it is tall.
5. One giant pawprint pressed into a strip of wet mud: a big rounded pad and four toe dents, seen from the side at a low angle so it reads as a deep dip in the mud. About four times as wide as it is tall.
6. A flat, mossy boulder with four deep, fresh claw scratches across its face.
