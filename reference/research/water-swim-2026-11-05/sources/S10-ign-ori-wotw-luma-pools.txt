Read: 2026-11-05 (TinyFish). Wesley L, IGN guide, 2020-03-12. Water-relevant excerpts verbatim (guide is a long walkthrough).

"First things first, if you haven't already purchased the ability that allows you to breath underwater from Opher, do so before the
Luma Pools trek."
"Head forward and jump onto the bubbles floating up for a quick launch into the air."
"This will open up every path and release water into a new area below. For now, head back out of the water and save at the newly-above
water spirit well."
"Head east through here and you'll find a spirit tree. Absorb its light to acquire the Swim Dash ability."
"swim dash into the bubble-making creatures so that the bubbles can float to the surface"
"Swim dash out of the water and glide to above the tree."
"Defeat the two enemies in here and water will fill the room. Keep fighting the waves of enemies and eventually the room's water will
return to normal."
"Follow the path below west until you reach three blocks that fall fast underwater. Previously, you would've been crushed by these but
thanks to your new swim dash ability, you can dash your way through to safety."
"Run left and bash off the hanging lantern. [...] Continue to run left until you reach water. Dive in and whenever structures fall on
you, swim dash over them. Swim your way through the water and swim dash out the other end"
