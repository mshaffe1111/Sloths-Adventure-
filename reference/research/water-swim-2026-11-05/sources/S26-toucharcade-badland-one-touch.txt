"It is, like so many App Store games, a one-button physics game: touching the screen causes a troupe of silhouetted gremlins [...] to
fly forward and upward; releasing your finger allows them to float to the ground."
"The one-touch control scheme is ideal for iOS games generally, but Badland's capricious physics engine and controls are fine-tuned in
such a way that no two runs are ever feel quite the same, and similar-seeming inputs don't always produce the intended effects on-screen."
"Badland is never particularly forthcoming in explaining its various mechanics or in telegraphing traps or dangers."
Reading (mine): Badland has no swimming, but its whole control is the swim pattern of 2D Mario / Kirby's Dream Land (press = rise,
release = sink, S09, S19), done with one touch; the review's complaint is unpredictable floaty physics, not the one-touch idea.
