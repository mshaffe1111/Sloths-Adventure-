disassembly of Sonic the Hedgehog (1991), object 0A. Lines kept verbatim from Drown_Countdown (the rest of the file is bubble drawing).

move.w #60-1,drown_numtime(a0) ; reset timer to one second (60 frames)
move.w (v_air).w,d0 ; check seconds of air remaining
cmpi.w #25,d0 ; 25 seconds left?        -> .warningSound
cmpi.w #20,d0 ; 20 seconds left?        -> .warningSound
cmpi.w #15,d0 ; 15 seconds left?        -> .warningSound
cmpi.w #12,d0 ; 12 seconds left?
move.w #bgm_Drowning,d0 ; set countdown music
move.w #sfx_Warning,d0 ; set "ding-ding" warning sound
subq.w #1,(v_air).w ; subtract 1 second from air remaining
; Sonic drowns here
move.b #$81,(f_playerctrl).w ; lock controls and disable object interaction
move.w #2*60,drown_restarttime(a0) ; wait 2 seconds before changing to Sonic_Death state
move.b #6,(v_player+obRoutine).w ; set Sonic to Sonic_Death to deduct life and restart level

Reading (mine): air is counted in whole seconds; the warning dings come at 25, 20 and 15 s left, the drowning music at 12 s left, and at
0 the controls lock and he loses a life 2 s later. With air starting at 30 s (StrategyWiki S02: "18 seconds, with warning bells after 5,
10, and 15 seconds", then "about 12 seconds"), the first warning is 5 s after going under. Same numbers in both sources.
