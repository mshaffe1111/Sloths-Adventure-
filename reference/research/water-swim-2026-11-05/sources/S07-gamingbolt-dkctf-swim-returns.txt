Read: 2026-11-05 (TinyFish). Martin Toney, GamingBolt, 2013-10-17 (pre-release). Water paragraph verbatim.

"A returning gameplay mechanic that was missing from Donkey Kong Country Returns is the ability to swim, but it has been further refined
and improved than its previous iterations. When the player is swimming under water they have the option of attacking the foe with a
spinning attack as a means of defence. As soon as the players character enteres the water, an oxygen gauge will appear on screen that
will depict how much air the Kongs have left, if they wish to survive underwater with resurfacing the player must touch bubbles in order
to gain more O2, similar to the means of replenishing oxygen in Sonic the Hedgehog."
