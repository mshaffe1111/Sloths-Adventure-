Read: 2026-11-05 (TinyFish, main content only). WiKirby. Verbatim excerpts.

"Commonalities include having the characters able to move about freely, using a limited moveset or the Water Gun to attack, and having
scuba gear on while underwater. When holding still, Kirby will slowly drift to the bottom. [...] Swimming characters are not at risk of
drowning (again, with one exception), and can remain underwater indefinitely."
"Kirby's Dream Land: [...] the only action Kirby can perform in the water is his jump, and jumping repeatedly is how he swims."
"Kirby's Adventure: [...] he can still rapidly approach the water's surface by jumping repeatedly."
"Kirby's Dream Land 2 and 3: [...] Kine, being a piscine character, naturally moves much more smoothly."
"Kirby 64: [...] he has largely the same moves underwater as on land, though much slower-paced."
"Kirby Mass Attack is the only game in the series where it is possible for Kirby to drown, as he is given an air meter [...] Air can be
replenished by catching air bubbles, and if it runs out, the Kirbys all perish."
"Kirby's Return to Dream Land revamped the swimming controls, making movement much smoother and quicker [...] It also added the ability
to float: while on the surface of water, Kirby and his friends gain an inner tube and can paddle left or right, or jump out."
"Kirby: Triple Deluxe overhauled the mechanic; instead of bringing him to the surface, the jump button instead makes Kirby perform a hard
stroke in the direction he is facing to pick up speed."
"Kirby and the Forgotten Land: [...] Underwater sections were completely cut from the game due to worries that such sections would be
difficult to control in 3D, which would go against the goal of the developers to create a 3D action game that is easy to play."
(cites Nintendo Dream, June 2022 developer interview)
