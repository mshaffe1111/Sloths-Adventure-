§10 Single-Player Mode, "Air meter":
"This is only displayed when you are in water, and it will decrease little by little while you are submerged. Be careful! If you run
out of air, you'll start taking damage. You can restore the air meter by touching bubbles or getting out of the water."

§11 Multiplayer: "Donkey Kong and his partner have individual health and air meters." / "If the partner is on Donkey Kong's back while
you are in water, then a single air meter will be displayed."

§12 Basic Actions: "Move / Swim ... Depending on how much you tilt [stick], your speed changes."
Underwater Actions: "Speed Swim [button] while swimming"; "Corkscrew ... Spin while swimming to deal damage to enemies in your path.
You can also use this move to destroy barrels and find hidden objects."

§13 Joint Actions, underwater:
"Jet Blast: Press and hold [button] while swimming. The jet blast from Diddy Kong's back gives you a speed boost for a certain period of time."
"Ponytail Propeller: Press and hold [button] while swimming. Dixie Kong can spin her ponytail like a propeller to give you a constant
speed boost. There are also places where you can swim against the flow of water only by using this ability."
"Kong Corkscrew: ... both characters can spin while swimming, damaging any enemies in their path."

§16 Barrels: "Kong Barrel: Throw the barrel to break it open, freeing the partner inside. This also fully restores your health (and your
air meter, when underwater)."

§17 Items (Funky's shop): "Blue Balloons: These save you from running out of air by refilling your air meter. They only work once each
and are automatically activated when you run out of air."
