"The Dolphin Kick Badge in Super Mario Bros. Wonder [...] is an Action Badge that allows players to burst forward in a single direction
while swimming in water; this provides a speed boost while also allowing for much more precise turning movement. The badge's effects can
be activated by pressing [button] or shaking the controller. If a player collides with Brick Blocks while performing a dolphin kick, the
blocks will be broken without slowing the player down. The speed boost from the badge is powerful enough to allow the player to easily
swim against currents without the aid of a shell. The effects emulate the enhanced swimming abilities of Frog Mario and Penguin Mario,
as well as Nabbit and Toadette in the New Super Mario Bros. series."
