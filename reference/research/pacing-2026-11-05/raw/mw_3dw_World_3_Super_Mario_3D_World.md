**World 3** is the third world of *Super Mario 3D World* and *Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury*. It is a snow-themed world surrounded by ice blocks. It lies northeast of World 1, north of World 2, east of World 4 and World 5, and below World 6, World Castle, and World Bowser. This world contains seven standard courses, a Captain Toad's Adventures course, a Sprixie House, a Castle, and a Boss Arena. After Mario has beaten Pom Pom, the Blue Sprixie Princess will create a Clear Pipe to World 3-B, and clearing that level unlocks World 4.
