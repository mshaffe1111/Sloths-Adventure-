**Chargin' Chuck Blockade**, otherwise known as **World 1-A**, is the Enemy Course of World 1 in *Super Mario 3D World* and its Nintendo Switch port *Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury*. The course consists of two Chargin' Chucks in a yellow caged room.
