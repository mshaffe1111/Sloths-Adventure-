**Captain Toad Goes Forth**, otherwise known as **World 1-Captain Toad**, is the first Captain Toad's Adventures course, found in World 1 in *Super Mario 3D World* and its Nintendo Switch port *Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury*. The level is visible as soon as the player starts the game, though accessing the level requires the player to complete Chargin' Chuck Blockade.

## Layout[edit]

The level contains a few hills to climb up, and is populated by Biddybuds. In *Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury*, the fan platform is replaced with a moving platform due to the Nintendo Switch's lack of mic controls.
