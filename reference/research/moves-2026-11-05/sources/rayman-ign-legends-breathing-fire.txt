# Breathing Fire!

Updated: Sep 6, 2013 2:44am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Breathing Fire!** is the boss level of the Teensies in Trouble world. You will face off against a large dragon in this level, and must use projectiles attacks to defeat it while dodging fire. 3 Teensies can also be rescued at various parts of the level.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 100

From the start of the level, jump up the two hoops to find **Teensy #1**.

Continue on and grab the projectile attack, and take out any enemies who try to ambush. Get to the last platform and the Dragon will appear. There are only certain point where the boss can be hit, so spend most of the fight ready to dodge his fireballs at any given moment. Watch the first salvo of fireballs come in from the top. When he moves to the left, don't worry about attacking, and keep focusing on dodging the fireballs. When he moves to the right, jump over the fireballs and get some air time by firing a quick salvo of attacks. A short burst should be enough to move to the fight to the next stage. As the boss destroys the platform, jump high to the right and rescue **Teensy #2** who floats into the screen.

In the next stage there will be a bounce pad below a hoop ring that will dip down when grabbed. When the boss moves to the right again, use the hoop ring as leverage, but don't let it dip too low before firing a salvo of attacks at the boss. As the next stage of the battle commences, jump right again past the hoop and look for **Teensy #3** trapped below the planks. You don't need to get on his level, as doing a ground pound on the level above him will be enough to free him and move on.

In this final stage, you can use the bounce pad to move between platforms, so wait for the boss to start bathing one area before moving to the other. After the Dragon blasts both platforms, avoid his fireballs and hit him with everything you've got to finish him off and end the level.
