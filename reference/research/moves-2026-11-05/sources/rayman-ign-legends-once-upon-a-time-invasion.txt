# Once Upon a Time (Invasion)

Updated: Sep 2, 2013 3:15am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Once Upon a Time (Invasion)** is an alternate timed level of Once Upon a Time. An Invasion level only appears when the player has reached a certain part of the game, and offers a more challenging run that must be completed in under a minute. There are also 3 Teensies that can be rescued, but only if the player reaches them before time runs out. The first Teensy will be lost after 40 seconds, the second after 50 seconds, and the last Teensy after 60 seconds.

Because of the short time limit, there is no checkpoint in the level.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: N/A

A great tip to increase your speed in every Invasion level is to master the use of the dash attack. While running, hold forward and tap attack to gain a temporary speed boost while attacking. When used during straight-aways and clearing enemies or bone walls, the speed boost can help you get to the Teensies in time. Just be careful not to use it before a ledge or jump as you may run off the side while in the attack animation.

This Invasion level is played from right to left, and includes enemies and objects from the Fiesta de los Muertos world. Move as fast as you can and bash and enemies out of your way. Try jumping on top of enemies when possible to bounce further, and avoid attacking when jumping to limit time spent slowed down. When you exit the castle, avoid the falling spiked objects and head to the mushroom to fly back to the foreground where a Luchador is waiting. Outrun and him dodge any spiked obstacles, and use the dash attack when the way is clear to reach the end in time.
