# Enchanted Forest

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 2:51am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Enchanted Forest** is the third level in the Teensies in Trouble World. The level features moving trees and roots that can help or hinder the player

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

Move forward through the level, running along moving tree branches until you come to a small vine hanging below a tree branch. Climb to the top of the tree branch and run up the side to the right to find a hidden area. Note the Eye, Hand, and Circle platforms and jump up them to reach the **Queen Teensy**.

As you move along the branches, the next branch will dip downwards with a vine on the end. Jump left from the vine across the gap and grab **Teensy #1**.

Immediately to the right will be an enemy beating up a poor Teensy. Attack him to free **Teensy #2**.

Moving along, drop down between two stone platforms held by roots that will move up and down, move past the right branch underneath the stone platform and past some roots to find **Teensy #3**.

Pass the moving branches and roots until you reach an area where two giant stone blocks are smashing against each other. Wall jump between them and jump onto the left stone block to find **Teensy #4**.

Just ahead will be another monster harassing **Teensy #5**, defeat the enemy to rescue him.

Below the previous Teensy, **Teensy #6** will be trapped below the moving root.

Continuing right will find another enemy attacking **Teensy #7**.

Look for a secret area below the previous Teensy, and double back under the branches after defeating a large enemy. Simply hop from enemy to enemy to rescue the **King Teensy**.

Hold off on finishing the level, and instead head underwater to locate **Teensy #8** hiding behind some weeds.
