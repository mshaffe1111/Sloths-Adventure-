Chargin' Chuck Blockade, otherwise known as World 1-A, is the Enemy Course of World 1 in Super Mario 3D World and its Nintendo Switch port Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury. The course consists of two Chargin' Chucks in a yellow caged room.

## Green Star

Present after the Chargin' Chucks in the course are defeated.

Big Galoomba Blockade, otherwise known as World 2-A, is the Enemy Battle of World 2 in Super Mario 3D World and its Nintendo Switch port Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury. The course takes place on a small piece of rock surrounded by lava, and the enemies in this course are three Big Galoombas.

## Green Star

Present after the Big Galoombas in the course are defeated.
