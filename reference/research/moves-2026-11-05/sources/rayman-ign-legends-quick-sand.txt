# Quick Sand

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 11:26pm UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Quick Sand** is the fifth level the Teensies in Trouble World. This is the first level that introduces a Dark Teensy Wizard, who has kidnapped one of the level's Teensies and needs to be caught to rescue the last Teensy in the level. The level also features crumbling structures that must be climbed and escaped from.

## Walkthrough

### teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

You'll come across the Dark Teensy Wizard just after the level starts, who will take off in a hurry. Don't worry about keeping him in your sights, as you will lose focus on the level thats crumbling around you.

Nearly every structure you come across will fall over and sink into the sand rapidly. While you'll be wall jumping up buildings to avoid this, the level becomes less about climbing and more about keeping pace on the ground as the structures sink into the ground. When you reach the structure that falls over sideways and into the ground next to a tall structure, wall jump up between them to locate a secret area. In here, quickly break a hole in the barrier on the right and wall jump upwards to the top, then break a hole on the left and continue up outpacing the flames to the **Teensy Queen**.

When you get to the next structure and pull on the chain, the building will sink. The camera won't move up, so the hero will actually be dodging platforms to not get trapped between a wall and the sand. Hug the left as the building sinks to spy **Teensy #1** on the way down.

Quickly use wall jumps to slide on the chains going upwards, then run all the way to the left to find **Teensy #2** on the outside of the structure and grab him.

Back inside the structure, continue wall jumping to get a lead over the sinking building until you spy **Teensy #3** beneath a bone barrier, ground attack the barrier before he sinks into the sands.

Continue right to the next structure and pull on the chain to bring it down. Keep moving upwards to combat the sinking, and break the barrier on the right to leave the structure and find **Teensy #4** before returning inside.

As you pull the next lever, **Teensy #5** will begin to take off, so hurry to catch him before he dissapears from view.

Run right through the sinking building and into yet another structure. This building will contain flaming ghosts, but you won't have Murfy to help. When you jump up to the first ring hoop, jump right to grab **Teensy #6** and jump back to start climbing.

Follow the Lum trails upwards and wall jump to find a longer hallway and move to the right before **Teensy #7** passes you by.

Escape the building and run through the crumbling remains, dodging all the fiery ghosts. Once you have escaped the fiery ghosts, another building will collapse and sink to the left. Double back and jump up to follow the building up to a secret area. Much like the previous parts of this level, you'll have to quickly dodge sinking platforms and avoid spiked walls until the building stops sinking and you can rescue the **Teensy King'**

Continue right to find the Dark Teensy Wizard and foil his plans, resucing **Teensy #8** and ending the level.
