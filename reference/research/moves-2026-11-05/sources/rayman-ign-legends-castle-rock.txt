# Castle Rock

Updated: Sep 6, 2013 3:34am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Castle Rock** is the music level of the Teensies in Trouble World. This level can only be unlocked after beating the boss of the world. Music levels differ from normal levels in that they use a unique musical track that flows in time with the beats a player will need to jump or attack to survive. The level will scroll forward at a certain pace, and any players will automatically adjust speed to the left side of the screen. This also means that players can keep momentum when sliding without worrying about slowing down. Three Teensies can also be rescued in this level.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 300

The level goes at a pretty steady pace, allowing you to easily pick out Teensies well before you get close to them. **Teensy #1** can be found after jumping over the 4th group of dancing enemies after sliding past the chain.

**Teensy #2** can be found after clearing the collapsed tower in mid jump.

Finally, **Teensy #3** can be found near the end during the final 'chorus' on the second platform in mid air.
