"Keep an eye out for large marine life. Some of those guys can get... testy." —Cranky Kong, Donkey Kong Country Returns

The Beach is the second world of Donkey Kong Country Returns and its Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo Switch ports. Like the other areas of Donkey Kong Island, it is infested by the Tiki Tak Tribe, who have taken Donkey Kong's banana hoard. Many of its levels take place around water or on ships. It also features some new mechanics that are not seen in the first world, Jungle, such as bomb-shooting ships. New enemies are also in this world, such as crabs and electric jellyfish. The Beach shares similarities to Aqua World from DK: King of Swing. When asked if they were the same location, senior designer Kynan Pearson approved of the idea.

The boss fought in the Beach area is the Scurvy Crew.

## Appearance

From the world map, the Beach appears to be a bay hosting several small islands on it, each one being a level. The world map shows Cranky's Shop and the boss level appearing on a patch of grass.

The earlier levels take place in a calm, peaceful atmosphere featuring a few clouds. A few patches of grass and palm trees appear in parts of the level. Many of the levels have small rocks sticking out from the water. Contrarily, a few of the later levels have dark clouds, strong waves, and a high tides. One of the levels takes place in mild, cloudy weather while another features numerous ships firing cannonballs directly at the Kongs.

Shipwrecks tend to appear in the level in a level, which can either assist him or impede his progress. Oftentimes Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong can only cross over the water by jumping from pieces of wrecked ships.

The Beach reappears in Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze but does not return as a world. However, it is featured as a representative level, Seashore War.

## Extras

The Beach diorama, unlocked after collecting all Puzzle Pieces in Tumblin' Temple and beating the Scurvy Crew

The following is extra content that can be unlocked in the Beach area. The unlockables can be seen in their respective galleries after selecting the "EXTRAS" option in a file's menu. For a full list of extras in the game, see List of extras in Donkey Kong Country Returns.

### Images

Images are unlocked by collecting all Puzzle Pieces in certain levels. All images that can be unlocked in the Beach area are listed below, along with their titles and the levels wherein they can be unlocked (mentioned in parentheses).

* Mugly (Poppin' Planks)
* Pinchly & Pal (Sloppy Sands)
* Snaggles (Peaceful Pier)
* Ship Attack! (Cannon Cluster)
* Squiddicus (Stormy Shore)
* Squid Shot (Blowhole Bound)
* Undersea Adventures (Tidal Terror)

### Music

A soundtrack selection for this world can be unlocked after beating the Scurvy Crew.

* "Poppin' Planks" – plays in Poppin' Planks and Sloppy Sands. It also plays in Gushin' Geysers, a level from the Cloud world.
* "Aquatic Ambiance Returns" – plays in Blowhole Bound. It is an arrangement of "Aquatic Ambiance" from Donkey Kong Country and shares its name with a different arrangement of the same theme, featured in Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze.
* "Tidal Terror" – plays in Tidal Terror.
* "Sloppy Sands" – plays in Sloppy Sands.
