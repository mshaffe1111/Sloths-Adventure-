# Dungeon Chase

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 11:44pm UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Dungeon Chase** is a short level that is required to save and unlock the Princess known as Elysia. The Level requires 60 Teensies to unlock, and is an optional level beyond the locked gate. It is also a level that relies on Murphy to win.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 300

Much like Dungeon Dash, use Murfy to slide and manipulate platforms to allow the hero to move through the level quickly and safely.

After walljumping up a narrow path after som fiery ghosts, slide a platform on the upper ledge close then slide it back to get near **Teensy #1** by a chain slide.

Continue on and be the on the lookout for several spiked objects tied to a rope. Only the middle on is actually above a pit, and cutting the other ropes will only block your path. After these three obstacles, look near the ceiling for **Teensy #2** posied above a pit. Wait until you are near to cut the rope and rescue him as he falls.

Avoid the flaming ghosts, guillotines and other obstacles until you reach the end where **Teensy** **#3** is waiting to be rescued.
