# Toad Story

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 3:29am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Toad Story** is the second world in Rayman Legends. Unlocked after collecting 12 Teensies, The world features enemies such as toads, ogres, various aggressive plants and one mean armored toad! The world consists of towering beanstalks, swamp ridden marshes, giant castles in the sky and more.

There are two princesses that can rescued in this world. Aurora can be rescued by completing the 600 Feet Under Level once you have collected 35 Teensies. Twilaa can be rescued after completing 6000 Feet Under once the player has collected 115 Teensies.
