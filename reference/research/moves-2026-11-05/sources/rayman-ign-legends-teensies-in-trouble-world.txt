# Teensies in Trouble

Posted: Sep 3, 2013 2:51am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Teensies in Trouble** is the first playable world in Rayman Legends. The world features various enemies such as goblins, ogres, dragons and the like. The players will traverse enchanted forests and marshes, delve into creepy castles and run across crumbling desert wastes.

Upon starting the game for the first time, the game will automatically begin in the first level, Once Upon a Time. After which, levels in **Teensies in Trouble** can be started normally by jumping into the painting.

There are two princesses that can rescued in this world. Barbara can be rescued by completing the Dungeon Dash Level once you have collected 15 Teensies. Elysia can be rescued after completing Dungeon Chase once the player has collected 60 Teensies.
