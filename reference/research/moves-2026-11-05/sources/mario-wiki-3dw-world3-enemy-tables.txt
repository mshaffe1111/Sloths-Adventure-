NOTE: EXCERPT. Kept verbatim: the World 3 level table, and each course's "Name | Count" enemies table (image column dropped). Left out:
Green Star / stamp location tables and "Names in other languages" tables. Magikoopa Blockade's page has no enemies table; the level

=== World 3 level table ===
| Level number | Level name | Description | Unlocks |
| World 3-1 | Snowball Park | A snow/ice level with snowballs, Ice Skates, and a Propeller Box. This level introduces the Super Leaf. | Chain-Link Charge Shifty Boo Mansion |
| World 3-2 | Chain-Link Charge | An auto-scrolling sky level with moving fences. This level requires 30 Green Stars to unlock. | Pretty Plaza Panic |
| World 3-3 | Shifty Boo Mansion | The first Ghost House level that features moving staircases and Boos. | Pretty Plaza Panic Captain Toad Makes a Splash |
| World 3-4 | Pretty Plaza Panic | A bright pink grass level with only 100 seconds to reach the Goal Pole. It contains a Princess Peach switch that yields a stamp when Princess Peach stands on it. | Magikoopa Blockade |
| World 3-A | Magikoopa Blockade | A small fortress arena with three Magikoopas for Mario to defeat. | Pipeline Lagoon |
| World 3-5 | Pipeline Lagoon | An underwater level with Cheep Cheeps and Porcupuffers. This is also the second appearance of the Cannon Box. | Mount Must Dash Switchboard Falls |
| World 3-6 | Mount Must Dash | A Super Mario Kart-styled mountain level with Dash Panels and Spring Platforms. | N/A |
| World 3-7 | Switchboard Falls | An autumn-themed forest level with Switchboards. | The Bullet Bill Express Toad House |
| World 3-Train | The Bullet Bill Express | A boss level that takes place on a Bowser train. This level introduces Pom Pom. | A Banquet with Hisstocrat |
| World 3-B | A Banquet with Hisstocrat | A circus tent-like boss arena featuring Hisstocrat and his minions. | World 4 |
