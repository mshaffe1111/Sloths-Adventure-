Title: Tiki Zing (big)
---
[Stripped: "Names in other languages" table, references.]

Big Tiki Zings are indestructible razor-like enemies in Donkey Kong Country Returns and its enhanced Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo Switch ports. These large Tiki Zings have seven razorblades and a sinister face. They appear in only two levels—the Forest levels Wigglevine Wonders and Blast & Bounce—where they serve as obstacles and move vertically next to Barrel Cannons, trying to harm the Kongs as they are shot out. If Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong make contact with these enemies, they will lose a heart.

Names: Japanese メガティキスパイク Mega Tiki Supaiku, "Mega Tiki Spike; shared with silhouetted variant"
