# Ray and the Beanstalk

Updated: Sep 6, 2013 4:57am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Ray and the Beanstalk** is the first level in the Toad Story World. The level features large swampy areas mixed with gigantic beanstalks rising into the sky, and uses gusts of wind to allow the player to fly through parts of the level.

## Walkthrough

### teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

Enjoy the scenery as the level starts and use the drafts of wind to float upwards. After passing through the first beanstalk, look up in the air for a flying cage holding **Teensy #1**.

After the first beanstalk follow the flowing water right, and head into the swampy waters to the left looking for a secret entrance. Inside, simply bounce on the bulbs to reach the **Teensy Queen**.

Before heading through the next beanstalk, drop down to the swamp and swim under the beanstalk to find **Teensy #2**.

Head through the next beanstalk and float over the water monster to track down **Teensy #3** who is slowly floating away to the right.

In the next area, bounce of the bulb to float towards **Teensy #4** dangling dangerously close to a river monster, free him quickly!

From the last Teensy, fly upwards and to the left to find a branch from the beanstalk. Run left to find a secret area where you can play Soccer Pong to set the **Teensy King** free!

Begin scaling the beanstalks while floating upwards with the gusts of wind. Be on the lookout on the left side of the way up for **Teensy #5** behind a bone barrier.

Immediatly look to the right of the rescued Teensy for a hole in the beanstalk. Pass through to find **Teensy #6** amid some thorns.

Head upwards dodging the paratrooping Toads (Now is a good chance to get the Bad Joke Achievement by breaking a parachute or two). Look around on the right after a thorny vine for **Teensy #7**.

Finally, take out the stilt wearing Toads on the thorny vines to make your way over to **Teensy #8** and head upwards to the exit.
