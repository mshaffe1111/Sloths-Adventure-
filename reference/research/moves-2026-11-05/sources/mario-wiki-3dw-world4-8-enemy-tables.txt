Title: Super Mario 3D World, Worlds 4-8: level tables and each course's Enemies table (Super Mario Wiki)
NOTE: EXCERPT. Kept verbatim: the World 4-8 level tables (Preview column dropped), and each course's "Name | Count" enemies table (image column dropped;
a Notes column is kept only where the page filled it). Left out: Green Star / stamp location tables and "Names in other languages" tables, and the
Captain Toad, Mystery House, Sprixie House and Toad House entries. World 7 is "World Castle" and World 8 is "World Bowser" on the wiki; codes below use
7-x / 8-x. Codes: letters (A, B, C) follow the wiki's own level numbers (so 4-B, 5-B, 6-B, 7-C are Fire Bros. Hideouts and 6-C is a boss course);
"Castle" = castle course; T = Train (5-T, 8-T) or Tank (6-T). Blockades, Fire Bros. Hideouts and Motley Bossblob courses have no enemies table;
the five fetches marked BLOCKED at the end returned no wikitable at all. Clear Pipe Cruise has two enemies tables on its page; both kept, in page order.
