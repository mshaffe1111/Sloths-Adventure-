# Quick Sand (Invasion)

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 11:29pm UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Quick Sand (Invasion)** is an alternate timed level of Quick Sand. An Invasion level only appears when the player has reached a certain part of the game, and offers a more challenging run that must be completed in under a minute. There are also 3 Teensies that can be rescued, but only if the player reaches them before time runs out. The first Teensy will be lost after 40 seconds, the second after 50 seconds, and the last Teensy after 60 seconds.

Because of the short time limit, there is no checkpoint in the level.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: N/A

A great tip to increase your speed in every Invasion level is to master the use of the dash attack. While running, hold forward and tap attack to gain a temporary speed boost while attacking. When used during straight-aways and clearing enemies or bone walls, the speed boost can help you get to the Teensies in time. Just be careful not to use it before a ledge or jump as you may run off the side while in the attack animation.

Enemies from the Fiesta de los Muertos world have taken over, so watch out for spikes snakes and skeletons. Maximize your dash attack to move as quick as possible over long stretches. Try to aim for higher platforms to move above the fray below and keep dashing whenever possible.
