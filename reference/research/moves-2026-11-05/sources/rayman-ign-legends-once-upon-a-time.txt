# Once Upon a Time

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 2:51am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Once Upon a Time** is the first level of Rayman Legends, located in the Teensies in Trouble World. When starting the game for the first time, the player will automatically begin the level at the end of the opening cutscene. Afterwards, the level can be entered normally from the level select screen. Players will be limited to Rayman, Globox, Grand Minimus and Goth Teensy, and will be able to switch outfits after completing the first level.

This is also the first level where Murphy appears as a playable character. Note that the walkthrough is primarily written for versions other than the Wii U/Vita, so players may have to direct the AI towards hidden Teensies when playing on the Wii U or Vita.

## Walkthrough

### teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

As the level begins, don't rush off to right just yet. In true platformer fashion, a secret lies to the left through a hole in the giant tree. A collective "oooooooh" will alert you to a secret area, where the first of 10 Teensies in this level is being held captive. Jump up and attack the cage to break it and rescue **Teensie #1**.

As you continue right, you'll see groups of yellow Lums, usually led by a purple Lum. The purple Lums are worth twice as many points, and if collected first, the very next Lum in line will turn purple as well. Complete a chain of purple Lums to increase your score at the end and earn a Lum Cup! Jump up on the first platform and smash the cage to free **Teensie #2**.

After the platform will be plants sticking out of the ground that can be uprooted with a ground attack. These turnips can be smacked into enemies, or hit while still in the air for twice the amount of Lums. After running over the hill, attack downwards to kill the shielded enemies, and go left under the hill to reveal a secret area where a Teensy is held. Enter the area and collect the flying Lums until **Teensie Queen** appears and is rescued.

Moving onwards a giant spiked enemy will appear. This signals Murphy's introduction into the game. On the Xbox 360, PS3, and PC versions of the game, the player can tap a button to disable the enemy while moving the hero forward. On the Wii U and Vita, the player takes on the role of Murphy directly, and the current hero disspears and is replaced by a computer controlled Sir Globax. The player must use Murphy by tapping specific areas to allow the computer to pass, but has no direct control over the hero.

Continue to the blue mushroom and jump on it to cross the water to the castle, (Players controlling Murphy can bend the slingshot at an angle to have the computer get the Lums in the far left corner.) With Murphy's help, rescue **Teensie #3** from the enemy dangling him over a ledge, and tickle the large enemy before attacking him, then enter the castle.

Once inside, use Murphy to sever ropes holding spiked blocks, and raise and lower platforms. Once you reach an area with a narrow vertical platform next to a lever holding a log in place, move to the left and lower the platform and jump across to defeat an enemy and find **Teensie #4** tied to a stick.

After making your way over the water, have Murphy cut a rope near the top to form two dangling ropes that you can swing across to free **Teensie #5** high up in the air.

Sever the next rope holding the spiked log, but do not sever any more ropes. the Horizontal log has **Teensie #6** Roasting above the fire, so keep it in the air until you can rescue him.

Have Murphy help you across the moving platforms until you reach a rotating platform. Using Murphy, angle the platform up to the top right of the screen where a secret area is located. This area can be similarly rotated, once the hero is safely hanging from a ring, angle the giant platform so the hero can drop safely below, and make your way through to the other side to rescue the **Teensie King**.

Continue on and rotate wall with the chain to the top left to leave this area. In this next area, snap the ropes on each side to drop down, then cut the rope to the left to make it to **Teensie #7**.

Move right to find a trapped Teensie hanging above an enemy. Cut the rope to send the cage crashing down on him, then rescue **Teensie #8**.

Use Murphy to move platforms and fight off ghosts to make it across the large chasm, and tickle the final enemy to make it to the goal.
