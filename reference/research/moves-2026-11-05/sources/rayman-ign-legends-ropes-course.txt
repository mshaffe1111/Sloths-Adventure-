# Ropes Course

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 11:02pm UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Ropes Course** is the fourth level in the Teensies in Trouble World. The level utilizes Murphy to cut, climb, and swing on various ropes.

## Walkthrough

### teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

The level features several enemies on stilts. They can be taken out either by whittling the stilts down to ground level, or by hitting them from above. Stilts also make great wall jump points as well.

Once Murfy joins in the fun, head to the first submerged hill with eye and use Murfy to poke the hill creature, causing it to rise up. Use the chain to slide safely to the other side. When you reach the second submerged hill creature, poke it in the eye and swim under to find **Teensy #1** trapped under the water.

Continue on, using Murfy to slap unwanted enemies away and wall jump up the gap and between two stilt wearing enemies. Look to the right and up among the foliage to spot **Teensy #2** high out of reach in cage. Use Murfy to cut the rope and free him as he drops down.

Slap some more monsters away and tickle the Ogre until you can hit him. Shortly after the Ogre, two goblins will spring down from above on ropes. Take them out and look for a plant on the ground. Uproot it to find **Teensy #3**.

Eliminate the next few roped enemies, and look for a platform with a chain running past a spiked obstacle. Cut it with Murfy, but don't cut **Teensy #4** down behind it or you risk losing him to the abyss. Simply slide on by and rescue him.

After reaching the bottom, look for a spiked log held up, and have Murfy cut the left side. Run up the log and wall jump off the posts to find **Teensy** **#5** high up in the foliage.

Move forward and slide down some more chains until you come to another log hoisted above a creature. cut the left rope again to run up the log and into a hidden area. In this area, begin sliding down a rope until about halfway, then have Murfy cut the rope to catapult the hero over to the next rope. Repeat until you reach the **Teensy Queen**.

Moving along, cut the rope to swing towards the chains, and slide down the chains until you encounter a monster near two ring hoops. Poke the monsters eye and jump left to find a hidden area. Here you will have to move the hoop ring close to jump on it, move all the way right, and then jump off as you move it all the way left to rescue the **Teensy King**.

Moving to  the next monster, poke it in the eye until its safe to drop down to the lower level where **Teensy #6**

Poke the remaining monsters in the eyes, and move on to find some goblins tied to an ogre. Dropping down below to the right will find a goblin tied to **Teensy #7** behind some bushes.

Use the ropes to the right to wall jump up to the top level, where you will find a goblin trying in vain to drag **Teensy #8** away. Rescue him and continue on to finish the level, don't forget to poke the last monster in the eye!
