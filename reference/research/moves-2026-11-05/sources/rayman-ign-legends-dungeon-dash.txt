# Dungeon Dash

Updated: Sep 3, 2013 3:12am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**Dungeon Dash** is a short level that is required to save and unlock the Princess known as Barbara. The Level requires 15 Teensies to unlock, and is an optional level beyond the locked gate. It is also a level that relies on Murphy to win.

## Walkthrough

### Teensies: 3

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 300

Once Murphy is introduced, begin by raising and lowering levers and platforms to allow the hero access through the dungeon. Note the fire in the background that means you won't have all the time in the world to decide or take your time on creating a path. Around 30 seconds into the level, you'll have to lower some platforms over a pit for the hero to cross. Raise the last platform high into the air so that the hero can jump and rescue **Teensy #1**.

Continue raising and lowering levers and platforms and avoid pitfalls. Soon after you'll have to lower a bellows looking platform into the ground to create a path. When on the platform, quickly raise it into the air to give the hero a boost to get **Teensy #2**.

Continue dodging enemies and manipulating platforms until you cross several chains and rescue the **Teensy #3** to complete the level.
