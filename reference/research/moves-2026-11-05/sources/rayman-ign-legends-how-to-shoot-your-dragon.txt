# How to Shoot Your Dragon

Updated: Sep 6, 2013 2:30am UTC

Add IGN on Google

**How to Shoot Your Dragon** is the sixth level in the Teensies in Trouble World. The level can only be unlocked with 30 or more Teensies rescued. This level marks the return of the Dark Teensy Wizard, who when spotted, will summon dragons to aid him to stop you. You will also gain the ability to shoot projectiles to fight against the dragons.

## Walkthrough

### teensies: 10

### Gold Lum Cup Minimum: 600 Lums

Start by avoiding the fire and make your way through the level until Murfy arrives to help you out. Raise the first platform and head over to find a bone barrier with a spiked platform beneath it. Move the platform with Murfy and drop down. Bring the platform back to hang on, then move over to **Teensy #1** and down attack to rescue him, hit the pillow and get back to the platform.

The next bone barrier has no platform below, so move it over first with Murfy before dropping down. Jump left to reveal a hidden area. Using Murfy,  rotate the platforms to grab the ring, jump to the next platform, wall jump to the ring, rotate to jump up to the platform above, then jump to the top ring, rotate the platform on the left to reach the **Queen Teensy**.

Move forward to an area with two platforms divided by a pillar of fire. Use a chain to jump over the fire and lower the platform down, then head left to find **Teensy #2**.

Continue on until there is a gap with a platform. Raise the platform up to find a secret area in the top right. Once again, you'll need to rotate the platform so that the hero can safely get to the other side to rescue the **Teensy King**.

In the very next area, raise the vertical platform up so the hero can walljump up to find **Teensy #3**.

Move onward past the large troll until you slide down two chains seprated by a spiked log. Hop down and look left for **Teensy #4**.

Stay sharp as you slide down the chain, as fire will start emerging to chase you down the hallways. Remember to look before leaping as some platforms need to be moved before you descend further. When you get to the area where the platforms begin to crumble, look for several chains to slide down on. The last one will be a rope, use Murphy to cut it to swing to **Teensy #5**.

Continue sliding down chains and avoiding monsters until you reach an area with a vertical platform, two chains and several flaming ghosts. Raise the platform to wall jump onto the second chain, and slide all the way to the right to find an enemy beating up **Teensy #6**.

Continue along using Murphy to cut any spiked logs out of the way. Once you pass a horizontal spiked log held by two ropes, drop down from the next chain to find **Teensy #7**. at the end of a spiked log - so don't cut it!

Exit the castle's interior to catch up with the Dark Teensy Wizard, who will unleash dragons upon you. If you are on the Wii U, use Murphy to take out any dragons. Otherwise you'll have access to projectiles to do it yourself. Look out for **Teensy #8** past the second dragon and use a projectile to free him from his perch. Continue defeating dragons and avoiding their flames to end the level.
