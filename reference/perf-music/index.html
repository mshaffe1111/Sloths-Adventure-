Differences you may hear (on purpose)

  1. New has the fuller desktop sound (full arrangement, big hall) on every phone. Phones used to get a thinner version.
  2. Danger and a boss's anger no longer speed the music up. A recording played faster would go up in pitch.
  3. A part that fades in with how busy the moment is now rises and falls as a level change, not as an instrument entering.
  4. To fit the old phones' memory, the loops are short (20 to 42 seconds for a stage), the danger, exit and dark layers are mono, and everything but the tune is kept to 12 kHz (as the old orchestra's instruments already were).
