# Sequel ideas (Matt, 2026-10-30: "a cliffhanger for a sequel... go after Pokey, and we make Meeca the playable character")

Nothing here is built. The sequel waits until this game ships. Threads the story already leaves open (reference/STORY-REWRITE.md):

1. **The storm.** The game's last line: "Far off in the east, the last of the storm said something." The storm killed Meeca's grandma, broke
   the General, and is why the Iron Pact exists. Grandma's letter says "It was only weather." A sequel can show it wasn't only weather:
   something in the east takes Pokey, and Meeca (fast, the one who waited in game one) goes after the slow one.
2. **Thornbark.** Ten on the council plus the General; nine said yes to the Iron Pact, Thornbark alone said no, and at the end he
   listens from his hollow instead of coming to tea. Why he refused, and what he knew, is never answered: a guide or a villain.
3. **The collars.** The Iron Pact's thorn collars are never explained. If the General didn't make them, someone did, and may want them back.

**The lead's recommendation (to Matt, 2026-10-30): keep the cliffhanger small.** A paid game should feel complete. One short scene after
the credits: the storm in the east, a broken collar on the riverbank, Meeca picks it up and looks east; the last line becomes
"...the last of the storm said something. This time, it said Pokey's name." One painted card (Gemini prompt for Matt first) and 2-3 lines.
Waiting on Matt's yes.
