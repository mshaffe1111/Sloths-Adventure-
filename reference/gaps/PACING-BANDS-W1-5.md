1. **The climb is not there.** Normal band middles: W1 19.6, W2 45.2, W3 20.6, W4 22.3, W5 16.3 (Easy 20.9, 46, 22.4, 23.9, 26.5). World 3 sits far below World 2, and World 5 below World 4 on Normal, on all 3 seeds. Worlds 1, 3, 4 and 5 are nearly the same band; only World 2 stands out.

Either World 2 is genuinely too hard for its place, or World 3-5 need to rise; Matt's call (STAGE-RULES §1b says never flatten the climb).
3. **Out of band (3-seed means; most hold on every seed, see the list below):** 1-8 and 1-6 (Easy) reach World 2's middle; 3-8 (44) is as hard as a World 4 stage and 3-9 (33) too; 4-4 (46) and 4-7 (48) are World-5-hard (both Normal and Easy), and 4-3, 4-5, 4-6, 4-9 sit above World 5's middle on Normal; 5-1, 5-2, 5-4, 5-5 sit below World 4's middle; 4-8 and 4-1 below World 3's; 2-6 is below World 1's on Easy.
4. **Easy is not gentler than Normal on pacing:** the beats are the same on both (density 0.43-0.55 on both), so Easy has few of its 2.0-2.5 s rests: Easy "bursts" run 26-35 s (aim 6-8 s), up to 120-150 s with no 2 s rest on 1-4, 1-8, 2-6, 3-4, 4-1, 4-7.
5. **Normal is sparse and choppy:** 0.43-0.55 actions a second in every world (aim 0.75-1.15); bursts are 3.5-5 s of 2-3 actions (aim 10-14 s, 8-12 actions). Sparsest: 2-6 and 3-4 (0.24/s), 1-4 (0.29), 4-8 (0.30), 4-7 (0.31).
6. **World 5's walks:** the longest walk with no action is 9-11 s on 5-1, 5-2, 5-4, 5-5 (40-64% of the stage in walks over 2 s; the silk rule halves this in the inspector's dead time, the pacing measure does not).
7. **Tension:** the curve matches Gemini's sawtooth weakly everywhere (mean match 0.07-0.32); most stages are flat at 0.2-0.45 with no climax near the end.
8. **Floors:** only 5-6, 5-7, 5-9 (and 5-3 on one seed) on Easy break the kid's 85% per jump (worst 72%, 5-7). No Normal stage breaks 70%.

Score parts by world (Normal, mean of 3 seeds): lives lost/min W1 0.6, W2 7.1, W3 0.3, W4 1.8, W5 2.0; the noisy bots' hardest jumps are a small part everywhere (they rarely miss); W5's busy part is lowest (25% dead).
