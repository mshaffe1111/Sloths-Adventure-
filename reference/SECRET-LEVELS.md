# Secret levels

Matt's plan for hidden levels. Each one is optional: missing it loses nothing, finding it changes something small later.

## Under the Ruins (World 7) — planned 2026-10-24

**Why it fits the story.** In World 1 Pokey says "So I can't tell her I'm coming": notes get out through the net, nothing gets in.
By the middle of World 6 the net is sealed completely and Dot leaves to find a seam. In World 7 Pokey is alone, and he has never
sent Meeca a word. The net runs "floor to cloud", but nobody fenced the ground under it.

**The way in.** World 7's opening describes a ring of ten stone chairs around a cracked floor where a faint sigil still glows. One
World 7 stage hides a crack like it; dropping through it opens the secret level.

**How it plays.** Dark tunnels under the temple: glowworm threads on the roof, roots to climb, flooded passages, and a small lantern
glow around Pokey. It should feel unlike every other level.

**The story beat.** At the far end the tunnel passes under the net. It is too narrow for Pokey, but he can push a folded note up
through the crack: the first message that reaches Meeca in the whole game.

**The payoff (only if it was found).** Dot reaches her window in World 8 (storyWorlds W8_B), so she learns there that Pokey is coming;
the note is still the first thing in his own words to reach her, a world earlier. So the payoff rests on the words, not the news:
- World 8: Dot mentions she already had something under her pillow, folded small.
- The ending: she quotes his note back to him.
- The note's words are still to write.

**Art.** All new, written as Gemini prompts when World 7 is built (listed in ART-TO-GET.md, item 11): tunnel pieces, glowworm threads,
roots, flooded passages, both cracks, Pokey pushing the note up, Meeca finding it.

**Still to decide when World 7 is built.** The level's art, the note's wording, the exact stage that hides
the crack, and a mock-up of the new screens for Matt to approve before building.
