# R-150.8: the first-timer bot's climbs on 1-4 (fix-petals, fun-validator)

## The defect
1-4 layout 1, x 2229-2346 (and every trunk like it): a trunk stands on a branch, and the next branch is 85 px to the left and 30 px up.
At the trunk's top the engine sets Pokey off into the air. The bot held the way on ("in the air off a ledge: steer the way on"), came
down 7-12 px from the branch's end at a run, and ran off it, back to the trunk's foot. Three times a trunk, then a rescue.
On 1-4, 38 of the 58 rescues (seeds 1-3, kid and adult) were "fell back down from here 3 times"; 20 were the squirrel-lift puzzle rooms.

## The fix (tools/inspect/firsttimer.ts)
In the air, over a ledge at least 150 px wide, within 60 px under his feet, whose end is under 40 px ahead, with the way on not dropping:
he steers back to land on it, then jumps the gap from there, as a person does.
The first cut also fired as he dropped into 1-1's 70 px dips. That took one 1-1 run from 8 to 20 rescues; the width and height limits
came from that.

## Proof
- Fixture (src/game/firstTimerBot.test.ts, copied from 1-4 layout 1, 5045 px lower): the old bot runs off the branch's end on every
  seed, adult and kid, and fails. The new bot never runs off it and crosses on 5 of 5, adult and kid.
- botBaseline, Worlds 1-10, seeds 1-3, kid and adult, the same base (0cae9a91: the lead branch's merge) before and after:

| stage / world | rescues before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 (kid s1-3, adult s1-3) | 7/7/8, 11/12/13 (58) | 2/11/3, 2/0/1 (19) |
| 1-4 by cause | fell back 38, puzzle rooms 20 | fell back 12, puzzle rooms 7 |
| 1-1, 1-2, 1-3, 1-5, 1-6, 1-7, 1-8, 1-9 | 42, 11, 1, 29, 40, 3, 0, 10 | the same, run for run (1-9 one run 2 -> 1 and one 1 -> 2) |
| World 1 | 194 rescues, 35 shrines | 155 rescues, 35 shrines |
| Worlds 1-10 (552 runs) | 2,006 rescues, 331 shrines, 1,873 lives | 1,915 rescues, 344 shrines, 1,867 lives |

- No shipped data moved: 5-9's kid runs are 8, 3, 3, 5, 7, 5, as recorded (KID_RUNS_59 unchanged).

## Named, not fixed
- 1-4 kid seed 2: still 11 rescues and a time-out (from 7). The quality supervisor saw it too.
- World 10: rescues 279 -> 298 while its shrines rose 31 -> 36 (the bot gets further and meets more). 10-4 adult seed 3 went 9 -> 14.
- The 7 puzzle-room rescues on 1-4: the bot does not work the squirrel lift. A bot limit, not this defect.

## Method used
reference/methods/stage-fix.md (find the spot from the bot's stuck list, trace it frame by frame, fix the cause) with the validator method's
D15 rule (a fixture that fails before and passes after, in the real engine, before any real-stage number counts).

## Lessons applied
- D15: the fixture was proven to fail on the old bot (both bots, every seed) before the fix was trusted.
- D23: before and after were measured on the same base (the lead branch merged first, the "before" from a worktree of that commit).
- A side effect is a defect too: the first cut's 1-1 regression (one run 8 -> 20 rescues) was found by the ten-world table, not by the
  1-4 numbers, and fixed before the report.
- A fixture borrows nothing from a real stage by id: under the id '1-4' the stage's puzzle rooms took the bot over in the bare world.

## Method update (for the lead to fold into stage-fix.md and validator.md)
- Copy the failing spot from the real stage into a bare-world fixture first (its platforms and vines, at a fixed offset), then fix
  against the fixture, then run tools/inspect/botBaseline.ts on Worlds 1-10, seeds 1-3, kid and adult, before and after on the same base,
  and read every stage's before/after, not only the target stage's. (bot-fix: about 32 minutes against a 1.5 h estimate.)
- Assert the defect itself in the fixture (here "ran off the branch's end with no jump pressed"), not an outcome a genuine miss can
  also cause (a kid's straight-up flick that falls short is not this defect).
- Give a fixture a stage id with nothing at that spot, or a stage's own rooms and hints leak into the bare world.
