# Blind play-test: 5-M "On the March North" (2026-10-31)

Tester: blind QA helper. Screenshots are in `reference/qa-reports/5-M-blind-2026-10-31/`; this report calls them `shots/NNNN.png`.

**Setup notes (not game bugs, but they cost time):**
- `npm ci` fails because the lockfile is out of sync. I had to run `npm install --legacy-peer-deps`, which leaves an untracked `package-lock.json`; I didn't commit it.
- The **release build** (`npx vite build` then `vite preview` on 4303) doesn't expose `window.__play`, so `qa.cjs start` fails with `TypeError: window.__play is not a function`. The whole session was played on the dev server (`npx vite --port 4303`).
- On the dev server, **starting from a flag fails**: `qa.cjs start 5-M land 2` and `start 5-M port 3` both print `ERROR page.evaluate: TypeError: window.__engine is not a function`. The `land 2` start opened the stage from the very beginning; the `port 3` start printed only the error. I couldn't skip past the spot where I got stuck.

## 1. Verdict

I couldn't finish it. I got about 2 minutes 40 seconds in: past the sleeping camp, past the first pit by way of the high logs, and down to the stump where Flicker asks you to "mark it". There I could not carve the circle mark reliably, and steering right does nothing until the mark is made. The first thing to fix is the first high-log jump at the cliff edge: it threw me back into the pit about 10 times. From the hint text ("Too tall to walk") I couldn't tell that you have to hop onto the small raised ledge next to the cocoon first, then jump again from there. That is almost certainly Matt's "climb that sends me back". The night camp looks lovely and I'd play on if that jump were taught properly.

## 2. What worked

- **The opening camp looks great**: four soldier sloths asleep in green uniforms with backpacks, a tent, a campfire, a lantern on a post, a big blue moon, glowing orchids (`shots/0001-start.png`, `shots/0177.png`). It sets a sneaky night mood straight away. Meeca's thought "All asleep. Soft feet. Go." is a nice, short setup.
- The **"Warnings 0/5"** HUD sign clearly tells you this is a stealth stage.
- **The vine post climbs cleanly**: "steer up" on the vine-wrapped post took Meeca straight up onto the log (`shots/0140.png`). It does what a player expects.
- **Hopping log to log** once you're up is fun and readable. The red thorny plants on the logs didn't hurt me (`shots/0137.png`, `shots/0144.png`).
- **Flicker the firefly** is charming. The lines "You're not from here. Nobody from here walks that loud." and "Not a poke. Draw circles on the bark." have personality. Flicker riding on Meeca's head when she walks off (`shots/0159.png`) is a cute touch.
- Bursts at the start (`shots/0003-burst0.png`, `0007-burst4.png`) and during jumps were smooth: no flicker, no pop-in, no camera jitter.
- No PAGE errors from the game the whole session.

## 3. Issues

### [blocker] Can't get past the "mark it" stump: the circle gesture isn't taught clearly and didn't take reliably
- Where: 5-M, about 2:20 into the stage, upright, game time 139-171 s.
- What happened: Walking right past the logs, Meeca thinks "Out! ...Which way is back?" and Flicker appears ("You're not from here...", then "Mark it. Then you can find your way back."). A glowing ring with a faint "M." sits on a big stump. A tap gave "Not a poke. Draw circles on the bark." Steering right does nothing: Meeca stays beside the fern and won't walk on (`shots/0160.png`, burst `0163-burst1.png`, `0167-burst5.png`).
  - Tries: (1) tap; (2) tap and hold 800 ms on the stump; (3) eight flicks up/right/down/left; (4) left-thumb circle with `steer up/right/down/left`, about 150 ms each, twice round. After (4) the ring vanished, the bark showed "M." and Flicker came and sat on Meeca's head (`shots/0157.png`, `0159.png`), which looked like success. But steering right then put me back at the stump, with the ring and Flicker back on the bark (`shots/0160.png`), and Meeca still wouldn't move right. (5) a smaller, faster left-thumb circle (80 ms per side, three rounds) just walked Meeca around (`shots/0173.png`).
- What I expected: a lesson card saying how to draw the circle (which thumb, where) before it's needed, and a clear "done" moment after which the way on opens.
- How to make it happen again: play from the start to the stump (see the next issue for the log jump), then `do "tap"`, then `do "steer up 150" "steer right 150" "steer down 150" "steer left 150" ×2 "lift"`, then `do "steer right 3500"`.
- Note: the harness may not be able to draw a real circle, so a human on a phone might get this one. Even so, the only teaching is Flicker's line *after* you get it wrong, and nothing on screen showed whether the mark had "taken".
- Screenshots: `shots/0146.png`, `0148.png`, `0150.png`, `0153.png`, `0154.png`, `0156.png`, `0157.png`, `0159.png`, `0160.png`, `0163-burst1.png`, `0167-burst5.png`, `0173.png`.

### [major] The cliff edge after the camp keeps dropping you back into the pit (the "climb that sends you back")
- Where: 5-M, about 45 s in, sideways, just right of the cracked cocoon at the cliff edge.
- What happened: Past the camp is a cliff with a stepped pit below and two high mossy logs above. The hint reads "Too tall to walk. Keep sliding and flick up!" (`shots/0013.png`, `shots/0086.png`). Steering right off the edge drops you into the pit, where the hint changes to "Too far to jump. The high way, then." (`shots/0016.png`). Climbing back out means about six separate step-hops (each `steer left 250 + flick up`), around 8 game seconds every time. Running jumps from the edge, and `flickhold up 600-700`, fell short of the first log every time (`shots/0047.png`, `0056.png`). Once, the burst showed Meeca level with the top of the log, but she dropped through it into the pit anyway (`shots/0068-burst7.png` → `0069.png`; again in `0083-burst5.png`-`0085-burst7.png`). What finally worked, on about the 11th try: Meeca was blocked by a small raised ledge right next to the cocoon (`shots/0100.png`); a short hop onto that ledge (`flick up`, steer right 150; `shots/0114.png`), then `steer right 50` + `flick up` from the ledge, landed on the log (`shots/0133-burst5.png`, `0136.png`, `0137.png`). One go from the ledge also failed and dropped me back in (`shots/0115.png`).
- What I expected: either the step up onto the ledge is taught ("hop up first, then jump"), or the "Too tall to walk" hint shows only at the ledge. As it is, "Keep sliding and flick up!" makes you think you should run and jump off the edge, which drops you in the pit. Nothing warns you that the pit is a long climb back.
- How to make it happen again: `start 5-M land`, `tap`, `steer right 6000`, then `steer right 200` + `flick up` (lands in the pit).
- Screenshots: `shots/0013.png`, `0016.png`, `0047.png`, `0056.png`, `0068-burst7.png`, `0069.png`, `0083-burst5.png`, `0084-burst6.png`, `0085-burst7.png`, `0086.png`, `0100.png`, `0114.png`, `0115.png`, `0126.png`, `0133-burst5.png`, `0136.png`, `0137.png`.
