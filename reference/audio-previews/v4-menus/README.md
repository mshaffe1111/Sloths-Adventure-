# Title and menu music, v4 (2026-10-29, revised)

You asked for start-screen and menu music that is "bold and unique", then said what you meant: "still fits the theme, just stands out. So
it's not repetitive within the game." So this is the game's own family (marimba, flute, harp, strings, hand drums, Pokey's motif), with a
key, a groove and a tune of its own. No world plays in B-flat or in this rolling 12/8 hand-drum groove, and no world's tune is reused.
(The first version was a brassy fanfare at 140 bpm on Pokey's full tune; this replaces it.)

## The files

- **`title.mp3`** (75 s): the start screen. The whole piece is about 55 s, then round again.
  - **0:00** the groove alone: a clave-like wooden bell, hand drums, a rolling plucked bass, in 12/8.
  - **0:05** the hook on the marimba. It opens with Pokey's two-bar motif, then becomes its own tune.
  - **0:16** the warm part: the violins sing over the cellos, and the drums thin out.
  - **0:27** the hook again, with the flute an octave above it and a soft choir.
  - **0:38** a quiet passage: the harp plays the motif over the hand drums.
  - **0:44** the hook to close, then round again.
- **`menu-loop.mp3`** (2 min): the journal, badges and library. The same tune and groove, lighter and slower, cut into chunks the game
  strings together in a new order each time, so it doesn't tire.
- **`map-walk.mp3`** (80 s): the World Map. The menu music with the tune handed to each world's own lead as you move: World 1's flute
  (0:00), World 3's harp (0:20), World 7's cello (0:40), World 9's trombone (1:00).
- **`old-title.mp3`** (40 s): today's title (World 1's tune), for comparison.

The pause menu keeps the stage's own music, quieter and muffled.
