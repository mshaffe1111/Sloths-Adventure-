Worlds 5-10 art to pick from

Made on Hugging Face on 28 October with the game's motion and style models: 238 clips and 120 stills. Nothing here is in the game yet. Every guardian clip starts from one of its own painted poses; attacks wind up, hold (the warning), then strike. Two takes of each move; green outline = my pick, red = broken (kept so you can see why). Tap a section to jump.

Known gaps: the Marsh Reaver will not walk (he stands and sways; skipped for now). The style model will not draw props flat side-on, so those come from your Gemini kit sheets. The title pair (Pokey and Meeca) was done first and handed over separately.
