# Matt's playtest notebook

## 2026-10-26 (World 5, upright, persona session 8)
- Plain `flick up` from a standstill against a waist-high block often does nothing useful. What gets you up: lift your thumb, `flickhold up 400`, wait ~200 ms, `flick up` again. If that fails, back off a step, run in (`steer right 200`), hold-flick, keep steering, second flick.
- A dip with a one-step ledge out of it is the classic trap: steering into the wall does nothing. `flick up-right` from inside the dip is the move that pops you out most often.
- Ground webs (yellow rings) grab you. If Pokey stops dead or throws his arms up, shake (left/right 200 ms, four to six times) before trying to jump; jumps while stuck get eaten.
- Hopping (`flick up-right` then a long steer, repeated) clears webs and cakes better than walking. Cakes slow you; it wears off.
- Silk lines: a hold-flick up into them, wait about half a second, then `flick up-right` to let go. A rhythm of steer 1500 / hold-flick / wait 500 / up-right / steer 1500 carries you through silk bridges cleanly.
- Spiders come from behind as well as ahead. Tap two or three times before moving on when Dot mentions them; a hummed silk means more are coming.
- Hearts drain in ones and twos from things low in the grass that are hard to pick out; when health is low, slow down and punch ahead.
- Collect-type stages (flowers, animals) need you to look around, not just run right: running straight to the end leaves you short and Dot tells you how many are left.
- Budget pictures: you get about 40 per run; spend them on stuck spots, not on running.

## 2026-10-26, later run (World 5 again, upright, session 8)
- Always `lift` before a `flick up-right`. If your thumb is still steering, the hop often gets eaten and you stand at the foot of a one-step ledge for ages. Lift, flick up-right, short steer 300, lift: that pops you up nearly every time.
- `flickhold up` from a standstill only crouches; Pokey doesn't leave the ground. Give it a run-in or do it with your thumb already moving.
- Getting onto a closing orchid is tight: it's shut before a standing hop lands. Run in and hop while moving, not from under it.
- On silk you can end up at the very top of a slanted line. `flick up-right` gets you off; holding up again re-grabs, so don't hold-flick right after letting go.
- If hearts drop and you saw nothing, a spider is behind you. Kick back (`flick left`) and punch every couple of steps; the "!" shows up right at the screen edge, so it's easy to miss.
- Animal and flower stages don't fill up just from running right and punching. The things to collect sit up high, so plan to climb, and spend your pictures there.
- Meeca's stage starts on a "Tap to begin" card, and none of the screen words show it. Tap first. Her thoughts don't come through as words, so spend pictures there early.

## 2026-10-28 (Worlds 1-2 rebuilt, upright, session 8)
- Hiding in a bush: stand inside it, LIFT, then steer down. Steering down while your thumb is still sliding right does nothing; Pokey just walks out the other side. You know it worked when sparkles show and Pokey goes dim.
- Sneak stages: the bird hits you about every 3 seconds of open running. Hop bush to bush; don't do long 3-second slides in the open.
- "Flick up into the trunk" means a short trunk/post right next to you, not the big trees in the background. Plain `flick up` standing next to it, then `steer up` to climb onto the rope above. Run-in hold-flicks overshoot it.
- On a rope you creep: 4 seconds of steering gets you about a screen.
- Waist-high grass steps still stop a plain walk dead. Lift, `flick up-right`, steer 300, lift, every time you see one. A high ledge out of a dip needs the run-in: back off 300, steer in 250, `flickhold up 400`, keep steering, second `flick up`.
- Predators sneak up from behind. `flick left` to kick back, then tap. A step into a narrow gap between stumps traps you; two lifted `flick up-right`s get you out.
- Water and drifting logs: never steer more than a moment on a log; it carries you. Wait on the bank, then lifted `flick up-right` hop to hop, one per second. Falling in costs a whole life, and you can lose all three in seconds.
- Fight-count stages show no "how far along" bar, so you can't tell if you're moving. Spend a picture early to get your bearings.
- Pictures go fast. Ration to about 8 per stage, or the last stages end up played blind.

## 2026-10-28, later (v216: Meeca night stage, 2-2, Easy 1-2/3-3/4-3, upright, session 8)
- As Meeca, plain steering through the ground webs gets you nowhere: she sticks and stands still while the thumb slides. Lifted `flick up-right` hops are the only reliable way to move through a webby stretch. If she freezes, `tap` once, then hop.
- Meeca is slow on foot. Don't read "the picture didn't change" as a wall until you've hopped twice.
- `hold 700` while standing under a dashed ring on a trunk carves her mark. Punching and steering up do nothing there.
- If the trees start to look the same, you're going round in a loop. Mark the first one you see so you know when you're back. Spend a sideways picture (`turn`): it shows far more of the route than upright does.
- Water: walking off a bank only splashes you back for free. Missing a lily pad costs a whole life and sends you right back to the start of the stage. Stop at the bank and look before the pads, every time.
- On a log, hop off within a second. Waiting carries you back out.
- Waterfall pushing you back means stop jumping into it. Look for a tree or vine you already passed, and climb it.
- Two-step rock ledges: a lifted `flick up-right`, wait 200, `flick up-right` again (the double jump) beats any hold-flick run-in.
- Vines and trunks are slow climbs: about 8 seconds of `steer up` to get to the top.
- Pictures: I blew past 40 again in the first stage. Ration about 10 per stage and go by the % bar.

## 2026-10-28, night run (v216, Meeca night stage, upright with a short sideways stretch, session 8)
- On foot she's slower than ever. Two seconds of steering barely moves the screen, so don't count "same picture" as stuck until you've also tried going the other way.
- Tall ledges, about one and a half Meecas high: the plain double hop (lift, `flick up-right`, wait 250, `flick up-right`) gets her level with the lip and then she drops back. Mix it up and keep at it: back off 400, run in 300 and double hop, or `flick up`, steer 150, `flick up`. One of them catches within about five goes. Budget for it.
- The circle gesture (`circle 3 2000`) while standing at a trunk puts a scratch mark on it. Use it on the first tree you see so you can tell when you're back.
- Woods that look the same probably are the same: a bit of steady running and you're at a ledge you already climbed. Spend a sideways picture to check before you burn time.
- On a trunk wrapped in vine, stand right against it, `flick up`, then `steer up` about 6 s to reach the log on top. Don't steer up from a step away; nothing happens.
- If she hunches over with a reddish tint and stops reacting, taps, shakes, holds and circles don't free her. Don't spend pictures waiting on it. Note the time and spot.
- Pictures: about 15 went on the first tall ledge and the loop. Turn sideways once early on to get the lay of the land, then go by the words.

## 2026-10-28, puzzle rooms run (v216, Meeca night stage rooms 2-5, upright with one room sideways, session 8)
- `tap` next to a loose object picks it up; Meeca says the drop or throw gesture once and then never again. Tap sets it down; `flick up` throws it a short way. Read that first line carefully, it doesn't come back.
- The pick-up tap only catches when you're right on top of the thing. A step off and the tap is just a punch. Step in 100-150 ms at a time and tap each step.
- Carving: `circle 3 2000` standing at the left foot of the trunk, right under the dotted letters, is what fills them in. Holds do nothing. The circle drifts her a little, so start close.
- Sideways (`turn`) shows a whole puzzle room in one picture; upright only shows half. Turn first in any room with parts spread out, then turn back.
- Steering gets eaten for the first second or so after a warp or near webs. Wiggle, then lifted `flick up-right` hops.
- Pictures: puzzle rooms eat them fast because you can't tell what changed from the words. Budget 8 per room and turn sideways once instead of taking three upright shots.

## 2026-10-28, v219 replays (Worlds 1-4, upright with one sideways stretch, session 8)
- Tall stump with a spring mushroom beside it: plain `flick up` + `steer up` at the stump face does nothing, and walking onto the mushroom doesn't bounce you. What worked: step back onto the mushroom (`steer left 400`), lift, wait 300, `flickhold up 400`, wait 200, then steer right: Pokey catches the lip. Then `steer up` 500 and steer right to pull over.
- Walking speed upright: about 180 px of screen per 650 ms of steering. Use that to stop at a ladder instead of overshooting.
- Ladders climb slowly: 6 s of `steer up` from the bottom to the top. If you lift halfway he just hangs there, which is fine.
- Narrow wooden platforms: a long steer walks you straight off the end, and you drop all the way to the bottom of the climb. Keep it to short steers on platforms (under 900 ms).
- Upright, the next ladder or post is often off the edge of the screen. One sideways look (`turn`) shows the whole floor. Do it first on any climbing stage.
- Cave gaps over water: a plain walk drops you into a narrow gap and the water takes a whole life. Lifted hops don't clear it reliably either. Stop at every edge in a cave and look before you go on.
- A wall taller than a double jump with Dot saying "This way": the way up is usually back the way you came. Go back up the step you dropped from, then double hop right off it.
- Sneak stages: the bird or eagle hits you every few seconds in the open. Steer down inside a bush or behind a waterfall, wait 1500, then go. Upright you often can't see the next bush in time.
- Pictures: blew past 40 again, about 85 this time. Ladder stages and caves are where they go. Budget 5 per play and spend the first one on a sideways look.
