# Jen's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (session 1, first time playing)

- One flick clears small rocks and stumps. Tall stumps need a second flick in the air (double jump). If I bonk a wall
  and slide down, double jump right away.
- A plain jump carries me barely a body length forward unless I slide in the air. Short walks between jumps, or I run off
  the end of raised logs and drop to the low path.
- Mushrooms: just walk onto them. No flick needed, and they put you up high.
- Ledge grabs: when I'm hanging off an edge, sliding up sometimes pulls me on and sometimes drops me. Double jumping
  onto it is safer.
- Collect-goal stages: the low path is thinner on goodies than the top. Stay high if the counter matters.
- Vines: jumping grabs them easily, even when I meant to jump past one. Letting go is the hard part. A quick flick up
  climbs the vine instead of letting go. A flick forward (toward where I'm going) while swinging that way lets go.
  If I've climbed too high and the swing is tiny, slide down first.
- Drifting logs on water: wait until the log touches the bank before stepping on. Standing on a log is safe, but going
  from one log to the next cost me a life again and again, even across a tiny gap and even just walking.
  Treat every log-to-log move as a big risk and look closely first.
- Tips pop up mid-move and swallow the rest of my gestures. After a tip, expect to redo the last thing I tried.
- Sideways shows more of what's ahead. Upright, I can't see a river until I'm nearly at it.

## 2026-10-25 (session 2)

- Falling-rock warnings: when Dot says wait for the shadow, actually stop. Walking under it while it's still up there cost
  me a heart. Stand still, let it drop, then go.
- Trunks: flick up next to the trunk, then keep the thumb pushed up. Letting go too early just drops me back down.
- Ropes overhead: flick up into it, then slide along. It holds you fine.
- Flying as Dot (the hummingbird):
  - Each flick up is one flap, a small hop (roughly a third of a thumb-width of screen). Four or five flicks to climb
    a real distance. Flicking fast in a row stacks them.
  - Sliding left/right flies, but she slowly sinks while I slide. Long slides end up much lower than I started.
  - Sliding down drops her fast. A short nudge is plenty; a long one put me straight into the bottom thorns.
  - The thing I kept getting wrong: lining up height. Stop, flap or sink until she sits level with the middle of the
    gap, THEN slide through in short pushes. Don't flap and slide in the same breath.
  - Thorny vines hurt on touch and there's very little room. After a hit she blinks and I lose track of where she is
    for a moment; wait for her to show before the next move.
  - The yellow bar is nectar. Flowers refill it. Don't burn it flapping for nothing.
- "Continue from checkpoint" after running out of lives may put me back at the very start, not the last flower.

## 2026-10-26 (session 5, bosses, sideways)

### Bosses

- General: the real opening is a bright gold crosshair, often with "NOW!". Until it shows, hits just say "NOT YET!".
  Get close BEFORE it opens; openings don't last long, and walking in from far away I got there too late.
- Stack hits fast: one jump-punch, then a punch or two more while it's still gold. Two or three hits per opening is normal.
- A journal card can pop up mid-fight and eat the moment. Expect to miss that opening.
- Sky Sovereign (4-10): a long fight with lots of hits. Do what Dot says right away: step off the red mark when she
  dives, crouch or get behind cover for the screech and the feathers, stay on the ground when she rakes up high.
  After she crashes or screams herself hoarse, her head and chest glow. Jump-punch then. I lost hearts by standing
  still out in the open while waiting. Keep near cover between openings, and when the chest lights up, go at once:
  if anything is in the way, I didn't make it in time.
- Venom Fang (1-10): when he rears back, get up off the ground (a double jump to something high works), then run
  straight to his head once it's "DAZED". Punch fast, three or four taps. The little snakes die to one punch.
- Shadow Prowler (3-10): he crouches low with his eyes glowing before a pounce, so that's the moment to jump.
  When the lights go out he glows green and is still easy to see. Being up high didn't save me from a pounce:
  better to jump over him at the right moment than to sit and wait. Double jumping over him can leave you standing
  on his back for a second; just walk off. The glow opens just after a pounce or when he's caught in the light.
