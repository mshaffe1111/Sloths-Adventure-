# Brycen's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (session 1, iPhone 440 wide, two thumbs)
- Taps punch now, they don't jump. My old tap-to-jump habit just throws a punch at the edge. Jump is a flick up with the right thumb.
- A plain flick up clears a small gap if the left thumb keeps sliding forward while you're in the air. For a wide gap, flick up, then flick up again near the top (double jump), and keep steering forward the whole time.
- Flick a little before the edge, not at it. Waiting for the edge got me dropped.
- On a branch that breaks behind you, don't stop to look. Every time I stopped to see what was ahead, it went out from under me.
- Punching snakes: stop, then tap two or three times when they're about a body length away. Tapping while still running in got me bitten.
- Swinging vines: jump into the vine and it grabs you. Slide left and right to swing. Letting go is a flick up while you swing forward. My timing was off both times and I fell. Next time pump a few more swings first, and let go at the front of the swing, not the middle.
- Upright on the phone you can't see far ahead. Where you'd land after a vine or a long jump can be off the screen.
- Falling into a pit took hearts first and put me back at the flag. A life only went once the hearts ran out. Watch the hearts, not just the life count.
- The "Got it!" button on tips doesn't work for about a second after it shows up. Wait a beat before tapping it.
- Baskets: standing in one didn't take me up, whether I walked in, waited or jumped in.

## 2026-10-25 (session 2, iPhone 440 wide, two thumbs, first flying stage)
- Always check the hearts AND the little red marks under the nectar bar. Each scratch on a thorn costs a heart; when hearts run out a life goes, and it can happen with no warning line from Dot.
- Mist at the bottom costs a heart and puts you back at the last flower.
- "Got it!"-style tips aren't the only teaching: the talk box at the bottom changes as you go. Read it, it moves on fast.

### Flying (as Dot)
- Sliding the left thumb left/right flies sideways. If you only slide, you slowly sink. Don't hold a long slide at the start: flap first.
- Flick up = one flap, a big hop. Three in a row and the wings need a rest. Flaps go higher than you think; one flick is usually enough to line up with a hole.
- Quick taps = hover in place. Hovering holds height but does not climb. To get up to a flower's head, flick up first, then tap to stay.
- To drink, be right in the flower's head and tap-tap-tap. Being on the stem below it does nothing. A drained flower droops and refills after a few seconds.
- Nectar (bar bottom-left, also a small arc over Dot's head) goes faster than it looks when you flap a lot. Top up at every flower, don't skip one.
- Iron netting just bonks you back. When the hole is lower than you, stop flapping and let yourself sink before sliding in. When it's higher, one flick, then slide.
- Holes with spiky bundles on the lips: line up the height first, hover with taps, then slide through in short pushes. Flapping while inside the hole hits the top spikes.
- Flying through dandelion puffs pops them and can give a lot of points.
- Upright, Dot's talk box sits at the bottom right over the nectar bar. Watch the arc over Dot's head instead.

## 2026-10-25 (session 3, iPhone 440 wide, two thumbs, retest run)
- Tips like "Got it!" can stay greyed out longer than a beat. If it won't take the tap, keep playing a second (nudge the thumb) and try again.
- Two flicks while the left thumb keeps steering does give the double jump (the journal page for it popped up). In the air he does a little somersault on the second flick.
- Tall steps ("too tall for one jump"): don't run at them full speed. Every time I sprinted in and flicked twice, I bonked the face and slid back down. Start right next to the step, flick, then the second flick at the top of the first jump, and only then push forward.
- Turning the phone sideways shows more of the steps above you. When stuck on a climb upright, turn the phone and look before trying again.
- Climbing a vine trunk: flick up beside it, then keep the thumb pushed up. If he stops halfway, lift the thumb and push up again.
- Water knocking you down off a trunk: keep holding up, it's slow but you get there.
- Big boulder in the way: just walk into it and keep pushing, it rolls.
- When a "Water ahead!" card pops up right as you jump, you lose the jump. Land first, read, then go.
