# Brycen's notebook

## 2026-10-25 (session 1, iPhone 440 wide, two thumbs)
- Taps punch now, they don't jump. My old tap-to-jump habit just throws a punch at the edge. Jump is a flick up with the right thumb.
- A plain flick up clears a small gap if the left thumb keeps sliding forward while you're in the air. For a wide gap, flick up, then flick up again near the top (double jump), and keep steering forward the whole time.
- Flick a little before the edge, not at it. Waiting for the edge got me dropped.
- On a branch that breaks behind you, don't stop to look. Every time I stopped to see what was ahead, it went out from under me.
- Punching snakes: stop, then tap two or three times when they're about a body length away. Tapping while still running in got me bitten.
- Swinging vines: jump into the vine and it grabs you. Slide left and right to swing. Letting go is a flick up while you swing forward. My timing was off both times and I fell. Next time pump a few more swings first, and let go at the front of the swing, not the middle.
- Upright on the phone you can't see far ahead. Where you'd land after a vine or a long jump can be off the screen.
- Falling into a pit took hearts first and put me back at the flag. A life only went once the hearts ran out. Watch the hearts, not just the life count.
- The "Got it!" button on tips doesn't work for about a second after it shows up. Wait a beat before tapping it.
- A quick tap while my other thumb was running once came out as a mega punch.
- Baskets: standing in one didn't take me up, whether I walked in, waited or jumped in.
