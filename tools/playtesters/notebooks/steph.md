# Steph's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (session 1, first real go)
- Tips pop up a lot early on. The "Got it!" button stays greyed out for about a second, so wait a beat before you hit it or the tap does nothing.
- A plain flick up only carries you a short way. If you need height, hold the flick (flickhold), then flick again near the top for the double jump. Even that barely clears a ledge that sits about two sloth-heights up.
- Don't jump from right under a ledge: you bonk your head and drop straight back down. Step out past its edge first, then jump in toward it.
- Side kick flicks can throw you up into the air. With a cat right next to you, a tap punch on the ground is safer. Hold the press (mega punch) for the big cats. It takes a few hits and you want to be facing it first: give a tiny nudge toward it, then punch.
- Stomping works if you drop onto them, but you can't always count it as a kill.
- Bushes: a long slide runs you straight past the bush. Use short slides and stop inside it, then slide down and keep your thumb down until the bird's gone. Look for the faded sloth inside the bush to know you're hidden.
- Rushing with long slides means you run into hits you never see coming. In dark places, go in shorter bursts.
- Sideways in the dark cave you only see a small circle around you. Upright shows more.

## 2026-10-25 (session 2, retests)
- "Got it!" doesn't wake up while the game is frozen. Let the game run a beat (thumb resting, not moving) before hitting it, or it just sits there greyed out.
- My jump is short. Even a held flick plus a second flick only lifts me about one sloth-height and a bit. Anything that looks three sloth-heights up is not a jump; look for a vine, a tree or another way round.
- A running flick (keep the steering thumb sliding, flick up with the other) is great for little steps: it carries forward and plants you on top first go.
- Two quick flicks while still steering didn't get me any higher than one held flick. Holding the first flick is what gives the height.
- Punching: after you land next to something, check which way you're facing. A tiny nudge toward it first, then tap-tap.
- Big walls of falling water just shove you back. No point walking or jumping into them; deal with the top.
- When Dot says stand still under something, really stand still with your thumb off. Moving even a bit seems to cancel it.
- Jumping off a high shelf with a double flick sends you way up and the top bar can hide you for a moment. Keep steering where you want to land.
- Rushing with long slides blows past nests and pickups. In nest stages, go in shorter slides and look up.

## 2026-10-26 (session 12, bosses, upright)

### Bosses
- General: the real opening is a big bright gold crosshair with "NOW!". Punching anything else just says "NOT YET!" or "TOO TOUGH". Openings are short, so stay close enough to reach one in under a second. Don't park far away where you're safe.
- 1-10 (snake): Dot's tip shows up before his first lunge, and he takes a few seconds to start, so read it. When he rears back, get off the ground; climbing beats jumping for me. Drop straight down and tap-tap his head while he's dazed. What got me: I dodged to the far side of him and couldn't run back to his head before he woke up. Dodge so you land on his head's side.
- 3-10 (panther): even in the dark you can see him. He's a faint ghost while he prowls and lights up when he's about to do something. The low crouch means a pounce is coming. Jump early, because a double jump right at the pounce still clipped me. When he stands up and roars, the gold mark goes on his chest: run in and tap fast, it gives a lot of hits. Later the mark can sit on his back end, not his head. Punching his face does nothing, so go to where the gold is right away.
- 4-10 (bird) (see also the 2026-10-26 session 13 notes below): stand by a rock and step off her red mark. When she hits the rock, her head is open: face her with a tiny nudge, then tap-tap. When she lands to bite, a held flick plus a second flick gets you clear. Once she's mad, stay low when she rakes, and hide by a rock or crouch for the screech. After the screech her head is open, but I was too far away and missed it. Be ready to run at her the moment the screech ends.

## 2026-10-26 (session 13, bosses again, upright)

### Bosses
- General: when the gold "NOW!" mark shows, one tiny nudge toward it and then a string of fast taps gives two or three hits per opening. Once it says "NOT YET!", stop tapping and step back: standing next to a boss and mashing after the opening closes is how I got clipped.
- 1-10 (snake): don't climb high up the tree. A short climb, just off the ground, is enough to dodge. Then a sideways nudge drops you right onto his dazed head. From way up the trunk, climbing down takes too long. Running back to his head from the far coils is also too slow, so dodge on the tree side or onto the stump.
- 3-10 (panther): when he goes red and lunges along the floor, a double jump still got clipped. Get well away early instead. When he stands and roars late in the fight, a pounce follows fast, and standing at the edge of the glowing floor patch isn't far enough: get clear of it by a full body length. After his lunge the gold can be on his back end. Stay on the floor for it, because if you end up on a low ledge above him you can't get down in time.
- 4-10 (bird): crouching (thumb held down) works for the feathers, the ledge rake and the screech. After the screech, being one short run away was close enough this time. When she lands to bite, a held flick plus a second flick gets you clear, and then turn round and tap her head while she's down.

## 2026-10-26 (session 14, boss gate retest, upright with a turn sideways)

### Bosses
- General: a tap string of six right after a tiny nudge is the sweet spot; more than that and the extra taps just say "NOT YET!". If the punches whiff, you're about half a body short: one more short nudge, then tap again.
- 1-10 (snake): a short climb, then just wait on the trunk with thumb up. When he's dazed, one short slide drops you on his head. From the very top, holding the thumb down climbs you back down fast enough now. When you hop off up high, steer while you fall so you land where you want.
- 1-10: in the late part the little snakes run along the ground. A drop onto one is a stomp and it's free, but don't walk into the big one's mouth as he sweeps along the floor: that's where my one hit came from.
- 3-10 (panther): the ring on the floor is honest. A held flick plus a second flick, steering away, gets you out of it even from right next to him.
- 3-10: when the gold sits on his back end but his face points at you, don't run or jump at him. Going at his face or over his head both cost me hearts. Wait for him to turn or stop, then go.
- 3-10: when he stands up and roars, don't just run flat out. I took a hit running away. Watch for the ring, then jump out of it.
- 3-10: when he gets "caught in the moon" he's stunned for a good while with the gold on his chest. That's the big opening, so run straight in and tap-tap.

## 2026-10-26 (session 15, boss gate retest, upright with a turn sideways)

### Bosses
- General: the camera slides around a lot in boss fights upright, so where something sits on the screen isn't where it sits in the arena. Don't aim a long slide off an old picture; take a fresh look first.
- General: taps you fire at the end of a string can still land a beat later. Don't panic-tap after "NOW!" goes away, but don't be surprised if the count drops one more.
- General: a run-in, six taps, then a held flick plus a second flick steering away is a good rhythm. It kept me alive even when I couldn't see well.
- 1-10 (snake): red eyes and a puff of breath means the floor lunge is coming. Be off the ground before it, not during it. Running along the floor beside his mouth is how I got bitten twice.
- 1-10: don't hold the thumb up at the top of the tree. You don't stay there; you get flung off and land somewhere you didn't pick. Climb a short way and let go.
- 1-10: touching his coils hurts. When you jump to a hanging ledge near him, steer less: too much and you sail past it into his body.
- 1-10: from the far side of his body, a long run back to his head arrives just as he wakes. Don't count on that hit.
- 1-10: the little snakes: a kick (flick sideways) gets rid of them when they're close; a jump-and-drop onto them doesn't always land.
- 3-10 (panther): he walks right over you sometimes. If the gold crosshair turns up on his side right where you stand, just tap; it counts.
- 3-10: when he stands up tall the gold goes on his chest. One short slide in, then tap. If the count doesn't drop, nudge once more and tap again.

## 2026-10-26 (session 16, World 5 first look, sideways with a short go upright)
- Spiders here take more than one tap, and a tap string often whiffs. A flick kick toward them first, then a string of taps, works better than nudging up close and tapping. Nudging and tapping right beside one is where most of my hits came from.
- When the ground under a spider glows red or orange it's about to rush. Kick it straight away or hop clear. Don't stand there lining up.
- Threads on the floor: start the held flick a short slide before the thread, not right on top of it. Jumping late, I still clipped one and it hummed.
- Long slides eat things you can't see yet. The sleepy cakes slow you right down. Slide left-right-left-right quickly to shake it off, then give it a beat before running again.
- Tall steps: back off a little, short slide in, held flick, then wait a moment longer than feels right before the second flick, and keep steering. If Dot pipes up about flicking twice, the second flick was too early.
- Hearts float about head-high. Running under them doesn't grab them; a little hop does.
- Sideways you see a good way ahead. Upright you see only two or three body-lengths ahead, so go in short slides upright.
- Turning the phone mid-stage is safe. Nothing resets.
- Down to one heart, don't keep pushing in long slides. Both lives I lost came from one more hit at one heart, not from falls.
