# Steph's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (session 1, first real go)
- Tips pop up a lot early on. The "Got it!" button stays greyed out for about a second, so wait a beat before you hit it or the tap does nothing.
- A plain flick up only carries you a short way. If you need height, hold the flick (flickhold), then flick again near the top for the double jump. Even that barely clears a ledge that sits about two sloth-heights up.
- Don't jump from right under a ledge: you bonk your head and drop straight back down. Step out past its edge first, then jump in toward it.
- Side kick flicks can throw you up into the air. With a cat right next to you, a tap punch on the ground is safer. Hold the press (mega punch) for the big cats. It takes a few hits and you want to be facing it first: give a tiny nudge toward it, then punch.
- Stomping works if you drop onto them, but you can't always count it as a kill.
- Bushes: a long slide runs you straight past the bush. Use short slides and stop inside it, then slide down and keep your thumb down until the bird's gone. Look for the faded sloth inside the bush to know you're hidden.
- Rushing with long slides means you run into hits you never see coming. In dark places, go in shorter bursts.
- Sideways in the dark cave you only see a small circle around you. Upright shows more.

## 2026-10-25 (session 2, retests)
- "Got it!" doesn't wake up while the game is frozen. Let the game run a beat (thumb resting, not moving) before hitting it, or it just sits there greyed out.
- My jump is short. Even a held flick plus a second flick only lifts me about one sloth-height and a bit. Anything that looks three sloth-heights up is not a jump; look for a vine, a tree or another way round.
- A running flick (keep the steering thumb sliding, flick up with the other) is great for little steps: it carries forward and plants you on top first go.
- Two quick flicks while still steering didn't get me any higher than one held flick. Holding the first flick is what gives the height.
- Punching: after you land next to something, check which way you're facing. A tiny nudge toward it first, then tap-tap.
- Big walls of falling water just shove you back. No point walking or jumping into them; deal with the top.
- When Dot says stand still under something, really stand still with your thumb off. Moving even a bit seems to cancel it.
- Jumping off a high shelf with a double flick sends you way up and the top bar can hide you for a moment. Keep steering where you want to land.
- Rushing with long slides blows past nests and pickups. In nest stages, go in shorter slides and look up.
