# Sally's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (first real session, small phone upright, one finger)
- Slow slides are fine for walking, but a long slide keeps her walking. On a high plank, slide in short bits
  (a third of a second) and stop to look. Twice I walked straight off the end of a plank.
- Falling off a high plank doesn't cost a life, but it drops you all the way to the bottom and you redo every climb.
  Up high, go slower, not faster.
- Plain flick up = a small hop. Flick-and-hold = a bit higher. Neither gets over the tall stones on its own.
- Trunks and ladders: stand right next to them (touching), flick up to grab, then keep the finger pushed up.
  If you're even a small step away, the flick is just a hop and you don't grab on. Climbing is slow: hold up for
  4 or 5 seconds, then check.
- At the top of a ladder she may hang there. Push up a bit more, then a small step sideways.
- Ropes: walk right up to the post, flick up, wait, then slide along. At the end slide down to let go.
- Tapping punches. Hopping past things sometimes gets you further than walking into them.
- The grey "Got it!" button can't be pressed for a second after a tip opens. Wait a beat.
- Sideways the boxes at the top cover what's above you. Upright is easier to see on this phone.

## 2026-10-25 (second session, small phone upright, one finger)
- Tip cards: the "Got it!" button stays grey until the game has run a moment. Let a second of play pass, then press it.
- Leaf glide: flick up, then slide your finger forward and keep it down the whole way across. It carries a gap fine,
  but I sail right over the flowers in the air. Don't chase them; landing safe matters more.
- When the ground steps up a bit in front of you and walking stops, a plain flick up while sliding forward gets you up.
- Trunks: standing touching it, flick up then push up for 4 to 5 seconds, then 4 more. At the top he stands on the branch.
- Wind: when the screen fills with streaks and leaves, walking into it gets you nowhere, or pushes you back. Lift your
  finger and wait. A flick-flick (double jump) while sliding forward sometimes gains a little ground in it.
- Hiding: a hiding waterfall is bright blue and in front; the grey ones far back don't count. Walk inside it, let go,
  and wait 5 or 6 seconds. Bushes: walk right into the middle, then slide down.
- I lose hearts while hiding-and-walking without seeing why. Check the hearts at top left after every few steps,
  and when they drop, stop at the next cover and wait longer before moving.
- A lookout animal: tap a few times near it before walking past. I never saw the fruit, so I'm not sure it worked.
- Sideways shows more of what's ahead on the ground (good for spotting cover), but the top bar hides the sky.

## 2026-10-26 (third session, small phone upright, one finger, new top bar and Dot the bird switched on)
- Double jump: flick up, wait about a quarter second, flick up again, THEN slide forward. Two flicks back to back
  too fast only gives one hop. Waiting a third of a second for the second flick also works.
- A plain hop only clears knee-high steps. Anything as tall as Pokey needs the double jump.
- When a ledge hangs out over you, jumping from right under it bumps your head and you drop. Take a small step back
  first, or jump straight up and only slide forward after the second flick.
- Up-right flick (up and over) is a good one-finger way to hop forward onto low rocks and stumps.
- Snakes: walk until you're nearly touching, let go, then tap three times. Tapping from too far just punches air,
  and the snake bites while you're doing it. Two taps wasn't always enough.
- Hearts can drop while you walk along flat ground and you won't see why. Look at the vine of hearts top left every
  few slides, and slow down when the ground is full of plants.
- Dot the bird: when she talks, stop and read. Her tips stay up until you do the thing. If she says "This way, try
  over here", go where her arrow is, not where you were trying.
- The big counter at the top tells you how many you have out of how many. Keep an eye on it after every few steps.
- Ropes: jumping at a rope from the low ground didn't catch it for me. Try from the ledge right next to the post.
- In the Menu you can make Dot quiet. Then she stops teaching, so only do that once you know the moves.
- Sideways the screen is short: things above you (snakes on logs) are easier to see, but it's harder to see the ground.

## 2026-10-26 (World 5, first go, new look on)
- A short flick up only gets Pokey onto a knee-high block. For anything taller, flick up, wait a quarter second, then flick up again in the air, and only then slide toward the ledge. Sliding sideways during the first jump tends to bump the edge and drop back down.
- A held flick goes higher but not further; for a wide gap do the held flick, then a second flick in the air while sliding.
- If you slide into a step and stop moving, you're just pushed against it. Lift your thumb and flick up; wiggling left and right does nothing.
- Spiders that sleep are fine to pass if you hop over the thin threads. If one wakes, stand still, face it and tap two or three times as it lands next to you. Tapping too early, while it's still far off, misses.
- Spiders often come from behind after you hop over them. After any landing, turn round with a tiny slide and tap once or twice before going on.
- Don't stand still in World 5. After a few seconds silk wraps your feet. One flick up gets you out.
- Cakes put Pokey to sleep for a bit (little z over his head). Jump over anything sitting on the ground that looks like food.
- Dot's speech box sits low on a small phone and can cover Pokey. Wait it out before a tricky jump.
- "Skip tips" is safe to press: Pokey doesn't jump or punch when you press it.
