# Sally's notebook

> Corrected 2026-10-25 by the QA lead: the test rig lifted the wrong thumb when two were down (now fixed). Lessons that came from that bug were removed: a flick while steering works, the double jump works with a steering thumb down, and a quick tap doesn't turn into a mega punch.

## 2026-10-25 (first real session, small phone upright, one finger)
- Slow slides are fine for walking, but a long slide keeps her walking. On a high plank, slide in short bits
  (a third of a second) and stop to look. Twice I walked straight off the end of a plank.
- Falling off a high plank doesn't cost a life, but it drops you all the way to the bottom and you redo every climb.
  Up high, go slower, not faster.
- Plain flick up = a small hop. Flick-and-hold = a bit higher. Neither gets over the tall stones on its own.
- Trunks and ladders: stand right next to them (touching), flick up to grab, then keep the finger pushed up.
  If you're even a small step away, the flick is just a hop and you don't grab on. Climbing is slow: hold up for
  4 or 5 seconds, then check.
- At the top of a ladder she may hang there. Push up a bit more, then a small step sideways.
- Ropes: walk right up to the post, flick up, wait, then slide along. At the end slide down to let go.
- Tapping punches. Hopping past things sometimes gets you further than walking into them.
- The grey "Got it!" button can't be pressed for a second after a tip opens. Wait a beat.
- Sideways the boxes at the top cover what's above you. Upright is easier to see on this phone.

## 2026-10-25 (second session, small phone upright, one finger)
- Tip cards: the "Got it!" button stays grey until the game has run a moment. Let a second of play pass, then press it.
- Leaf glide: flick up, then slide your finger forward and keep it down the whole way across. It carries a gap fine,
  but I sail right over the flowers in the air. Don't chase them; landing safe matters more.
- When the ground steps up a bit in front of you and walking stops, a plain flick up while sliding forward gets you up.
- Trunks: standing touching it, flick up then push up for 4 to 5 seconds, then 4 more. At the top he stands on the branch.
- Wind: when the screen fills with streaks and leaves, walking into it gets you nowhere, or pushes you back. Lift your
  finger and wait. A flick-flick (double jump) while sliding forward sometimes gains a little ground in it.
- Hiding: a hiding waterfall is bright blue and in front; the grey ones far back don't count. Walk inside it, let go,
  and wait 5 or 6 seconds. Bushes: walk right into the middle, then slide down.
- I lose hearts while hiding-and-walking without seeing why. Check the hearts at top left after every few steps,
  and when they drop, stop at the next cover and wait longer before moving.
- A lookout animal: tap a few times near it before walking past. I never saw the fruit, so I'm not sure it worked.
- Sideways shows more of what's ahead on the ground (good for spotting cover), but the top bar hides the sky.
