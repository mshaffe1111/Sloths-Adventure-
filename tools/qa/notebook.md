# Player's notebook

What play-testers have learned about playing Sloths Rainforest Adventures, carried from one session to the next. Player skills
only: how the controls feel and how to move. Nothing about what is in any stage.


## Notes (2026-10-31, first tester)

- A tap jump (`flick up`) clears about one sloth-height. If a ledge is blocked "too tall", try a short hop onto a smaller step first, then jump again from there; a running jump from below often falls short.
- `steer` + `flick up` together gives a jump that carries forward; steering 50-150 ms before the flick is enough. Longer run-ups don't add much distance.
- A short step-hop (`steer left 250` + `flick up`, then lift) climbs a small stair one step at a time. It's slow but reliable.
- `steer up` on a vine climbs straight up.
- The left thumb moves Meeca. Steering in a small square doesn't "draw" anything reliably, and taps are read as pokes.
- Upright, you see less of what's ahead to the right; turn sideways for long jumps.
