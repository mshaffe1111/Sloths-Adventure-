Privacy

Sloths Rainforest Adventures · updated October 30, 2026

The short version

The game collects no data about you or your child. Nothing is sent anywhere. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (stages cleared, stars, the trophies and the journal), your settings (sound, vibration, difficulty) and which stories you have seen are saved on your phone or computer only, in the game's own storage.
  • Nobody else can see them, including us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes them.
  • "Save & Load" can copy your progress as a save code, so you can move it to another device yourself. It is only ever in the place you paste it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect names, emails, contacts, location, photos, device IDs or advertising IDs.
  • It does not share anything with third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not connect to the internet: everything it needs comes inside the app.
  • It does not ask for any special permissions. (Android lists "vibration" for the game's optional buzz when Pokey is hit.)

Children

The game is made for all ages, children included. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to review, correct or delete beyond the progress on your own device.

The free web version's test link

The Google Play app never contains this. On the website only, a separate test link that our volunteer testers open on purpose can send a recording of how a test level was played (taps and moves, the kind of device, and a nickname the tester types) to help us fix the controls. It never runs unless that link is opened, and the normal game and the app do not have it.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so first. Questions: use the contact email on the game's Google Play listing.
